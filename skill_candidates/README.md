## tikz-diagram-craft

A proposed **v2 figure language** for the Book (`templates/pd-figure-language-v2.tex`):
a 9-hue concept-color system, a 3-voice typography ladder (title/label/note), a
1 : 1.8 : 3.2 stroke-weight ratio, plus 10 reusable diagram templates
(block-stack, quadrant, grid-matrix, nested-sets, sequence, state-machine,
swimlane-gantt, timeline-epochs, tree-dag, before-after) and 3 worked redraw
examples of real Book figures (anchor card lifecycle, commitment oracle,
work-unit machine).

All 13 example/template fragments render cleanly under the proposed v2 language
— see `skills/tikz-diagram-craft/foundlings-v2-language.pdf`. They do **not**
compile under the Book's *current* figure language (v1) — that's expected, since
v2 renames and restructures several of v1's macros; it isn't a defect in either
file. Whether to adopt v2 (and what it would mean for every existing figure that
inputs v1) is an open call for whoever owns figure language decisions.
