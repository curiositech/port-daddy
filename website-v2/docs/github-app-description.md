Port Daddy Fleet crews your pull requests with a fleet of AI ships, driven by
the `pd-fleet.yml` already in your repo. Reviewer ships (code-reviewer, qa,
red-team, tautology-sniffer) map-reduce the diff and post one inline review
with severity-ranked findings and a PASS/BLOCK verdict — blocking ships fail
closed. An ideation crew (spark, spider, lookout, snipe) proposes roadmap
items, assignable work, and skills, and can code small fixes as stacked PRs on
your branch. The Purser steel-mans your PR's stated purpose into a testable
contract, authors adversarial tests, optionally executes them in a sandbox,
and stacks them under your PR. Every run emits a signed deliberation
transcript; see yours at relay.portdaddy.dev/account/runs. Nothing blocks on
tests that never ran.
