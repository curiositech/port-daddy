\caption{\textbf{Recursive auditing becomes finite only when corrupt opportunity
contracts.} Both curves plot the chapter's hand-checked numbers from
{\normalsize$G_0=400$}, $\rho d=0.2$: heterogeneous sealed sampling across
$C=8$ cliques contracts geometrically and reaches the honest-root floor at
level 27, while a monoculture ($C=1$) first bleeds linearly at $10$ per level
and only reaches the floor at level 53. What proves this is not the picture's
height but the governing conditions: local deterrence
{\normalsize$\rho dB\ge G_k$}, and contraction
{\normalsize$G_{k+1}=(1-\rho d)G_k$}. The amber floor is drawn separately on
purpose: the imported theorem bounds the tower but does not supply its
exogenously honest root or the telemetry that estimates $d$ [verified, three
