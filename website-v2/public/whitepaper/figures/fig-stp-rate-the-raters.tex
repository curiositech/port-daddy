\caption{\textbf{The rate-the-raters recursion.} Conjecture~\ref{conj:contract}
asks whether sampling, a slash exceeding the maximum bribe, and a conflict-free
re-auditor pool larger than any clique make the incentive to corrupt each level
strictly smaller than the level below. If proved, the tower would contract toward a
finite honest fixed point. The paper names this as an unproved obligation rather
than depicting the desired result as established.}
