\caption{\textbf{The rate-the-raters recursion, closed.} A recursive audit design
owes two obligations, and the companion inspection-game paper discharges both
(Thm.~\ref{thm:tower-imported}): expected forfeiture $\rho d B$ must beat the
judge's corrupt gain $G_k$ at each level, and the pool model must shrink the
remaining corruption opportunity by a uniform factor $\lambda<1$ --- which sealed
$C$-clique sampling \emph{derives} as $\lambda=1-\rho d$ rather than assuming. A
bond larger than one bribe is still not enough when re-audit is probabilistic; what
makes the tower finite is that the depth is logarithmic in $G_0$ ($27$ levels /
\$1{,}350 of bond at $C{=}8$ against $53$ / \$2{,}650 at $C{=}1$). What the theorem
does \emph{not} supply is the honest root the tower terminates against.}
