\caption{\textbf{The rate-the-raters recursion.} Bond-and-slash pushes the grading
problem up a level: who audits the auditors? Under sampling, a slash exceeding the
maximum bribe, and a conflict-free re-auditor pool larger than any clique, the
incentive to corrupt each level is strictly smaller than the level below ---
making the tower contractive with a finite honest fixed point. This is the precise
statement of what Paper~4's central IC theorem needs and currently lacks.}
