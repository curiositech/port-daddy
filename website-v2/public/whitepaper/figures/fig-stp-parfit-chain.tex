\caption{\textbf{Continuity vs.\ connectedness.} Direct memory connectedness
(the solid top arcs) overlaps but is not transitive --- the general need not
remember being the boy (Reid's objection, the dashed arc). Parfit's repair:
\emph{continuity}, the overlapping \emph{chain} of links, \textbf{is} transitive,
and it is what survives. Reputation must accumulate across arbitrarily many
incarnations, so it can only attach to the transitive thing --- the
\textbf{witnessed-outcome ledger}, not the memory stream.}
\label{fig:parfit}
