\caption{\textbf{The non-forgeable root forecloses the two classic attacks.} With

non-forgeable identity bound to a non-re-pickable credential and a principal
(bottom) makes both costly: churn costs more than a clean record
(Thm.~\ref{thm:whitewash-cost}), and sanctions survive respawns
(Thm.~\ref{thm:necessity}). This is the one genuinely architectural piece of the
program.}
