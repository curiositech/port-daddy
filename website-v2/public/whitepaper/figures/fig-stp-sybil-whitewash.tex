\caption{\textbf{Non-forgeable identity changes the economics of both classic
attacks.} Panel A: a self-asserted label lets a sanctioned penalty drop to $0$
after a single respawn, while a principal-bound identity carries a penalty of
$1.0$ across every body lease. Panel B: free labels turn $n$ minted aliases
into $n$ units of quorum weight, while principal binding holds the
accountable weight at $1$ regardless of how many bodies the principal spawns.
The claim is deliberately local: daemon-side minting and principal binding
close both attacks inside one trusted domain; neither solves cross-operator
attestation [internal].}
