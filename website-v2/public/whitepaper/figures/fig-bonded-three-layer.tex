\caption{The Bonded Commons three-layer architecture. Capability scope (Layer~1) refuses unauthorized actions at the boundary; the evidence chain (Layer~2) records every authorized action in a tamper-evident Merkle structure; the collateral pool (Layer~3) prices authorized-but-harmful actions via posted bonds that pay for damage. The two paths --- \emph{prevented} (Layer~1 refuses; dotted, stopped at the boundary) and \emph{priced} (Layer~1 admits, Layer~3 charges; solid in, dashed exit) --- together compose defense-in-depth without requiring OS-level isolation. The settlement arrow returns to the principal as refund or slash, depending on whether the evidence chain shows the bonded contract was honoured.}
