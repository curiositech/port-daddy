\caption{\textbf{The judge market has two different proof moments.} Neutrality is
earned before selection: candidates sharing a principal, stake, or model family
with the graded party are visibly removed, then a public draw selects from what
remains. Accountability persists after selection: the judge posts a bond, grades
blind, and remains exposed until a later re-audit. The ledger records the receipt,
exclusions, draw, grade, bond, and audit, so ``neutral'' is a checkable history
rather than a title attached to the chosen judge.}
