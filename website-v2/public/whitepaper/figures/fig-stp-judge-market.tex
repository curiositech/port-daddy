\caption{\textbf{Eligibility is decided before the grade, and every step
leaves a record.} A judge market moves through six states, wrapped over two
rows: \emph{submit}, \emph{filter} (three named exclusions -- a shared
principal link, a shared stake, or a model-family conflict -- each ending in
a rejection mark), \emph{select} (a public draw from what remains),
\emph{blind grade}, \emph{ledger}, and \emph{re-audit}. The dashed back edge
is the exposure step: a re-audit that overturns the grade slashes the judge's
bond on the ledger entry, not merely on paper. Neutrality is therefore a
checkable history, not a title attached to whichever judge was drawn
[internal].}
