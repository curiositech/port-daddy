\caption{Conservation composes exactly within one unit of account but not across
currencies without further assumptions. In one native unit, the supply delta of
a composed trace is the sum of its step deltas, so zero-delta internal operations
remain conservative. A cross-currency transfer instead carries a named valuation
function and instant, with fee, slippage, and exposure accounts kept visible.
Category-theoretic language does not remove those accounting assumptions.}
