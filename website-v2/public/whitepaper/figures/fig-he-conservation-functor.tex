\caption{Conservation as a (lax) functor. Within a single unit of account the map
$F:\mathbf{Harbor}\to\mathbf{Federation}$ is a \emph{conservative functor}: it
preserves the composition of settlement morphisms, so the per-harbor invariant
$\text{wallet}+\text{escrow}+\text{commons}=\text{supply}$ lifts exactly to the
federation (proven). The moment harbors price bonds in heterogeneous units, $F$ is
at most a \emph{lax} functor --- conservation is preserved only up to the
bounded-escrow slack $\varphi$, and the coherence conditions are unproven. The
headline ``conservation composes upward'' is licensed \emph{only} with this scope
rider. The three cross-harbor open problems (settlement, equivocation, unit-of-
account) are one sheaf-gluing obstruction in disguise; the double-spend race is
literally the non-vanishing of the first cohomology $H^1$ of the gluing.}
