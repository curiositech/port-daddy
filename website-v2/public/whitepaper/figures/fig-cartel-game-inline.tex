\caption{Cartel repeated-game node under the grim-trigger strategy. Each round a
colluding member chooses between maintaining the floor $q_{\mathrm{floor}}$ or
undercutting it by $\varepsilon$. Detection (probability $p_d$) lands on the red
$\times$ path: the colluder is slashed $-5(q_f-\mu)$. One-shot defection takes the
$\pi_D$ gain once, then collapses the future $\pi_C$ stream to $\pi_N\approx 0$. The
cartel is self-sustaining exactly when the inequality at the bottom holds; raising
$p_d$ (sampled adversarial re-audit) or capping $\pi_C$ (diminishing-returns
concavity on pairwise outcomes) pushes the critical discount factor past $1$ and
breaks it.}
