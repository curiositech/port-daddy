\caption{One round of the cartel, under a grim-trigger punishment model. Each round
a member either maintains the agreed floor or undercuts it by $\varepsilon$;
detection occurs before the current-round payoff and applies loss $L$, while a
one-shot deviation banks $\pi_D$ and ends the cartel stream for good. The finding is
in the box: the cartel holds only while the \emph{discounted} colluding stream $V_C$
--- which is decreasing in the detection rate $p_d$ and increasing in the discount
factor $\delta$, the weight a member puts on next round --- still beats the one-shot
deviation payoff $\pi_D$. Raising $p_d$ (sampled re-audit of high-velocity pairs) or
lowering $\delta$ (making membership churn) breaks it from either side. The one
outlined outcome is the slashed branch, since it is the branch the defense is buying;
the displayed recurrence is specific to this event timing and payoff model.}
