\pdmargincaption{The commons authority's three layers of trust, read top to bottom
as a defense-in-depth stack: each layer catches exactly what the layer
above it structurally cannot. Structural prevention (\S\ref{sec:structural})
makes an out-of-scope operation unrepresentable, but an agent with
legitimate access can still write garbage; immutable attribution
(\S\ref{sec:attribution}) makes every action traceable to a bonded
principal even after the acting identity is discarded, but a traceable
action is not thereby a costly one; economic alignment (\S\ref{sec:economic})
closes that gap by pricing the evidence the first two layers produce. No
single layer is complete on its own --- the guarantee is the composition.
[internal]}
