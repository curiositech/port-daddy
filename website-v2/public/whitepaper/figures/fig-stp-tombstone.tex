entry {\normalsize$f(o_3)$}, and the imported tombstone are all preserved as
distinct, ordered facts. The lower step function shows what changes:
{\normalsize$o_3$}'s contribution to spendable reputation sits at $1$ from
settlement until the tombstone reaches an importing harbor, then falls to
$0$. The measured interval $\tau$ is the federation's revocation-convergence
obligation: the protocol bounds that window rather than pretending it never
existed [internal].}
