\caption{\textbf{Tombstone revocation on an append-only provenance tree.} A fraudulent ancestor is not deleted: an appended \emph{tombstone} nullifies that record and requires its dependent outcomes to be recomputed. An independent sibling retains its reference. This preserves auditability while making individual outcomes revocable; memory length remains a tunable parameter rather than a claim that reputation is permanently monotone.}
