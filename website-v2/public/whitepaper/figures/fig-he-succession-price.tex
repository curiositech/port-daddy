\caption{The succession price as a boundary in the plane the criterion is
actually stated over. Below the curve a high enough skill premium can still
justify renting one sole specialist; above it the pool wins at \emph{every}
finite premium, however large. The chapter's worked instance puts
$\eta K=0.2591$, so the boundary sits at $D^{\star}=0.3496$ --- and the
script's own canary, at $\xi/\eta=2.0$, sits $5.7$ times higher up that same
vertical read. What makes the specialist unrescuable there is the theorem's
floor rather than any premium: $W_{\infty}=\xi/(\eta(\xi+\eta))=2.67$ already

buys. The curve steepens without limit as $\eta K\to1$; past $\eta K=1$ the
