\caption{Assurance is bought in units of $k$, and the price of a unit is one bounty
plus one bond carry. The mechanism is geometric on the risk side and linear on the
cost side --- three independent reviewers at $d=0.6$ take a planted flaw's survival
odds from $40\%$ to $6.4\%$ for three bounties --- which is why an operator can quote
an assurance level as a line item at all. Two conditions carry the whole figure. The
gate underneath sets what a report is worth: a test-writer is deterred from
inventing or suppressing flaws only while its expected forfeiture $\rho d B$ covers
the gain $G$ from misreporting, i.e.\ only while the platform commits to re-auditing
at $\rho \ge \rho^\star = G/(dB)$. And the word \emph{independent} in the hypothesis
is the real cost centre: correlated test-writers --- same model family, shared blind
spot, or visible submissions --- collapse $k$ draws toward one, so the priced
assurance overstates the delivered assurance. Buying assurance means buying
diversity, not headcount.}
