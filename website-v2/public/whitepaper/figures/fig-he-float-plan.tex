database transaction; the daemon then signs $\{\textit{anchor id},\,\textit{plan

conservation invariant ($\text{wallet}+\text{escrow}+\text{commons}=\text{supply}$,

and is the \emph{implemented} floor the whole economy stands on --- the one claim
