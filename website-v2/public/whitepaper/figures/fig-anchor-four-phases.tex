\caption{The four phases of the Anchor Protocol. Each phase is a refinement of the previous: the cryptographic primitive changes, the property strengthens, and a specific threat is foreclosed. The teal pill on each arrow records \emph{why} the next phase was forced --- not as historical narrative but as a constructive argument that no earlier phase suffices on its own. Phases 1--3 are mechanized in ProVerif (\S\ref{app:mech}); Phase 4 is enforced at runtime against monotonicity and reanimation invariants (\S\ref{sec:revocation}).}
