\caption{The volume's spine, climbing left to right. Memory makes a spawn legible
to itself; a checkpoint makes it survive its own death; a witnessed outcome ledger
makes the world able to vouch for it; a durable identity binds those outcomes to
one accountable principal; reputation scores that principal; and the score, once
durable, becomes a tradeable asset. The marks are honest: the bond-ledger floor and
episodic memory are \built{}; the \textbf{checkpoint is the weak link} --- it passes
notes, not live execution state --- so it is drawn half-faded; everything from the
outcome ledger rightward is \designed{}. The score is cheap; the substrate it scores
over --- witnessed outcomes on a non-forgeable identity --- is the gate.}
