\caption{Federated revocation propagation across three harbors. The cuckoo
filter mechanism is unchanged from Anchor~\S2.4 of the
accompanying chapter, but two new pieces are bolted on: (a) per-epoch roots
$R_X^{(e)}$ published to a shared witness log so a third party can audit any
harbor's local view, and (b) cross-witness signatures so the auditor pattern
generalizes Certificate Transparency~\cite{laurie2013ct}. The illustrated
$\Delta$-spaced hops are one execution, not a universal deadline: availability
depends on overlay connectivity and delivery assumptions.}
