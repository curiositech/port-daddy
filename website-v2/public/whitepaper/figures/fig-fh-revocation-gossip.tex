\caption{Federated revocation propagation across three harbors. The
membership-filter mechanism is the same one the companion paper proves for the
single-harbor mesh, but two new pieces are bolted on: (a) per-epoch roots
$R_X^{(e)}$ published to a shared witness log so a third party can audit any
harbor's local view, and (b) cross-witness signatures so the auditor pattern
generalizes Certificate Transparency~\cite{laurie2013ct}. The window between
$t=0$ and $t=\Delta$ is the structural attacker window: bounded, named, and
priced into the bond in \S\ref{sec:fh-settlement}.}
