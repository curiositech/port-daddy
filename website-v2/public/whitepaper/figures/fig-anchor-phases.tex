\caption{The three Harbor Card phases. Each phase is a refinement of the previous, both in capability and in adversary model. Phase~1 (HS256) is a teaching baseline; Phase~2 (Ed25519) removes the shared-secret trust boundary; Phase~3 (delegation) adds capability attenuation across an agent chain. All three are mechanized in ProVerif (\S\ref{app:anchor-mech}).}
