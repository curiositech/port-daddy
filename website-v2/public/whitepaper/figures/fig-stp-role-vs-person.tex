\caption{\textbf{A role is a slot; a person is a longitudinal record.} The upper
rail holds one reviewer role fixed while three unrelated bodies $A$, $B$, $C$ fill
it in turn, one per spawn date: the slot carries obligation, capability, and
authority, but no history of its own, so each filler's record dies with it. The
lower rail follows one non-forgeable identity {\normalsize$\kappa_7$} through
three role intervals while four witnessed outcomes
{\normalsize$o_1,\ldots,o_4$} accumulate on that same thread. The readable fact is the contrast in kind: the role rail \emph{replaces}
at every date, the person rail \emph{accumulates} at every outcome [internal].}
