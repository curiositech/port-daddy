\caption{Advisory claims do not beat enforced locks everywhere --- they beat them only to the right of the dashed vertical, where agents hold private information the allocator cannot have. To its left, locking costs nothing and buys determinism, so Sen's warning does not apply and this paper's design choice would be wrong. Above the dashed horizontal, neither regime works and the hard-lock fallback of \S\ref{sec:governance} takes over. The marked point is where LLM coding agents actually sit, which is the entire argument for the advisory default of \S\ref{sec:advisory}.}
