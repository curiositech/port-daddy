\caption{\linespread{1.08}\selectfont\textbf{Deposit-only Sybil deterrence
saturates at the coverage ceiling.} The slash a defaulting identity suffers
rises one-to-one with its posted deposit only until the deposit reaches the
transaction coverage $B_T$; past that knee, $s=\min(B_{\mathrm{dep}},B_T)=B_T$
and additional posted capital returns intact on no-loss transactions. One
principal funding $K$ interchangeable identities therefore multiplies entry
cost, not the loss attached to any single default. Figure~\ref{fig:sybil-mc-inline}
shows the corresponding simulated profit and commons-deficit sweeps
[internal].}
