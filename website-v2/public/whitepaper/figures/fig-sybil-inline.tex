\caption{Sybil attack flow. The attacker spawns $K$ Sybil identities, each posting deposit $B_{\mathrm{dep}}$, then undercuts honest bids by $\varepsilon$ and defaults on loss. The protocol confiscates the Sybil's deposit on default, but the slash amount is capped at the coverage $B_T$ --- so beyond $B_{\mathrm{dep}} \geq B_T$ additional deposit provides no extra deterrence. Empirically demonstrated in Figure~\ref{fig:sybil-mc-inline}.}
