% message carries its signing key above the arrow and the capabilities it
% conveys below it. The readable fact is the dotted stretch of the daemon's
% lifeline: after message 1 nothing returns to the issuer.

\caption{The issue--delegate--verify ceremony in time, for the chapter's own
worked delegation. Above each arrow is the key that signs the message; below it
are the capabilities the message conveys. Message~1 is the only one the daemon
takes part in: it binds agent~A, the harbor, a fresh \texttt{jti} and the set
\texttt{\{db:read, db:write\}} under the harbor's signing key. Message~2 is
minted by agent~A alone --- it binds the whole parent card \emph{including its
signature}, agent~B's identity and the narrowed set \texttt{\{db:read\}} ---
which is why the daemon's lifeline is dotted from that point down. The verifier
then walks the chain: the root signature under \texttt{pk\_root}, each later hop
under the key carried by the hop before it, and a subset check at every hop.
Delegation costs no round trip to the issuer, and the chain is the only evidence
that travels. [internal]}
\label{fig:anchor-handshake-ladder}
