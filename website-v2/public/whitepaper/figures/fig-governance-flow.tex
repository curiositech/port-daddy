\caption{Governance and appeals: four-stage escalation path. The daemon resolves most disputes at stage~1 or~2 (zero or near-zero cost). Bonded preemption (stage~3) prices the blocked agent's claim that the holder is wrong; if the preemption is wrong, the bond is slashed under settlement. Human escalation (stage~4) is reserved for the cases the daemon cannot mechanically decide. \emph{Every} resolution is appended to the evidence chain with the same Merkle-Forest binding as any agent action, so all four stages remain auditable.}
