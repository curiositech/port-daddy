\caption{Dispute resolution is an escalation staircase. The system first offers a zero-coercion exit, then waits for the claimant's TTL, then permits priced preemption, and reaches human judgment only after the machine-readable options are exhausted. Every station can terminate the dispute, and every termination descends to the same evidence rail. Escalation therefore changes the decision cost and decision-maker without discarding the record needed for appeal.}
