\caption{The keystone split (C2). Papers 1--3 rest on \textbf{ADR-0040}: a
non-forgeable actor identity \emph{within one trusted daemon} --- a single trusted
root that vouches for every agent it mints. That is exactly what a single-operator
harbor needs. But L3 is \emph{defined} by mutually-distrusting operators trading
across a boundary neither controls, and ADR-0040 explicitly disclaims that threat
model (intra-fleet, ``not a PKI,'' no hostile-operator defense). Binding
\texttt{actor\_id} keys across harbors you do not own is a different, unsolved
problem: \textbf{ADR-0040b}, cross-operator attestation. It is the highest-leverage
unbuilt keystone, and it gates the entire market thesis.}
