\caption{Where this paper sits. The L0$\to$L3 stack: each rung serves

mutually distrusting. It consumes the \emph{implemented} bond ledger from L0, the
cryptographic hop-bound authorization chain from L1, and the legible \emph{person}

spine: the same memory that makes the single-player wedge good is what turns a spawn
