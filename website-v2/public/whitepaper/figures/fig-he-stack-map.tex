\caption{This paper is the top rung of a four-layer stack, and it depends on
every rung below it. The L0$\to$L3 stack: each rung serves
a different \emph{whom}, and each announces what it assumes from below. Chapters
I--III establish legibility, the kernel, and the L3 identity bridge; \emph{The Harbor
Economy} is the top rung --- the only layer whose participants are plural and
mutually distrusting, and it depends on every rung below it. It consumes the
\emph{implemented} bond ledger from L0, the cryptographic hop-bound authorization
chain from L1, and the legible \emph{person} from Chapter~III, and turns them into a
market. Chapters V--VII deepen the protocol, incentive, and federation seams;
chapter numbers are not layer numbers. The series spine runs up this stack: memory $\to$ checkpoint
$\to$ continuity $\to$ a \emph{person} not a \emph{spawn} $\to$ registered outcomes
$\to$ \textbf{reputation} $\to$ a tradeable asset $\to$ \textbf{the market} --- the
same memory that makes the single-player wedge good is what turns a spawn into a
tradeable asset.}
