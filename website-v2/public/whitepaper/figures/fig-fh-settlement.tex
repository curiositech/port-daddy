\caption{Cross-harbor settlement protocol. The escrow is a third party in
the sense that neither $A$ nor $B$ alone can compel an outcome, but it is
\emph{not} a trusted third party in the operational sense: the protocol
restricts its decision space to two outcomes (clear or refuse) and prices its
maximum extractable fee $\phi$ up front. Redirecting funds, equivocating
about a clearing decision, or delaying past TTL are structurally forbidden by
the property checks in Theorem~\ref{thm:fh-escrow-bound}. The escrow is a
\emph{bounded} third party --- the bond it holds plus the cap on $\phi$ make
its misbehavior bounded-loss rather than catastrophic.}
