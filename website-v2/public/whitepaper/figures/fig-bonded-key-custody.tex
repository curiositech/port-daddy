\caption{Who holds which key, and which compromise breaks which clause. Each of the theorem's four negative guarantees fails to exactly one custody compromise, and only clause~(iii) requires two --- email \emph{and} passphrase together, which is why the recovery pair is the one place the architecture depends on a conjunction rather than a single secret. The KMS is the only party that stores everything and can decrypt nothing; the daemon is the only party that ever holds the harbor session key unwrapped, which is precisely why the theorem must exclude the same-user adversary on the daemon's host.}
