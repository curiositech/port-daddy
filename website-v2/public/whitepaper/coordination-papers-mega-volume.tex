\begin{abstract}\noindent
Agentic development fails in a predictable way when concurrency grows faster
than accountability. More processes produce more work, but they also create more
unread evidence, more overlapping effects, more disposable identities, and more
ways for a confident report to outrun what actually happened. Better models do
not remove this institutional problem.

This book makes one claim: \emph{autonomy scales only when authority, evidence,
and consequence remain coupled at every effect boundary}. Eight chapters
establish that claim in the order the argument requires. They place one serial
authority at the local write boundary; prove that delegated capability only
narrows on its way there; seal the room in which two distrustful owners compute
one answer, and price what its only slot can leak; derive the information cost
of legible supervision;
bind work to a durable principal and outcome history; separate payment,
collateral, evidence, and settlement; connect witnessed acts to audit and
bounded remedy; and state what may cross between mutually distrustful machines
without moving either machine's sovereignty. The result is not a plea to trust
agents. It is a design for making trust unnecessary where structure can
suffice, measurable where uncertainty remains, and contestable everywhere else.
\end{abstract}

\section*{What is built, what is modelled, what is proposed}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{What is built, what is modelled, what is proposed}
This book is ahead of the product, and it says so here rather than in a
footnote. What runs today is a local daemon --- a single-writer SQLite/WAL
path for sessions, claims, messages, commitments, and coordination state ---
together with the operator projections derived from those records (each with
a route back to the artifact it summarizes), durable work and obligation
records that outlive any one process, a Rust card verifier whose harnesses
are bounded proofs rather than full ones, and a relay that carries
authenticated events outbound. Those parts are built and regression-tested,
and each of them is graded \emph{built, weakly} in
\hyperref[app:volume-status]{the implementation boundary}, because
filesystem and network effects outside the mediated paths lie outside the
transaction, and the grade is honest about that. Most of the rest is
modelled and machine-checked but not running: the sealed room's
noninterference and its release ledger, attenuation at every hop, the
incentive to signal claims truthfully, conservation of the bond ledger, and
the seventeen executed results whose numbers the chapters work by hand. And
some of it is proposed, neither built nor modelled --- the confined mode
itself (an enforcement point below the agent: a per-agent machine with
brokered system calls and metered egress), custody and settlement across
authorities, and the federation's witness log.

Two consequences follow for how to read the chapters. First, the kernel
chapter's supervisory-control result says that an interceptor at the tool
level can observe but not regiment; the git shim and the coordination guard
that ship today are such interceptors, and the book treats them as interim.
The confinement the book prescribes requires the enforcement point below the
agent. Second, whether the single-writer rail is the right \emph{collaboration}
model, and not only the right confinement model, is an open empirical
question, and a pre-registered study kept separate from the product
(\texttt{studies/substrate-study} in the repository) is testing it against
per-agent worktrees with a merge queue. Until that study reports, the rail
stands as a design invariant; the book does not yet know whether it is also a
good model of collaboration, and it would rather say so than guess. Every
important statement in the chapters is labelled by kind (theorem, design
invariant, model-checked property, or empirical hypothesis), every runtime
claim names its assurance mode, and the appendix keeps the ledger of both.

\section*{How to use this textbook}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to use this textbook}
The unit of account in this book is the work, not the agent. A work unit is a
case file: who authorized it, what it may touch, what it did, what evidence it
left, what it still owes, and who pays if it fails. Every chapter builds or
defends one part of that file. The chapters are ordered so that each stands on
the ones before it, and each proving chapter follows the chapter whose promises
it discharges. Read in order for the argument, or enter at the question you
need: where a rule can be made real (\pdchapref{swk}{The Single-Writer
Kernel}); how authority travels without growing (\pdchapref{anchor}{The Anchor
Protocol}); what two owners who distrust each other may compute together,
and what its only exit may leak (\pdchapref{sealed}{The Sealed Harbor});
what an operator must be able to see (\pdchapref{ls}{The Legible
Swarm}); what remains long enough to owe anything (\pdchapref{stp}{From Spawn
to Person}); what makes exchange rational (\pdchapref{he}{The Harbor Economy});
who may decide, on what evidence (\pdchapref{bonded}{The Bonded Commons}); and
what may cross a trust boundary (\pdchapref{fh}{The Federated Harbor}). The
standalone editions of the chapters keep their abstracts and reader maps; here
each chapter opens on the question it answers and begins with its argument.

There are two ways through every result. Each opens with an express lane, the
claim in one breath and a pointer to the box that states it precisely; readers
who know the field read the lane and the box and move on, and everyone else
reads straight through the scene, the analogy, the box, the numbers worked by
hand, what the result buys, and where it stops. Worked numbers carry a tag:
\textsf{[verified]} when a named script in the repository regenerates them,
\textsf{[internal]} when only the chapter's own derivation does. Exercises come
in three kinds, \textsc{Check}, \textsc{Trace}, and \textsc{Open}, rated one to
three; their solutions are collected at the back of the book, and each
exercise and solution links to the other.

Every important statement is labeled by what backs it. A \emph{Theorem} follows
from a stated formal model with explicit assumptions. A \emph{Design invariant}
is intended to hold and is backed by the implementation and its tests. A
\emph{Model-checked property} holds for a bounded model that a checker
exhausted. An \emph{Empirical hypothesis} awaits measurement. Runtime claims
name one of five assurance modes, \emph{Observed}, \emph{Coordinated},
\emph{Brokered}, \emph{Confined}, and \emph{Attested}, in increasing order of
what the harbor itself enforces. The implementation status vocabulary is
equally strict: \Built{} means running code exercised by tests; \BuiltWeak{}
means a running slice that does not yet satisfy its full contract;
\Designed{} means a concrete design without a shipping realization;
\Vision{} is a research direction. Mathematical proofs, imported frameworks,
finite model checks, repository experiments, and running code remain separate
kinds of evidence throughout, and none is silently promoted into another.

\paragraph{How a claim about the harness earns its grade.} The vocabulary
above grades claims about mechanisms. Claims about the harness itself ---
that a compaction preserved the agent's intent, that a conflict was settled
rather than papered over, that a resumed body actually continued the work ---
are graded the same way, and for them the book borrows the vocabulary its own
experiments use. A
\emph{Genesis} is a reproducible initial world and a schedule of
perturbations: a repository, some agents, and the failures, messages, and
context pressure they will meet. A \emph{Voyage} is one execution of a Genesis
under a particular model and harness. The \emph{Wake} is the behaviour it
leaves; the \emph{Logbook} is the structured record of that behaviour; the
\emph{Receipt} is the set of claims we are willing to assert afterwards,
adjudicated against the Logbook. The unit of evidence is the provable
transition: a before-state, a stimulus, what the harness observed and put in
front of the model, which authority permitted or refused the next act, what
the agent did, the after-state, and the receipt that binds them. That is the
work unit's receipt chain of \pdchapref{swk}{The Single-Writer Kernel} applied
to the harness's own conduct. The invariant is the one this book applies to
everything else. Watching a feature run once and behave establishes nothing
(one of us has watched a harness behave beautifully for an evening and fall
over at breakfast); what establishes a harness claim is a controlled Genesis
that produces a Voyage whose consequential transitions are supported by
durable evidence and an adjudication receipt. The first Genesis the book
cites is the substrate study
named above, and the same Genesis replayed under different harness conditions
is how the questions in this book that are still empirical hypotheses become
results.

Evidence is typed as well. When a chapter reports what a harbor observed, it
names the channel that produced the observation, and each channel has a blind
spot that no amount of volume removes. A \emph{deterministic verifier} (a test,
a type checker, a model checker) shows that a stated property holds of the
artifact it examined, and nothing about properties it did not state. A
\emph{provenance witness} (a signature, a hash, a Merkle inclusion, an
append-only log) shows attribution and tamper-evidence relative to a
commitment, never that every event was logged or that a description is true. A
\emph{protected meter} (a counter its subject cannot rewrite) shows a quantity,
not its cause. \emph{Expert judgment} shows an assessment under a named rubric
and carries the assessor's error rate. \emph{Buyer preference} shows what was
chosen, not that the choice was right. A \emph{delayed outcome} shows what
happened, and arrives too late to prevent it. The reader should ask the same
question of any dashboard: which channel, and what can it not show.

Cross-references are live. Chapter titles, section numbers, theorems, figures,
tables, exercises, page numbers, and citations are links, and every
bibliography entry ends with the pages that cite it. The standalone research
papers and earlier printings cite chapters by first-edition numerals; the
concordance on the next page translates.

\section{Introduction: the unit of account is the work}

Agentic software is usually measured by the agent: capability, runtime, tool
access, and parallel copies. That is the wrong unit of account. A process may
disappear, rename itself, summarize its mistakes, or hand work off. The durable
object must be the work: its authorization, permitted effects, evidence, open
obligations, and liability for the outcome.

This book develops that claim in eight propositions, one per chapter, in the
order the book is read.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Prevention begins at the effect boundary.} Rules can be enforced
  before an act only when the system controls the channel through which the act
  occurs. Local effects therefore pass through one serial authority; semantic
  failures that no gate can decide remain matters of detection and remedy.

  \item \textbf{Delegation only narrows.} Every child capability is a strict
  subset of its parent's authority and lifetime. Authentication says who signed;
  attenuation says what the signature may authorize. Both are needed at every
  hop.

  \item \textbf{Confinement is a property of the room, not of the tenant.}
  Two owners who trust neither each other nor the venue can still take one
  answer out of a sealed room when its only exit is a metered slot: silence
  except through the slot, a gate on the only enforceable boundary, a budget
  that conserves, and canaries for what the gate cannot prove. What the room
  cannot promise is stated as a limit, at the same prominence as the claims.

  \item \textbf{Supervision has an information cost.} A digest cannot guarantee
  that a human will notice any one of many relevant events unless it spends
  enough bits, attention, or artifact openings to distinguish them. A summary
  is therefore an index into evidence, not a substitute for it.

  \item \textbf{Accountability must outlive the process.} A task acquires a
  durable principal, capability history, evidence record, obligations, and
  outcome. Restarting an agent or choosing a new name must not erase that case
  file or refill the resources it already consumed.

  \item \textbf{Exchange requires separated instruments.} Payment rewards the
  requested work. Collateral prices a bounded failure. Evidence supports a
  claim. Settlement closes it exactly once. Combining those functions in one
  score or token makes each of them less trustworthy.

  \item \textbf{Uncertainty needs an institution.} Some harms cannot be
  prevented and some outcomes cannot be judged mechanically. Witnesses,
  randomized audits, bounded bonds, appeals, and conservation rules turn that
  residual uncertainty into a process that can be inspected and contested.

  \item \textbf{Federation relays evidence, not sovereignty.} Mutually
  distrustful machines may exchange signed capabilities, revocations, witness
  roots, and settlement claims without sharing a database or accepting a global
  ruler. Transport can carry evidence; it cannot decide what either machine is
  permitted to do with it.
\end{enumerate}

Together they form one causal chain. The kernel marks where effects become
real; the protocol keeps delegated authority from growing on its way there;
legibility states what a human must recover; continuity gives the record a
durable subject; the economy bounds consequence and prices it; the commons
connects evidence to remedy; federation carries those objects without
pretending that transport creates consensus.

\subsection{What counts as knowing}
Every consequential statement has a status and a boundary. Derivations establish
relations under named assumptions. Finite checks exhaust a stated state space,
not every deployment. Simulations estimate behavior under a stated generator;
repository tests exercise named paths; running code proves availability, not
universal mediation. Counterexamples remain in the record because they mark
claims the research killed rather than conclusions it merely failed to prove.

\Cref{app:volume-status} gives the implementation boundary;
\cref{app:result-atlas} records the results and their limits. The companion
\path{docs/harbor-research/mega-volume-epistemic-manifest.yaml} preserves each
result's source, status, conditions, boundary, chapter home, and verification
artifact. The chapters stand alone; the book's claim is the chain they make.
