Notes for the record

01

The radar wordmark stays primary.

The header mark is the product’s existing spinning p·d — same ring chart, same stem-and-bowl glyph geometry — reduced to two quiet animations: a 12s sweep rotation and an ensign-to-ink colour shift. Both freeze under reduced motion. The Papa·Delta hoist is demoted to the footer, where a vessel would fly its call sign.

02

IBM Plex does the hierarchy; Recursive says one word.

Plex Sans carries labels and body, Plex Mono carries ports, shortcuts, and the destination identity in tabular figures. Recursive casual appears exactly once — the surface name, “Scout.” No Helvetica anywhere.

03

One accent, hairline structure.

Signal red is spent on a single element: Send to harbor. Everything else is ink on chart paper with 1px rules — capture, annotate, and send read as one column separated by hairlines, not boxed cards. Foxtrot is the only flag in the working surface, at its verified Pub. 102 meaning.
