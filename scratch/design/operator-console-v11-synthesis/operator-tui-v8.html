Why a broker

              

                PD holds the keys once, in keychain at rest where it can, and lends a scoped, expiring credential to a spawned agent — so a key never lands in a transcript, a worktree, or a CI log. Backends read it through the single resolver (lib/llm-backend-resolver.ts), never their own env-read path.
