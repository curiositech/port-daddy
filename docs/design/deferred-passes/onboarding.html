Onboarding — the first five minutes

Two daemon-truth mechanisms kill the blank-fleet cold start. Face 1: the Shipwright first-run — a staff Agent Node surveys the repo, proposes a starter fleet of at most three as ambient-watcher Work Plans, consented one FleetBar gate card at a time, and seals a real Work Receipt on the operator's own repo within five minutes. Face 2: the do-this-next rail — the operator-facing twin of the M5 guidance channel, at most three evidence-backed actions at the entry of every sanctioned surface. The rail suggests; the operator decides; the daemon enforces.

Binding, not aspirational Ethical-engagement laws on every pixel above
1 Outcome KPI.

Receipts sealed on operator repos and time-to-first-receipt — never DAU, session length, or rail-tap rate.

2 Every step skippable.

Skip-all leaves a working install; the whole flow completes in under five minutes.

3 No guilt copy.

No loss-framing, no streaks. Nudges stop after 14 idle days; a return leads with accumulated value, never the gap.

4 No dark-pattern items.

Every rail item traces to daemon evidence of operator value — promotional items are forbidden by construction.

5 Cost before consent.

Estimated cost on every card and verb; ceilings are daemon-enforced hard caps, defaulting conservative.

6 Easy to leave.

“Pause everything” and pd fleet retire --starter are one gesture, receipts retained, no confirm-shaming.
