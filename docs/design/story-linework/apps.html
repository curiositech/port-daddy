Story linework · the three app surfaces · 2026-07-04

One system, three runtimes.

The sanctioned operator surfaces — FleetBar, the Fleet Control Center, and pd-console — wearing the same system as the site: palette v2 with its slab and wash cuts, the six-state grammar (stripe + dot + micro-flag + label + time), brackets on live panels only, one zone per view, and the signal vocabulary the token file has always named. Every color below exists in all three runtimes; the mapping table is at the end.
