Decision

Swiss Modern makes the typography do structural work the borders were doing before — it reads cleaner but loses the tactile assertiveness. Neobrutalist Refined keeps the assertiveness while removing the apology of tiny type. Both are viable; the question is whether the chrome is load-bearing brand DNA or just a habit.
