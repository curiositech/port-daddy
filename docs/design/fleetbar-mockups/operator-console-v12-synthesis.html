Section B · the synthesis

What I grok, what I'll build, and what the union offers

Your three docs spec the content and feel. What I shipped this session is the shell they assumed but never had. Here's how they fuse.

The one thing I grok: v9/v10/v11 always described what panes show — voyages, the Ledger, the ghost filetree, the avatar. They never built the thing that holds panes. I built that this session: a real pane-tree multiplexer (splits, merges, named tabs, macOS hover controls, command mode, GPU-native gpui). The net union: your voyage IA + motion soul become the surfaces and the feel; my multiplexer becomes the spatial substrate they live in. Neither is the product alone. Together they're the operator console you described: "the ONLY place I use Port Daddy."

where they meet

Your "screen splits, multiplex into any agent, panes for main/worktree/transcript/filetree" (VISION §22-27) is my pane tree. Your "labeled by role not PID, colorful, more intuitive than tmux" is the hover-controls + maritime-flag layer I'd add next. The v11 voyage content drops straight into my swappable SurfaceKind slots.

The substrate the docs assumed. Installed, running, PR'd (#464).

  • havePane-tree core — split/merge/focus/resize/swap, 12 unit tests.
  • haveNamed tabs (tmux windows) + click-switch + add.

  • haveLive-transcript route GET /agents/:id/stream (PR #462) — console consumer already wired.

The living heart vision

v10's sensorium — the GPU primitives that make it feel alive.

  • visionGhost filetree — each open voyage a parallel-universe plane over main; the conversation reconciles against it.
  • visionMerge-as-light — landing a voyage eases its ghost into main on a bezier → flash → settle. "See the outcome" as light.
  • visionBiofield — fireflies = live voyages over the filetree; pheromone heat-coding on lines/words (who touched, how hot).
  • visionSignal-drop steering — annotate a function/region/file; working agents see your pheromone/tuple/note.
  • visionFast editor + beautiful markdown — GPUI rope-buffer (Zed's bar) + pulldown-cmark→GPU text, side by side.

Honest scope & sequence

Warp and Zed are funded teams; a GPU-native console with a Zed-class editor, a gorgeous markdown renderer, and a living-harbor viz is a real product, not a weekend. But the spine is proven: the multiplexer renders Warp-fast against live daemon data today. The order that doesn't strand:

  • 1 · haveMultiplexer shell — splits, tabs, hover controls, command mode. Done.
  • 2 · nextFeel pass — motion curves, hover color, maritime flags, blinking cursors, add-pane palette. This mock is the target.
  • 3 · nextReal surfaces over live data — Ledger join, cartographer two-way, roadmap now-pile, fleet flags.
  • 4 · visionLiving heart — ghost filetree + merge-as-light + biofield as gpui canvas primitives.
  • 5 · visionOperator-avatar autonomy loop + the editor/markdown pair.
