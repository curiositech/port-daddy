# Manuscript-to-field gap research

Research date: 2026-09-23. Scope: the active eight-chapter Book, its substrate study, and targeted primary literature. This is a research and experiment-design report; no new empirical outcomes, deployed controls, or novelty claims are asserted.

## Decision

The highest-value next result is a trustworthy causal comparison, not more breadth in the architecture. The Book already acknowledges many of its deepest assumptions. Its remaining risk is that a finite proof, scripted simulation, context-loading event, or existing source module acquires a stronger interpretation when another chapter consumes it. Repair the S2 instrument before collecting more cells; test skill efficacy with randomized availability and independent outcomes; then join recovery, authorization, and observability through a common effect record.

The most promising commercial hypothesis is portable evidence of safe continuation across tools and failure boundaries. Generic sandboxes, policy gateways, and checkpointing already have substantial primary-source precedents. The evidence reviewed does not establish an unoccupied market. Customer demand and integration costs remain unmeasured.

The ranked gaps below distinguish model assumptions, finite checking, scripted workloads, LLM-agent evidence, human evidence, and production claims. Priority reflects dependency and consequence, not mathematical sophistication. All proposed numerical gates are study decisions to preregister, never reported findings.

## Ranked gaps and decisive experiments

### 1. S2 does not yet identify a collaboration-substrate effect

**Anchors:** M1 enforcement gap; S2 §§2.1–2.5; pilot completeness; changelog's D landing-order defect. **Priority/confidence:** prerequisite, high confidence in invalidity of the present comparison.

**Established:** the pilot documents an instrument failure and appropriately withholds H2/H4. The four missing D/N8 cells are not unfavorable observations to impute. H1's zero condition is a harness invariant; passing it cannot independently validate a deployed sandbox. B(0)=C is useful implementation consistency, not evidence that real agents never bypass instructions.

**External challenge:** a real GitHub merge queue tests changes on the current target plus preceding queued changes and reacts to failing checks. A historical-patch rebase loop without equivalent admission and validation is only one simplified queue implementation. [GitHub merge-queue documentation, accessed 2026-09-23](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/configuring-pull-request-merges/managing-a-merge-queue). More generally, cost and evaluation design can change claimed agent rankings. [Kapoor et al., 2024-07-01](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502).

**Decisive design:** preregister a correction/addendum while preserving the original protocol and pilot. First require serial-oracle replay of every corpus and an explicit task-dependency DAG. Use common task fixtures with known independent, dependent, and semantically conflicting edits; permit only dependency-ready work, equally across arms. A separate treatment may intentionally expose stale bases. Limit each D rebase to the intended patch ancestry. Define semantic preservation and acceptance tests independently of the substrate; absence of conflict markers and presence of a landing record are insufficient. Include a no-op that is truly redundant and a harmful silent patch drop as opposite oracle cases.

The parent lane's source audit additionally identified possible metric/scheduling confounds requiring focused tests: full-file overwrites count inherited content as lost without semantic comparison; immediate refusal can charge full work; duration and bypass sampling share an RNG stream; retry policies differ; and landing latency can omit prior queue waiting. These are source-derived audit leads, not measured bias magnitudes. Pre-sample task durations independent of treatment and separate working, waiting, retry, and integration time. Record completed useful tasks, preservation failures, work actually performed, and end-to-end latency. Historical bridging diffs need renewed binary/size/overlap characterization after filtering.

**Failure rule:** any known harmful silent drop that scores healthy, any invalid dependency execution, or a failed serial oracle blocks the full run. After repair, use the original H2/H4 decision rules and publish all cells, including null/harmful results. Estimate paired seed differences within corpus; resample task/dependency blocks where appropriate rather than pretending dependent commits are independent observations. Do not equate 20 seeds on three corpora with broad external validity.

### 2. Skill reputation lacks causal efficacy and version-transfer evidence

**Anchors:** M5 skill schema and pitfall, M5 skill-version open problem, M6 versioning gap. **Priority/confidence:** immediate, high confidence in the identification gap; uncertain sign of local skill benefit.

**Established:** witnessed grafts and outcomes support auditability. Matching on a task descriptor leaves skill-selection, agent competence, difficulty, context budget, and help-seeking confounding. Conditioning on successful invocation can select a different population from assigned availability.

**Counterevidence and benchmark:** SkillsBench v4 (2026-06-14) reports 87 tasks, eight domains, 18 model–harness configurations, and average paired improvement of 16.6 percentage points. Its §3 excludes low-separation tasks, and §6.1 explicitly calls for length-matched controls. Thus the reported gain does not estimate the average benefit of an arbitrary installed skill library. [SkillsBench v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12670v4). Repository-level context is a different intervention: the revised AGENTS.md study finds no general success improvement and over 20% average added inference cost in its tested settings. [Gloaguen et al., v2, 2026-06-23](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11988v2). Neither result settles this repository's skills.

**Decisive design:** freeze the eligible task pool before observing gains; randomize skill availability within task/model/harness blocks. Arms: absent; current skill; reconciled compact skill; length-matched relevant reference material; skill instructions without scripts; scripts/resources without prose. Keep mandatory safety instructions in every arm. Record available → selected → read → actually injected → used separately, exact content hash, context budget, tool environment, price schedule, and verifier hash. Estimate intention-to-treat first. Measure held-out correctness, task cost, time, harmful actions, and rework. Hold out repositories and skill versions, not merely repeat seeds on known answers.

**Failure rule:** no promotion for positive in-sample averages with harmful held-out slices, answer leakage, or lower quality under the agreed cost budget. A v2 skill inherits lineage, not its predecessor's demonstrated efficacy. A larger skill catalog is not success. The first result should be a paired release gate for the reconciled library, not a licensing valuation.

### 3. Compulsory mediation has a theorem-to-deployment gap, and the comparison class has moved

**Anchors:** M1 §§threat model/enforcement gap and S1; M2 implementation/boundaries; M3 only enforceable boundary. **Priority/confidence:** immediate for any confinement claim, high.

**Established:** the Book correctly distinguishes a cryptographically valid grant from forcing every effect through a verifier. S1 is a plan to measure that missing enforcement boundary. A source-level FFI test does not demonstrate the actual shipped process, credential route, sandbox profile, and egress proxy jointly enforce it.

**Relevant prior capability:** CaMeL separates trusted control flow from untrusted data and uses capabilities to restrict information flows; its artifact explicitly remains research code. [Debenedetti et al., 2025-03-24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18813), [official code and artifact warning](https://github.com/google-research/camel-prompt-injection). Anthropic documents OS filesystem/network isolation including descendants. [2025-10-20 engineering account](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-sandboxing). AgentCore's current official documentation covers default-deny/forbid-wins tool policies and session-aware temporal conditions through Cedar/Dogwood. [AWS core concepts, accessed 2026-09-23](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/policy-core-concepts.html). These are not proof of Port Daddy, nor guarantees over effects outside those systems' mediated boundaries.

**Decisive design:** compare a credential-free process, declared OS sandbox, container/VM boundary, and policy gateway under the same permitted workload. Build an effect inventory spanning direct filesystem writes, subprocess descendants, alternate binaries, path aliases, inherited handles, credential access, authenticated outbound operations, and broker failure. Attach testable acceptance/refusal receipts to each effect; inject policy/version drift and revocation between decision and effect. Evaluate benign completion jointly with prevention and overhead. Include provider/account/operation identity, not only hostnames.

**Failure rule:** one successful in-scope unmediated effect falsifies complete mediation for that configuration. No number of successful authorized requests repairs that failure. Gate the tested build/configuration explicitly; do not elevate symbolic attenuation or a passing S2 invariant into runtime enforcement.

### 4. “Read-poverty is the binding constraint” needs an operator study

**Anchors:** M4 §§operator/read-poverty/lower bound and status. **Priority/confidence:** high product leverage, high confidence in evidence gap; practical bottleneck ordering unknown.

**Established:** the digest counting bound constrains information under a specified zero-miss requirement; it does not determine human reading time, catch rate, alarm fatigue, or whether reading dominates reasoning, tests, review, or integration. “Two/fifty/five hundred” is an explanatory scaling story until measured.

**Counterevidence:** METR's 2026 update says its new developer-productivity estimate is unreliable because participation changed and simultaneous agents complicated time measurement. This argues for measuring actual operator allocation instead of extrapolating from task wall-clock or subjective acceleration. [METR, 2026-02-24](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/). Traverse studies hidden trajectory failures that final success can conceal; its September 2026 report is recent preprint evidence, not settled replication. [Rahman et al., 2026-09-15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17930v1).

**Decisive design:** counterbalanced operator crossover: raw chronological feed; fixed-budget summary; summary with evidence zoom; regret-ranked evidence zoom. Use identical underlying event streams with blinded critical incidents and realistic harmless anomalies. Independently vary number of concurrent tasks, event rate, uncertainty, and edit contention. Measure time to correct intervention, critical-miss rate, false interruption, decision calibration, evidence-opening cost, and recovery from stale summaries. Charge operator minutes and interruption cost separately from agent compute and elapsed time. Include experienced and novice operators and delayed re-entry into an existing session.

**Failure rule:** an interface that increases perceived confidence while worsening critical misses fails. The “binding” claim requires observing the bottleneck and an intervention that relieves it at matched quality; a shorter summary alone does not suffice. If it only helps high-event-load conditions, state that boundary rather than a universal swarm-scale claim.

### 5. Recovery must preserve external effects and authority, not just restore context

**Anchors:** M1 OP-4; M5 continuity, agent-death and branching open problems; M7 recovery fidelity. **Priority/confidence:** high, high.

**Established:** the Book acknowledges that portable inference state is not supplied by a Git SHA and message log. Its “behaviorally equivalent continuation” phrasing also needs a defined equivalence relation and distributional test; it should not silently follow from same provider/version.

**Prior art:** AgentRewind explicitly checkpoints agent context and controlled environment state together, restoring them with retained rewind memory. It evaluates long-horizon assignments in MettleBench. [Zhuang et al., 2026-08-14, framework section](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14380v1). LangGraph documents checkpointed graph state, thread memory, human interruption, and fault tolerance. [Official persistence documentation, accessed 2026-09-23](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence). External side effects still need application semantics: AWS's durable-execution guidance recommends stable idempotency keys across retries. [Official idempotency guidance, accessed 2026-09-23](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/durable-execution/patterns/best-practices/idempotency/).

**Decisive design:** inject crashes before an external request, after remote commit but before reply, after reply but before local receipt, and during a handoff. Compare notes-only, full transcript, structured continuation state, and effect-aware reconciliation. Use a mock external service with a separately durable ground-truth ledger; include both idempotent and non-idempotent operations. Compare same-model restart, new-model successor, and two concurrent successors. Revalidate credentials, expired grants, current repository head, external state, and budget before further effects. Score duplicate/omitted effects, stale-authority acts, requirement preservation, time to safe resumption, and final quality.

**Failure rule:** any duplicate irreversible operation, authority resurrection, or two successors each spending one surviving authorization fails the safety contract. Similar prose is not continuity. A safe stop with unresolved external state must remain a distinct outcome from successful recovery. This experiment can be meaningful without claiming restoration of a mind or latent inference.

### 6. Judge heterogeneity does not establish the independence required by the economy

**Anchors:** M5 oracle section and honest-root open problem; M6 arbitration; M7 incentive results. **Priority/confidence:** blocks strong market claims, high.

**Established:** a contraction theorem parameterized by audit rate, detection power, and disjointness does not measure those parameters. A trusted root, adjudication competence, and incentive-compatible capacity remain external requirements. A non-forgeable grade proves who graded; it does not make the grade true.

**Strong counterevidence:** an ICML 2025 study of more than 350 models finds correlated errors even across different architectures/providers. [Kim et al., 2025-06-09](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07962v1). Adaptive attacks against trusted monitors show that the review channel itself can carry adversarial instructions; stronger resampling is not automatically safer. [Terekhov et al., v2, 2026-03-02](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09462v2). These findings do not disprove the Book's conditional theorem; they undermine a casual claim that choosing different models discharges its hypotheses.

**Decisive design:** assemble hidden-gold tasks with faulty-but-plausible artifacts, collusive agreement, author/model cues, adversarial instructions embedded in evidence, and genuine ambiguous disputes. Compare same-family versus different-family judges, deterministic checks, independent human adjudication, and model-plus-human panels. Blind identity where irrelevant; hold out attack families; measure conditional joint false acceptance given a bad artifact, not raw agreement. Estimate detection power with intervals by failure class, judge load, and incentives. Model a common-mode adversary rather than multiplying independent marginal error rates by default.

**Failure rule:** if the measured lower confidence bound on detection power or observed dependence violates the proposed audit/bond operating region, do not claim priced assurance. If honest-root adjudication costs more than the value of the work at required quality, the market mechanism fails its practical test even if ledger conservation holds.

### 7. Canary and leakage results need adaptive-agent calibration

**Anchors:** M3 canary power/latency, channel capacity, and limitations. **Priority/confidence:** high for secrecy claims, high.

**Established:** independence yields the stated canary formula; a finite channel gives a capacity bound when the entire alphabet and event opportunities are included. The chapter already admits secret planting, timing exclusions, and Wald overshoot. An internal simulation with configured miss probability does not establish that probability for a deployed suppressor against adaptive attackers.

**Concrete counterexample class:** Anthropic reports exfiltration through an approved API domain using an attacker-owned key, despite functioning sandbox boundaries; the fix bound accepted traffic to the provisioned credential. [Anthropic containment account, 2026-05-25](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude). The lesson is operation/account-sensitive flow control, not that a particular vendor lacks confinement. AgentDojo supplies a reproducible security-and-utility testing framework. [Debenedetti et al., 2024-06-19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13352).

**Decisive design:** preregister benign and attacking workloads over planted corpora; measure detector performance on transformations, correlated misses, partial canary disclosure, paraphrase, multi-job composition, allowed-endpoint misuse, and response timing. Define leak success using hidden ground truth independent of the detector. Inventory statuses, errors, retries, lengths, filenames, artifact contents, and timing as potential channels. Run adaptive red teams with fixed budgets and untouched held-out attacks. Report useful completion alongside false alarms, missed leakage, and detection delay with censoring for never-detected runs.

**Failure rule:** measured error/delay outside the published operating envelope rejects that calibrated envelope. A channel omitted from the budget rejects the claim of aggregate coverage. Detection after release is detection, not prevention or retroactive confidentiality. The output channel may be acceptably bounded without being semantically harmless.

### 8. Scripted coordination is not evidence about LLM team behavior

**Anchors:** S2 worker definition; M4 coordination/specialization/scale; M7 repeated-game claim signaling. **Priority/confidence:** necessary external-validity step, high.

**Established:** the scripted study varies contention, timing, and refusal behavior while fixing patches. Real agents choose what to edit, request more authority, reinterpret refusals, exchange misleading evidence, and sometimes continue after failure. A file-overlap experiment cannot validate those behavioral mechanisms or economic preferences.

**Relevant evidence:** Scaling Agent Systems v3 studies 260 configurations across six benchmarks with controlled tools/prompts/compute; it finds strong task–architecture dependence and explicitly limits inference about larger and heterogeneous teams. Its predictive fit is incomplete. [Kim et al., v3, 2026-04-08](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08296v3). MAST identifies specification, inter-agent alignment, and verification/termination failures in observed multi-agent trajectories. [Cemri et al., 2025-03-17](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657). Repeated reliability should also be distinguished from “one of many tries works.” [Yao et al., tau-bench, 2024-06-17](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045v1).

**Decisive design:** after the repaired replay harness passes, add a small independently budgeted LLM study: single-agent, isolated worktrees/queue, rail, and region-claim rail; same tasks, grants, model snapshots, total token/time budget, and acceptance tests. Cross task dependency/overlap with planning topology rather than confounding topology and filesystem substrate. Observe refusal adaptations, semantic conflicts, fabricated completion, repeated-run reliability, and human intervention. Use a blocked pilot for variance/power planning, then freeze a held-out confirmatory corpus. Treat negotiated behavior and imposed “temperament” as distinct treatments.

**Failure rule:** if the rail's scripted advantage disappears at matched useful quality and spend, do not prescribe it as an LLM collaboration improvement. If more agents help only decomposable workloads, scope the result accordingly. No universal agent-count threshold should be copied from another benchmark into a skill.

### 9. Federation needs separate safety, liveness, and exposure results

**Anchors:** M6 gaps table around `harbor-economy.tex:1755`; M8 revocation/settlement/open questions; M7 conservation theorem. **Priority/confidence:** medium until cross-operator use, high on the distinction.

**Established:** orphaned escrow alone does not change `wallet + escrow + commons`; it can preserve supply while making value unrecoverable. M6's table currently suggests that having no settlement path itself breaks conservation. That conflates an arithmetic safety invariant with liveness/liquidity. Similarly, expected revocation convergence on a connected graph cannot price worst-case exposure during a partition.

**Prior-art comparison to read alongside a revised design:** Zanzibar is a deployed authorization-system reference, useful for examining causal authorization consistency rather than treating eventual dissemination as sufficient by default. [Pang et al., USENIX ATC 2019](https://research.google/pubs/zanzibar-googles-consistent-global-authorization-system/). This report located the primary paper but did not independently reproduce its system results; no latency or availability numbers are imported.

**Decisive design:** model settlement under crash, partition, replay, delayed evidence, key rotation, amendment, and operator exit. Prove conservation and no double spend separately from eventual resolution under stated fairness/connectivity assumptions. State which obligations intentionally freeze when those assumptions fail. In a bounded multi-host simulation, measure stale-authority spend and outstanding exposure over time, including correlated partitions and adversarial scheduling. Couple any monetary exposure bound to grant lifetimes, caps, admission, and a stated revocation service level; do not multiply an average latency by a maximum spend rate and call it a tail guarantee.

**Failure rule:** any conservation counterexample blocks safety claims. Indefinitely locked value in an allowed environment defeats a liveness claim even with perfect conservation. No finite unconditional partition deadline is admissible. A correct finite checker result is not evidence that strangers will join, arbitrate honestly, or accept the cost.

### W1. A skill release assay tied to customer workloads

**Customer job:** an engineering team wants to know whether a particular skill update helps its particular agents, before distributing it. **Hypothesis:** immutable skill/version provenance plus private paired holdouts and cost-quality regression evidence are more valuable than a larger catalog or public score. **Existing competition:** the paired-evaluation method is already present in SkillsBench; differentiation must come from organization-specific release operation, reproducible attribution, and drift handling. **Defensibility candidate:** maintained task fixtures and consented failure/repair evidence, not exclusive ownership of skill text. **Validation:** with consenting design partners, retrospectively evaluate accepted and rejected skill changes, then run a blinded release recommendation exercise. Reject the hypothesis if generic benchmark runs answer the same decision at equal cost or teams will not maintain representative holdouts.

### W2. A continuation receipt for consequential work

**Customer job:** resume after an agent, host, or provider fails without duplicating an external action or silently expanding authority. **Hypothesis:** a portable record coupling intended effect, current authorization, idempotency identity, observed external result, and successor ownership reduces unresolved or duplicate work across existing agent runtimes. **Existing competition:** durable workflow engines, LangGraph, and AgentRewind are credible baselines; checkpointing itself is not the differentiator. **Validation:** implement one bounded adapter each for a local repository effect and a mock remote write; run Gap 5's fault matrix against native recovery. Reject if the extra record adds no measurable safety or handoff benefit, or if integration requires rewriting every client.

### W3. Evidence-directed operator attention

**Customer job:** identify the next consequential intervention across concurrent agents without reading every trace. **Hypothesis:** task-specific risk and uncertainty linked to minimal checkable evidence outperform chronological activity and generic summaries at equal review time. **Existing competition:** failure-localization research and vendor observability already cover portions of this job. **Defensibility candidate:** a demonstrated operator outcome and provenance-preserving workflow, not a dashboard. **Validation:** Gap 4's blinded crossover with source-linked fault localization; add a control that uses the existing platform's strongest alerting. Reject if catch rate/decision time does not improve or confidence becomes less calibrated.

### W4. A portable effect-boundary conformance suite

**Customer job:** verify what a chosen agent sandbox/gateway configuration actually prevents, including permitted-domain cross-account misuse and broker failure. **Hypothesis:** configuration-bound conformance receipts covering local and remote effects let platform teams compare composed controls more reliably than policy documentation alone. **Existing competition:** sandbox vendors, AgentCore, CaMeL/AgentDojo and internal security test suites are substantial substitutes. **Defensibility candidate:** independently maintained adversarial fixtures and reproducible build/configuration evidence. **Validation:** partner-authorized tests against several configurations, including known injected control defects; measure detection, benign task utility, and setup cost. Reject a “universal assurance” claim whenever a required effect is uninstrumented; narrow the supported contract instead. This is research/product validation, not permission to test third-party systems.

The market hypotheses do not require implementing the full cross-operator bonded economy first. W1 and W2 produce evidence that the later reputation and settlement arguments could legitimately consume. Competitive rivalry and substitution are already material; model/vendor dependency creates supplier exposure; customers can assemble components themselves. A credible moat would need repeated outcome evidence and low integration burden, both presently hypotheses.
