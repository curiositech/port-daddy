# Agent Roster

This roster favors cognitive diversity over agent count. Six independent Phase 1 positions cover the major failure surfaces. The original authors return only after synthesis for the Phase 3 steel-man review. Product, engineering, and design reviewers are deliberately excluded until Phase 5.

| Lane | Independent tradition | Non-negotiable question | Phase 1 | Phase 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Privacy, capability security, and constitutional authority | What must never cross a person, repository, project, team, or organization boundary without explicit authority? | position paper | original-author commentary |
| B | Provenance, epistemology, argumentation, and formal methods | What can be proven, what is only inferred, and how should forward consequence claims expose premises and uncertainty? | position paper | original-author commentary |
| C | Distributed systems, reliability, and event-ledger architecture | How can evidence, projections, decisions, supersession, recovery, and offline work remain durable without last-writer-wins? | position paper | original-author commentary |
| D | Governance, institutional design, and game theory | Who must participate, who can decide, how are power and gaming constrained, and how do dissent and appeal survive? | position paper | original-author commentary |
| E | Operator UX, accessibility, and visual explanation | How can affected humans understand lost outcomes, dependencies, uncertainty, and replacement behavior before accepting change? | position paper | original-author commentary |
| F | Product strategy, economics, and organizational delivery | How does reconciliation preserve end-user value while bounding cost, schedule, attention, and prototype risk? | position paper | original-author commentary |

## Accountable roles

- Synthesis Steward: owns scope, durable process log, phase gates, consolidation decisions, and the final publication checkpoint. This is one accountable synthesizer, not an extra decision authority.
- Synthesizer: extracts principles, ranks convergence, maps tensions, and proposes the structural skeleton without erasing minority views.
- Lead architect: accepts or rejects every Phase 3 amendment with reasons and writes the consolidated document plus dissent.
- Fresh product manager: tests value, sequencing, stakeholder impact, metrics, and adoption.
- Fresh engineering manager: tests feasibility, operational load, migration-free cutover, security, testing, and ownership.
- Fresh design lead: tests comprehension, accessibility, consent, preview fidelity, and operator control.
- Polymath editor: addresses P0/P1 reality-check demands and emits the final constitution, guide, and editorial notes.

## Independence rules

- Phase 1 authors receive the same problem definition but no other position papers.
- The synthesizer receives all accepted Phase 1 artifacts and must cite each one in its extraction table.
- Phase 3 authors receive only their own position plus the synthesis and must steel-man before critiquing.
- Phase 5 reviewers receive the consolidated documents as first-time readers and no Phase 1-4 deliberation transcript.
- The Synthesis Steward may reuse no fresh-eyes reviewer as a prior author.

## Human participation

The operator may add a premise, stakeholder, constraint, or counterexample at any time. Routine progress does not wait for approval. Any input that changes authority, disclosure, publication, or canonical roadmap state becomes an explicit decision or blocker in the process log.
