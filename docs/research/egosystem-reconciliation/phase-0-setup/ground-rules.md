# Ground Rules

## Intellectual conduct

1. Steel-man before critique: state at least three things the challenged position gets right, one improvement it introduced, and one useful connection it reveals.
2. Label every statement as observed evidence, sourced fact, declared intent, design proposal, inference, prediction, or canonical decision.
3. A similarity score is discovery evidence, never logical entailment. A model-generated contradiction is an allegation until its premises and authoritative texts are inspected.
4. Preserve irreconcilable minority positions in a dissent appendix, including the conditions that would justify reopening the decision.
5. Prefer reversible experiments when evidence is weak, effects are containable, and the experiment itself does not violate privacy or authority.

## Authority and privacy

1. Default deny across people, repositories, projects, harbors, accounts, and organizations.
2. Capability grants are explicit, attributable, resource-specific, purpose-bound, time-bounded, attenuable, revocable, and auditable.
3. Consent to observe is separate from permission to quote, retain, embed, share, rank, prototype, or mutate.
4. Secret stripping precedes indexing or model use. Raw protected evidence remains sealed; authorized retrieval returns the narrowest necessary view or sanitized derivative.
5. Roadmap, source, and external-system mutations remain proposal-only until an authorized decision receipt names the exact effects.

## Synthesis and decision rules

1. Principle ranking records consensus strength, but votes do not override hard security, privacy, legal, or explicit owner authority.
2. Required participants are selected by affected authority and consequence edges, not popularity or who is online.
3. Silence, timeout, absence, or unread notification is not assent.
4. Quorum is valid only when all mandatory authority classes are represented or a documented emergency rule applies.
5. Supersession is additive: old evidence and decisions remain addressable, and the new receipt states what changed, why, who authorized it, affected artifacts, dissent, rollback, and review date.
6. No silent last-writer-wins merge is valid. Concurrent canonical proposals must be reconciled or explicitly serialized by the authority service.

## Research and implementation hygiene

1. External platform claims require current primary-source citations.
2. Current Port Daddy source, ADRs, plans, roadmap state, and active ownership are inspected before naming components or stores.
3. Active implementation lanes are read-only inputs. This research creates no parallel authority and claims no shipped behavior without executable evidence.
4. Every phase produces inspectable artifacts and passes its quality gate before the next phase begins.
5. The final constitution must be simpler to use than the process that produced it.
