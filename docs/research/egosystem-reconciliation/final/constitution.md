# Constitution for Project Epistemology

Proposed v0.1. This is a reviewable contract, not enacted project policy.

## Purpose

A project should remember what people and agents asserted, which evidence
they used, what it committed to, and who authorized a change. It should expose
consequences before work silently breaks an accepted outcome. It should ask a
human only when a material decision requires that person's judgment or authority.

The research object is \(\mathcal P_t=(E,B,G,I,C,A,V,U)\): evidence, actor
beliefs, goals, intentions, commitments, arguments, values and unresolved
questions. Institutional state is a separately typed projection of accepted
decisions over those objects. The phrase “project believes” is permitted only
as shorthand for a named decision, jurisdiction, effective interval and basis.

## Article 1 — Record warrants, not a collective mind

Every statement distinguishes its subject, author, asserted time, applicable
time, scope and warrant. An observation describes what a source showed. A
hypothesis predicts something. A policy governs conduct. A value preference
expresses an affected person's priorities. A blocking gate applies a named
rule to an exact action. None can acquire another category by aggregation.

Actor belief records are explicit attributable assertions, optionally corrected
by their author. The system must not infer a human's private beliefs, intentions,
personality or consent from observed behavior. A derived estimate is a claim by
the estimating system, with its own uncertainty and disclosure restrictions.

## Article 2 — Institutional authority is bounded

Each jurisdiction has an existing authoritative writer and an explicit policy
for authorized transitions. A proposal names the authority it needs. The writer
checks identity, scope, policy revision and current state before recording it.
Neither a model, a signed statement, a successful test nor a synthesis confers
decision authority. Concurrent proposals use preconditions and serialization;
stale proposals return for revision.

A decision record documents the exercise of existing authority; it does not
create a jurisdiction or appoint its writer. This package's plans, diagrams and
review checklists are proposals and projections, not a second canonical policy,
identity or roadmap registry. Their accepted implementation must use the
existing writers identified in the [Harbor integration contract](harbor-integration-contract.md).

A narrower decision cannot silently amend a broader rule. Conflicting
jurisdictions stop the affected action until the authorized owners resolve
their relationship. An emergency procedure may act only within a previously
adopted scope, expiry and compensating-review contract. Urgency creates no grant.

## Article 3 — Evidence is inspectable and claim-specific

An evidence reference binds source identity, revision or digest, observation
time, collection method, authorized audience, retention and transformation
lineage. Source text remains untrusted input. A receipt proves that a statement
was recorded; it does not certify the statement's meaning or truth.

Evidence fitness depends on the claim. An authoritative API may establish
service state. A source-bound recording may establish what a person could see.
Neither automatically proves cause, intent, completeness or future behavior.
Missing, stale, inaccessible or invalidated evidence remains visibly uncertain.

## Article 4 — Privacy travels through derivation

People, accounts, teams, projects, repositories and harbors are authorization
boundaries. Read, quote, derive, embed, retain, disclose existence, propose and
act are separate permissions. Retrieval filters authority before accessing or
ranking candidates. A comparison across boundaries requires a grant covering
the comparison and its result, including whether a protected fact exists.

Derived objects inherit the intersection of permitted audiences and the
strictest relevant retention/disclosure constraints. Redaction does not itself
widen access. A separately authorized disclosure review can approve a
sanitized derivative, with provenance and residual-risk review.

When a party cannot see a premise, use an authorized confidential adjudicator
and a minimal attestation if policy permits. Otherwise record an unresolved
constraint and withhold the affected action. Do not reveal a protected object's
name, count, digest, rank or existence to explain the denial.

## Article 5 — Attention and spending require admission

Every paid investigation has an operator-approved aggregate envelope covering
children, retries, verification, publication triggers and background work.
Reservation is atomic across the whole envelope. A new session, agent identity,
process restart or queue cannot reset it. Missing usage, unknown prices or an
unsettled prior run blocks further paid admission until reconciled.

The current operator halt is absorbing for automated execution. No component
may infer that time elapsed, a task resumed, or a queue became empty lifts it.
Only an explicit attributable operator instruction can do so. Local document
analysis can continue while paid execution remains unavailable.

Human interruptions also have a budget. Deduplicate allegations, batch related
asks, suppress immaterial candidates from the main surface, and preserve only
minimal calibration records under the relevant retention policy.

## Article 6 — Inquiry precedes synthesis

Where independently commissioned reviews are authorized, freeze the exact
change envelope and capture each first contribution before revealing peers'
conclusions. Record shared models, common evidence and correlated principals.
Sealing requires enforced read restrictions to count as experimental isolation;
a prompt requesting blindness alone is procedural discipline.

A solo assessment can apply multiple methods, but it must label the result
as one author's analysis. It cannot claim independent authorship, consensus,
fresh reviewers or measured benefit from epistemic separation.

## Article 7 — Disagreement is structured

Arguments identify premises, a warrant, conclusion, scope and uncertainty.
Support strengthens a stated target. Rebuttal attacks its conclusion.
Undermining challenges a premise. Undercutting challenges the inference.
Value tension and resource contention are separate classes from logical
contradiction. Negation requires aligned predicates, quantifiers, time and scope.

Retrieval and model extraction nominate candidates. Deterministic checks or
attributable verification inspect the actual premises and warrants. The absence
of an edge is not proof of absence; bounded search must report its coverage.

## Article 8 — One Steward integrates; named authorities decide

One Synthesis Steward owns the reconciliation packet and disposition ledger.
The Steward groups duplicates, explains support and attack, preserves dissent,
and proposes acceptance, rejection, deferral, supersession or escalation.
Each original finding stays addressable after grouping.

The outcome owner, durable role, Steward, actuator, deterministic queue and
operator are distinct responsibilities. A person may hold compatible roles,
but conflicts of interest and policy-required separation must be explicit.
An actuator executes the authorized command. A queue enforces ordering and
gates. Neither supplies missing judgment or accepts a value tradeoff.

Votes, popularity, agreement rewards and aggregate confidence do not clear a
finding. One approval cannot dispose of another contribution. A formal
argument acceptance set is a reasoned recommendation under specified semantics,
not an enactment of policy.

## Article 9 — Show consequences before commitment

Before changing an accepted plan, show the current and proposed outcome,
affected stakeholders, evidence paths, gains, losses, permissions, dependencies,
cost, uncertainty, alternatives, reversibility and unresolved dissent.
The preview binds to the exact proposal digest and state revision. If either
changes materially, acceptance of the old preview cannot authorize the new one.

Material conflicts among legitimate human values go to the authorized people.
The question must name the actual tradeoff. Additional model calls cannot
manufacture consent or resolve a disagreement about acceptable risk.

## Article 10 — Decisions and effects have separate receipts

An accepted decision records decider, authority basis, exact scope, rationale,
alternatives, dissent, effective interval, affected commitments and superseded
decisions. Its execution has a separate idempotent action receipt and verified
postcondition. “Accepted,” “executed” and “effective” are distinct states.

Mutable comments, dashboards and summaries project these events. Corrections
append a new event. A later action may compensate a prior effect but cannot
rewrite its history. A failed effect leaves the promise unresolved.

## Article 11 — Supersession, appeal and retention are explicit

Supersession names the exact prior decision and scope replaced. Partial
exceptions preserve the remaining rule. Appeal is available to an authorized
affected stakeholder, with an owner, basis, response deadline and a declared
stay policy. Opening an appeal does not silently undo a decision.

Append-only audit semantics do not mean retaining personal payloads forever.
Retention expiry, lawful deletion or consent withdrawal can erase payloads and
revoke derivatives; retain only permitted minimal tombstones. Content hashes
of low-entropy protected data can leak content and require protection too.
Every query states what was unavailable or purged within the caller's authority.

## Article 12 — Prove usefulness before increasing autonomy

Start with offline synthetic replay and temporal decision queries. Add bounded
consequence detection only if valid paths beat simpler baselines. Independent
deliberation and execution evidence are experimental conditions whose costs
must be measured. Null results may remove whole mechanisms.

No experiment, packet auditor or visual mock proves runtime enforcement.
Promotion requires separate evidence for privacy, authority, replay, reliability,
cost containment and operator comprehension. This constitution itself becomes
binding only through an authorized adoption decision.
