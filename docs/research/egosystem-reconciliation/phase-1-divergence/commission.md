## Independence contract

Each author receives the same immutable problem and research packet, plus one
method charter. Authors may retrieve primary sources independently inside the
same public/repository disclosure scope. They may not read another Phase 1
paper, another author's transcript, a shared draft, vote total, aggregate
confidence, or prestige signal before all six positions close.

If the exact change envelope moves, the round ends. A later head is a new round
or an explicitly labeled delta round. The Synthesis Steward will not reveal,
summarize, rank, or hint at another position before the independence gate
closes.

An author is attributable in two ways:

- the durable principal or semantic commission identity;
- the historical agent/session embodiment that performed this round.

The role charter is a method, not a credential or vote. Two seats filled by the
same underlying principal or model are disclosed and analyzed as correlated;
persona names do not manufacture independence.

## Required paper structure

Each sealed paper is 1,500–2,500 words and includes:

1. exactly three sentences of executive position;
2. five to nine ranked principles;
3. a proposed structural model that reuses named Port Daddy mechanisms;
4. one explicit case analysis of the Field Guide review system;
5. direct treatment of H1, H2, H3 and H4, including at least one plausible
   falsification or null result;
6. a typed contribution table using `hypothesis`, `verified_observation`,
   `policy_norm`, `value_preference`, or `blocking_gate`;
7. at least three tensions with other disciplines without pretending to know
   their unseen positions;
8. one abuse case, one privacy failure, one reliability failure and one
   human-value conflict;
9. a minimum viable experiment and explicit stop condition;
10. the top five requirements the synthesis must preserve;
11. uncertainties, rejected moves and one possible fundamental dissent;
12. exact sources for external factual claims.

The paper must separate accepted doctrine, source-present mechanism,
runtime-proven behavior, projection, proposal and author inference. It must not
claim priority from the bounded literature map.

## Close and reveal gate

A paper closes only when it satisfies the structure, records its exact frozen
head, and has a terminal transcript receipt. A timed-out or missing paper is
recorded as missing evidence and does not get silently reconstructed by the
Steward. When all six close or the declared timebox expires, the Steward opens
Phase 2 and reveals the papers together.

No paper is accepted by vote. Phase 2 must represent all material principles
and preserve dissent before it proposes a synthesis.
