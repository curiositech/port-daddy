## Source ledger: identity, access, inspected sections, and limits

- **Rao & Georgeff 1995, “BDI Agents: From Theory to Practice,” ICMAS’95, pp. 312–319.** [Official AAAI page](https://aaai.org/papers/icmas95-042-bdi-agents-from-theory-to-practice/) and [official PDF](https://cdn.aaai.org/ICMAS/1995/ICMAS95-042.pdf). Read full eight-page paper, including motivation/domain assumptions, commitment strategies, architecture/interpreter, discussion. It says practical BDI simplifies formal theories; logical proof procedures may be uncomputable and provide no latency guarantee. OASIS is a particular real-time air-traffic application, not proof BDI is generally necessary/sufficient. Blind, single-minded and open-minded commitment are application-dependent alternatives. AAAI HTML returns 403 but official PDF works.
- **Móra, Lopes, Viccari & Coelho, “BDI Models and Systems: Reducing the Gap,” ATAL’98, LNAI 1555, pp. 11–27 (published volume 1999), DOI [10.1007/3-540-49057-4_2](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-49057-4_2).** [Author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/226351978_BDI_Models_and_Systems_Reducing_the_Gap) read: formal representation, desire/intention formation, revision, conclusion. Proposes ELP with explicit negation/WFSX and Event Calculus with event IDs/duration; abduction checks intention feasibility; preference relations choose among revisions. **Scope correction:** assumes initially consistent beliefs and explicitly leaves belief update out of scope; “revisable literals” are user supplied. This is a cognitive model, not a complete implemented agent or empirical validation. Cite conference 1998 and volume publication 1999 accurately.
- **Rao & Georgeff 1991, “Modeling Rational Agents within a BDI-Architecture,” KR’91, pp. 473–484.** Identity confirmed by [DBLP](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/kr/RaoG91.html) and [Researchr](https://researchr.org/publication/RaoG91%3A1). [Full-text request](https://philpapers.org/archive/KHUIM.pdf) returns 403; no primary body inspected. Formal details inherited in the skill remain provisional.
- **Bratman, Israel & Pollack 1988, “Plans and Resource-Bounded Practical Reasoning,” Computational Intelligence 4, 349–355, DOI [10.1111/j.1467-8640.1988.tb00284.x](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-8640.1988.tb00284.x).** Full paper read from [author/institutional copy](https://www.emse.fr/~boissier/enseignement/defiia/up5/pdf/Bratman-PlansPracticalResoning.pdf); Wiley record verifies identity. Inspected pp. 1–7, 10–12, 14–19 and conclusion. Plans focus means-end reasoning, constrain options, bias beliefs and reduce deliberation; partial plans preserve flexibility. Commitment/reconsideration trades wasted effort against missed opportunities. It gives no universal numeric threshold.
- **Cohen & Levesque 1990, “Intention Is Choice with Commitment,” Artificial Intelligence 42(2–3), 213–261, DOI [10.1016/0004-3702(90)90055-5](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0004370290900555).** Publisher abstract/metadata read; [author copy](https://ai.stanford.edu/~epacuit/classes/lori-spr09/cohenlevesque-intention-aij90.pdf) is available but was not reread in this pass. Abstract supports commitment conditions, side-effect distinction, and background beliefs/goals; report as abstract-level verification.
- **Jenkins & Jarvis 2003, “BDI Agents: A Soft Model for Organisations?” ACIS 2003.** [Official AIS Electronic Library record](https://aisel.aisnet.org/acis2003/12/) abstract/metadata read. PDF returns 403. Abstract says it combines Checkland/Holwell POM with BDI, offers three possible mappings and future directions. Details of mappings/procedure/evidence remain unverified; characterize as conceptual proposal.
- **Checkland 2000, “Soft Systems Methodology: A Thirty Year Retrospective,” Systems Research and Behavioral Science 17(S1), S11–S58, DOI [10.1002/1099-1743(200011)17:1+<::AID-SRES374>3.0.CO;2-O](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/1099-1743%28200011%2917%3A1%2B%3C%3Aaid-sres374%3E3.0.CO%3B2-O).** Publisher metadata/abstract checked; [full-text mirror](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=c43688f73473838adec6878ff4e7e3e36ea08a7e&repid=rep1&type=pdf) failed parsing, so body not reviewed. Checkland 1989 [primary abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/HSM-1989-8405) describes purposeful-activity models as a basis to debate change that is systemically desirable and culturally feasible. Do not reduce SSM to a universally linear checklist.
- **Checkland & Holwell 1998, _Information, Systems and Information Systems: Making Sense of the Field_, Wiley.** [WorldCat record](https://search.worldcat.org/title/Information-systems-and-information-systems-%3A-making-sense-of-the-field/oclc/45732496) verifies identity/year/publisher; full book unavailable. Detailed POM claims are not primary-body-verified in this pass.
- **Tufis & Ganascia 2015, “Grafting Norms onto the BDI Agent Model,” DOI [10.1007/978-3-319-21548-8_7](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-21548-8_7), _A Construction Manual for Robots’ Ethical Systems_.** Publisher metadata/abstract/references checked; body copy was read in prior pass but not reopened now. Abstract supports proposed execution-loop extensions for norm instantiation/internalization and conflict, illustrates with fiction, and notes evaluation difficulty. This does not show legal/ethical correctness or field validation.
- **Park et al. 2023, “Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior,” arXiv:2304.03442 v2 (2023-08-06), later PACM HCI 7, CSCW1.** [Pinned v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442v2) and [full paper mirror](https://3dvar.com/Park2023Generative.pdf) checked; full text read in prior B03 pass, especially methods/evaluation/ablations. Memory stream, retrieval scored by recency/importance/relevance, reflection and recursive planning in 25-agent Smallville; evaluation is interview/believability and component ablation. Not human cognition or factual truth.
- **Du et al. 2023, “Improving Factuality and Reasoning in Language Models through Multiagent Debate,” arXiv:2305.14325 v1 (2023-05-23).** [Pinned v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14325v1), §§2.1–2.2, 3.1–3.3, 5, Appendix A.2 read. Independent candidate answers critique/update each other over rounds; experiments span six task families. Most runs use three agents/two rounds due cost; arithmetic gains plateau beyond four rounds and some debates converge incorrectly. Results are task/model/prompt-specific, not a monotonic law.
- **Li et al. 2026, “Recursive Synthesis for Long-Horizon Terminal Tasks,” arXiv:2608.05466 v1 (2026-08-05).** [Pinned v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.05466v1), methods/results and abstract read. RST extends a verified seed’s solution, realigns instruction/verifier, validates in a fresh sandbox, and admits accepted tasks as future seeds. Paper reports 15 rounds, 37,484 tasks and benchmark changes, but those are author-reported on that setup, not independently confirmed here. “No ceiling” extrapolates from 15 rounds; it is not an unbounded guarantee.

## Corrections, retained findings, and open gaps

- **Móra:** remove claims that its paper includes a complete sensor/belief-update algorithm. The opened paper explicitly excludes belief update. Distinguish ELP revision from external observation update.
- **Rao 1995:** full-paper audit now replaces prior metadata-only state. Supports resource constraints, application assumptions and multiple commitment strategies, not inherited numeric thresholds (one reconsideration/step, 10% plan changes, sub-second deliberation).
- **Rao 1991:** unresolved; exact formal axioms remain unverified until primary body is obtained.
- **Jenkins/Jarvis:** official abstract only; three mappings and procedure remain unverified because PDF fetch returns 403.
- **SSM/POM:** primary abstract supports purposeful-activity models as a debate basis and culturally feasible change. Checkland retrospective body and Checkland/Holwell book body were unavailable in this pass; detailed attribution requires a successful read.
- **Normative BDI:** publisher abstract supports a proposed normative extension and says evaluation is difficult; “normative” does not establish ethical, lawful, or safe behavior.
- **Generative agents / recursive synthesis:** pin version and task context; preserve positive mechanisms/results while distinguishing author-reported task-specific outcomes from general claims.
