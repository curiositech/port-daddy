## provable-action-adjudicator — cross-reference evaluation-evidence.md

## zhou-et-al-2026-formaljudge
**Source.** Jiayi Zhou, Yang Sheng, Hantao Lou, Yaodong Yang, and Jie Fu, “FormalJudge: A Neuro-Symbolic Paradigm for Agentic Oversight,” arXiv v2 (2026-02-12), https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11136v2 . The full arXiv abstract was opened. It proposes LLM compilation of natural-language requirements to atomic constraints and checking with Dafny/Z3; abstract reports three benchmarks/seven agent models and stated comparative outcomes. These are paper-specific results, not guarantees for a local judge. The skill’s phrases “immunity to persuasion” and numeric 70.7→99.8 claims must be tied to precise evaluation conditions/sections; formal solver certainty is conditional on the correctness and completeness of specification translation.
**Extension.** Use the paper as an architecture example: user requirement → atomic claim set → executable spec → solver verdict; keep an independent human review of spec coverage. Example: “never disclose secret” becomes a finite predicate over labeled outputs; anything not captured by the predicate remains out of scope. Evaluation: seed omission, ambiguous requirement, contradictory requirements, and maliciously persuasive output; measure translation errors separately from solver errors.
**Diagrams.** Natural language → atomic predicates → Dafny/Z3 → verdict plus uncovered-intent return loop; decomposition coverage matrix with explicit unsupported/unknown rows. Existing decision trees should be checked for thresholds absent from the paper.
**Book candidate.** Separate formal verification of a supplied spec from validation that the spec captures intent.
