## Source-grounding and duplicate findings

Primary-source identity checked before interpretation. Jenkins & Jarvis is *BDI Agents: A Soft Model for Organisations?* (ACIS 2003), author/title listing and abstract at [AIS Electronic Library](https://aisel.aisnet.org/acis2003/12/); that abstract says it combines Checkland & Holwell’s POM with BDI, gives three mappings and future directions. The full text was not opened here, so detailed claims beyond the abstract and inherited text remain provisional. Móra et al.’s exact title/authors are recorded in both matching inherited frontmatters, but I did not locate an authoritative full text in this pass; treat detailed attribution and claimed operational semantics as unverified pending source recovery. Rao & Georgeff 1991 metadata was found, but no primary full text was opened; assertions beyond source metadata remain provisional. Rao & Georgeff 1995 is listed by [AAAI](https://aaai.org/papers/icmas95-042-bdi-agents-from-theory-to-practice/) (page returned 403 to the browser), so don’t claim a full-paper audit. Park et al. 2023 paper was read in full from the [ACM-hosted paper mirror PDF](https://3dvar.com/Park2023Generative.pdf), and its identity was cross-checked against [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442). Tufis & Ganascia normative material was read in full from the linked chapter copy; source metadata: *A Construction Manual for Robots’ Ethical Systems*, 2015, DOI `10.1007/978-3-319-21548-8_7`; copy is not publisher-hosted, so recheck publisher metadata before final citations.

Semantic near-duplicates deserve one deliberate reconciliation, not blind dedupe:

- `bdi-agents-a-soft-model-for-organisation` and `bdi-soft-model-for-organisations` identify the same Jenkins/Jarvis 2003 source. The latter adds an IO contract and fuller references; likely merge into one canonical guide after full-paper check, retaining strongest provenance and the exact source spelling in bibliography.
- `bdi-soft-systems` shares ref names and much of the same conceptual content but has a different/unclear source identity and adds numerical heuristics. Keep distinct until its frontmatter/source file is verified; preserve useful SSM guidance only when grounded. Its “3+ consensus sessions” and “>30% re-deliberation” rules are unsupported heuristics, not findings of the source.
- `bdi-models-and-systems-reducing-the-gap` and `...-paper` share source title/authors and almost all prose/refs; the `-paper` variant adds an IO contract and broader refs, while pairing it differently. Candidate single canonical source record after recovering the actual paper and checking which extra guidance is faithful.
- `belief-desire-intention-model-of-agency` and `bdi-agency-model` are near-duplicate BDI overview content, but the former’s `description` is corrupt (`license: Apache-2.0 NOT...`). Repair identity metadata and reconcile content; preserve one distinct high-level explainer only if it adds evidence beyond the specialist Rao/Georgeff records.
- `normative-bdi-agent-architecture` and `normative-bdi-agents` overlap but offer different algorithmic detail and IO contracts. Preserve a specific normative extension distinct from base BDI; consolidate shared core, validate remaining distinctions against the actual chapter/paper.
- Keep `rao-georgeff-1991` (formal branching-time/possible-world semantics) distinct from `rao-georgeff-1995` (practical architecture/resource constraints). For 1995, separate source semantics from modern engineering heuristics. Do not copy the same generic “BDI loop” prose across both.

## Per-skill research and edit brief

### 1. `bdi-agents-a-soft-model-for-organisation` — 7 refs; no ASCII candidate
**Read/evidence:** Full skill and relevant refs on accommodation, organizational desire formation, abstraction and perceptual filters. Frontmatter names Jenkins & Jarvis, exact source title confirmed at AIS; source abstract only checked. **Improve:** frame as a proposed conceptual mapping of soft-systems/POM and BDI, not a predictive or empirically validated organizational model. Show how to elicit perspectives, record accommodations without fabricating consensus, and validate the model against actual decisions. Worked example: departments disagree about a release risk; record separate beliefs/desires, negotiated action and unresolved dissent. **Diagrams:** perspective-to-BDI mapping; accommodation protocol with dissent retained. **ASCII audit:** no fenced candidate found in main file; inspect 7 refs. **Book lead:** accommodation as action without consensus; no novelty claim.

### 7. `normative-bdi-agent-architecture` — 6 refs; no ASCII candidate
**Read/evidence:** Full skill, key normative refs and Tufis/Ganascia chapter copy. Chapter defines abstract norms with modality/activation/expiry/content/sanction/reward, norm instances, and distinguishes norm recognition/acquisition from application; its scenario is illustrative and evaluation limited. **Improve:** preserve norm source/issuer/jurisdiction, temporal validity, applicability evidence, conflicts, recognition/acceptance decision and runtime compliance evidence. ### 9. `park-2023-generative-agents` — 7 refs; 3 candidate ASCII blocks
**Read/evidence:** Full skill and its primary source. Paper explicitly implements memory stream, retrieval using relevance/recency/importance, reflection and recursive planning in a 25-agent Smallville sandbox; component ablations support believability on its interview evaluation, and main failure categories include missed retrieval, fabricated memory embellishments and overly formal behavior. It expressly calls the agents simulacra, not genuinely human-like agents. No evidence supports inherited tunings such as `0.1→0.3`, threshold `5→3`, fixed decay weights, or social diffusion probabilities as universal. **Improve:** cite exact paper equations/implementation and distinguish reported parameters from suggested tuning; include provenance, memory correction/deletion, fabricated-memory detection, world/action grounding and evaluation limits. Avoid generalizing believability to truth, agency, safety or real-world social science validity. **Worked example:** show observation with source, retrieval scores, reflection lineage, plan, grounded action and correction when a recalled event is false. **Diagrams:** memory lineage/retrieval/reflection graph; grounded plan-action feedback state flow. **ASCII audit:** 3 candidate blocks; preserve prompt/template output where code-like. **Sources:** [ACM paper PDF mirror](https://3dvar.com/Park2023Generative.pdf) (full paper opened); [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442) metadata/abstract. **Book lead:** keep simulated persona consistency distinct from verified factual memory; no novelty claim.
