# Gate policy and calibration

List the transition protected, authority, irreversible effects, required inputs, hard constraints, and costs of false accept/false halt. Track each field as satisfied, false, or unknown with provenance. Halt on a critical false/unknown. Allow reversible investigation only with explicit limits and owner. Ask the smallest decision-changing question, version the input, and rerun.

An aggregate score can hide a blocker. If a score remains, pin implementation, label as heuristic unless calibrated, define outcomes/error costs, separate calibration from held-out data, and monitor drift. Do not turn confidence into probability. Polya motivates asking for unknown, data, constraints, and desired output; it does not justify score weights or cutoffs.
