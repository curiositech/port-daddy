# Observer knowledge chains

## Scope

If cameras observe other cameras, add propositions and accessibility relations for those observations. A chain of reports does not automatically become common knowledge.

## Method and fixture

Record who observed, what was reported, delivery assumptions, and whether acknowledgement is public. Then evaluate the desired K, E, or C formula on the finite model.

## Evidence boundary

This reference distinguishes a formal or constructed model from observed runtime behavior. Record model version, inputs, assumptions, and outcome evidence before drawing a conclusion.
