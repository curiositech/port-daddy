# Centralization and distribution are different epistemic models

## Scope

A centralized observer may have a different information partition from distributed cameras. Compare the same proposition under explicitly defined local observation relations and an aggregation/report protocol; neither topology automatically dominates.

## Method and fixture

The Charrier model starts with stationary cameras at known planar positions. Adding a central log or a mobile camera changes the observation model and requires new analysis.

## Evidence boundary

This reference distinguishes a formal or constructed model from observed runtime behavior. Record model version, inputs, assumptions, and outcome evidence before drawing a conclusion.
