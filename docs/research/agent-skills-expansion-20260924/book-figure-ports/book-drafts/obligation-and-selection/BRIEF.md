These are standalone draft figures outside the manuscript and accepted atlas. They illustrate proposed interfaces and constructed arithmetic, not demonstrated efficacy, novelty, runtime enforcement, or whole-page integration. Source: `ASTRA-BOOK-REVIEW.md`, additions 6 and 7.

## 1. Obligation-complete context compilation

- Stable draft ID: `draft/obligation-context-receipt`.
- Reader question: After a context pack fits the budget, which declared requirement is still uncovered?
- Claim: A+C consumes the six-unit budget and covers R1, R2, R4; R3 remains BLOCKED because its sole candidate B is outside disclosure scope.
- Relation and grammar: authorization-filtered coverage matrix with a budget strip. A generic flowchart would hide the specific uncovered requirement and the denied source.
- Required evidence: A costs 4 and covers R1/R2; B costs 3 and covers R3 but is denied; C costs 2 and covers R4; budget 6; the receipt lists all four R IDs.
- Counter-reading: covering declared IDs proves semantic completeness or authorizes an effect. The figure says declared coverage only and no effect authority.
- Placement: Chapter 4 `legible-swarm.tex` before the context-degradation cascade near line 2455, with a Chapter 5 continuity cross-reference.
- Claim kind: proposed compiler contract and experiment. Source authorization, declared coverage, semantic adequacy, and actual use are separate tests.
- Five-second check: the reader can point to R3 as BLOCKED and explain why A+C fits.

## 2. Selection-aware skill promotion

- Stable draft ID: `draft/selection-aware-skill-promotion`.
- Reader question: What can an adaptively selected skill's success history establish?
- Claim: the constructed raw success ranking S>T reverses under an equal easy/hard mixture, T>S; routing logs alone do not justify release promotion.
- Mermaid relation and grammar: a quadrant comparison places the constructed raw rate on the x-axis and equal-mix rate on the y-axis, making the ranking reversal legible without inventing a causal claim. The Book port pairs an aligned strata table with separate routing and release lanes. A bandit loop would conceal the task mix and imply that use is promotion evidence.
- Required evidence: S easy 9/10, hard 1/2, raw 10/12 = 83.3%, equal mix 70%; T easy 1/1, hard 6/10, raw 7/11 = 63.6%, equal mix 80%.
- Counter-reading: equal-mixture arithmetic proves T is better or identifies a causal effect. Tiny cells and unmeasured selection uncertainty prevent that conclusion.
- Placement: Chapter 5 `spawn-to-person.tex` after the matched-comparison pitfall near lines 1973–2017; cross-reference bandit discussion near line 1809.
- Claim kind: proposed evaluation and release contract with constructed arithmetic; not a new algorithm or efficacy result.
- Five-second check: the reader reads S as the raw winner, T as the equal-mix winner, and sees promotion flow from held-out evaluation.
