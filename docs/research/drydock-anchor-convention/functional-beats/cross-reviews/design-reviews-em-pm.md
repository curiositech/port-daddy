# Reciprocal design and accessibility review

## Faithful steelmen

- **Engineering:** Build the smallest rigorous proof spine first—explicit authority transitions, deterministic fault injection, and fail-closed ambiguity—while withholding every live containment, cancellation, accessibility, and release claim until independently demonstrated.
- **Product:** The credible beachhead is one bounded coding run for an accountable operator, integrated with existing Git, CI, and security systems and governed by explicit market kill conditions, not the larger autonomous-economy vision.
- **Design:** A calm Observatory, separate command surface, explicit authority and freshness states, progressive evidence, and precise trust language can make a complicated system understandable without pretending the static prototype operates a reference monitor.

## Bounded corrections

### EM

Add an `OperatorSemanticContract` to S0. For every internal state and transition, define public wording, control authority, stale/unknown behavior, accessible name/role/state, announcement priority, and focus destination. Exercise it with deterministic fixtures; keep integrated visual composition in S6. This adds no runtime or authority writer.

### PM

Replace H3 with: “Operators can both understand and independently operate assent, denial, Stop, ambiguity, and resurrection.” Test comprehension and operability separately across keyboard, screen reader, 200% zoom, and forced colors, then require transfer to a novel scenario with zero unsafe actions. A copy repair may improve the design but cannot convert that same coached trial into a pass.

## Impossible concurrency and tradeoffs

- S0 cannot freeze authority vocabulary while human semantics wait until S6. Accessibility must be a cross-cutting contract lane, not a third authority-bearing implementation lane.
- A one-environment wedge can test usefulness; it cannot simultaneously prove provider neutrality.
- Exact evidence and low operator burden require layered summaries plus immutable primary evidence. Showing every field at once satisfies neither.
- A phone cannot both require host acknowledgement and honestly present an immediate Stop command.
- Static fixtures can test semantics, focus, reflow, and failure presentation; they cannot prove live containment, timing, assistive-technology integration, or teardown.
- Approval fatigue and exact assent are in tension. The answer is critical-field comparison and stable policy, not weaker disclosure.

## Dissent that must survive

- Human assent is evidence, never execution authority.
- Stop requested is not Stop completed.
- `UNKNOWN`, `STALE`, `AMBIGUOUS`, and `FAILED` must remain distinct and never aggregate to green.
- Internal lifecycle tokens are not automatically usable operator language.
- “No hidden work” remains prohibited until complete mediation is proven.
- Mobile pairing grants neither workspace nor wake authority.
- Two actions does not by itself establish accessibility.
- If native controls satisfy the job or buyers will not install and pay, the wedge should die rather than broaden rhetorically.
- One inaccessible emergency control fails the experience regardless of aggregate task completion.
