## Steelmen

- **Engineering:** The safest build path is a deterministic contract spine that closes admission atomicity, permanent ambiguity, and settlement handoff before any live claim; real containment remains blocked until a contained canary proves it.
- **Design:** Operator trust comes from visibly separating proposal, authority, state, evidence, and uncertainty, with staged intervention receipts and no projection or animation masquerading as control.
- **Product:** The credible wedge is one provider-neutral governed coding run whose bounded authority and inspectable evidence reduce review or incident burden enough to justify privileged deployment; demand and willingness to pay remain unproved.

## Bounded corrections

### EM

Replace the concurrency claim with:

> S0–S7 is the authority-bearing technical dependency order. Before S1 passes, no more than two authority-bearing implementation lanes may run. A separate read-only discovery lane may run problem interviews, static teach-back, security/procurement review, and pricing tests from S0; it cannot create runtime evidence.

### Design

Replace the static gate with:

> The V1 static gate covers one desktop run: exact proposal, assent-versus-admission, stale/unknown/ambiguous state, staged Stop, review evidence, and 200% keyboard/screen-reader operation. Mobile authority, resurrection, and settlement conflict remain research fixtures until the one-run wedge survives H1–H4. Any critical misconception blocks that prototype revision and requires repair plus retest.

## Impossible concurrency and tradeoffs

- Static comprehension and market discovery can proceed during the halt; live containment, cancellation, teardown, and canary claims cannot. `UNKNOWN` demand must not be relabeled `BLOCKED_BY_HALT`.
- Product discovery can parallelize; shared admission/effect authority cannot safely parallelize before S1.
- A one-run wedge cannot simultaneously ship mobile control, resurrection, settlement, and a six-destination platform without ceasing to be narrow.
- Stop can be requested immediately; external-effect finality cannot. The staged lifecycle must survive pressure for reassuring green.
- Policy can be provider-neutral; cancellation, idempotency, and reconciliation guarantees must remain provider-specific until proven otherwise.

## Dissent that must survive

- Engineering may still require Trial Basin integrity before any operational trust claim; early discovery does not waive that proof order.
- Design may be right that Continuity, Settlement, and Switchboard deserve dedicated destinations eventually; deferral must not delete their semantic states.
- Native provider controls may satisfy the buyer's job, falsifying the differentiation thesis.
- The privileged deployment and security-review burden may exceed the value produced even if the system works.
- Demand, payment, and renewal remain unresolved; engineering rigor and design clarity cannot substitute for them.
