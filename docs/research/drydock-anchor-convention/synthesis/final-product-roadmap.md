## Consensus kernel and evidence state

1. **Static proof remains bounded.** `SOURCE_PRESENT`
   Schemas, signatures, fixtures, reducers, diagrams, and validators establish only their static properties. They do not prove containment, operator control, provider behavior, accessibility, or settlement.

2. **Containment and settlement are disjoint authorities.** `PROPOSED`
   The effect boundary may deny, fence, terminate, and report. It may not grade work, suppress evidence, slash value, or settle compensation.

3. **Every consequential claim requires typed witnesses.** `PROPOSED`
   Guest, host, broker, provider, model, replay, and human evidence remain distinct. Signatures, panels, and Merkle inclusion do not upgrade the originating witness class.

4. **Provenance integrity and evaluation adequacy are orthogonal.** `PROPOSED`
   Evaluation claims require an exact `EvaluationEnvelopeV1`; authentic weak testing remains weak evidence.

5. **No decisive self-witness is acceptable.** `PROPOSED`
   Subject, scheduler, controller, recorder, oracle, adjudicator, and UI cannot collapse into one component for terminal claims.

6. **Context preparation cannot admit a successor.** `PROPOSED`
   `PrepareContinuation` may produce Context IR, obligation coverage, omissions, translation evidence, and a nonce. Only the lifecycle writer may consume that nonce after independent fence, effect, and capacity receipts.

7. **Truth is not directive authority.** `PROPOSED`
   Facts, transcripts, and historic intent remain cited data. Only current, audience-bound, revocation-checked guidance may affect framing, tools, or permissions.

8. **Eligibility, capacity, lease, and dispatch are serially joined.** `PROPOSED`
   Structural eligibility → single-use reservation commit → controller consumption → execution lease → durable dispatch intent.

9. **Capacity follows every attempt.** `PROPOSED`
   Lease, renewal, elastic slot, rework, provider-consuming gather, and retry each require a live native-unit reservation and terminal release, settlement, or ambiguity hold.

10. **Human assent is evidence, not execution authority.** `PROPOSED`
    Capture, immutable review, assent, adjudication, permit issuance, redemption, execution, acknowledgement, and witnessing remain separate events.

11. **Machine authority requires a human-semantic binding.** `PROPOSED`
    `OperatorReviewEnvelopeV1` records exactly what consequential semantics, omissions, unknowns, capacity, use count, and expiry were presented. It cannot execute.

12. **Ambiguity is durable.** `PROPOSED`
    Unknown external effects block retries, authority restoration, and capacity reuse until reconciliation or explicit quarantine. Compensation is a separate effect.

13. **The product wedge is one governed coding run.** `PROPOSED`; demand `UNKNOWN`
    One accountable operator, one agent, one repository, one bounded task, one controlled environment, no children, and no economic settlement.

14. **Operator semantics begin in S0.** `PROPOSED`
    Public wording, control ownership, stale/unknown behavior, focus movement, and accessible announcements must be defined before the admission state model hardens. Polished visual composition may come later.

15. **The current packet cannot claim sealed finality.** `SOURCE_PRESENT`

## Explicitly rejected or impossible combinations

The following combinations are rejected:

- Hostile-code containment with raw guest credentials, ambient sockets, host mounts, or canonical-checkout access.
- Complete mediation inferred from application-level policy interception.
- A hard spend ceiling proved only by an internal reservation ledger.
- Universal exactly-once effects after a lost external acknowledgement.
- Automatic retry or capacity release while an effect remains ambiguous.
- One component requesting, authorizing, executing, witnessing, and certifying an effect.
- A manager defining ship criteria and certifying its own success.
- Direct operator approval as an executable permit.
- A projection, selector, filter, repository choice, or evidence browser that mutates lifecycle authority.
- A read-only observatory that can also issue effects.
- One kernel scheduling, recording, minimizing, evaluating, and issuing its own verdict.
- A signed or Merkle-committed statement treated as factual truth or non-equivocation.
- A context steward fencing predecessors, resolving effects, or admitting successors.
- Historical truth or operator intent treated as current directive authority.
- A one-environment V1 described as runtime provider-neutral.
- Static usability success promoted to live accessibility, containment, or cancellation evidence.
- “Immediate Stop” interpreted as immediate externally witnessed shutdown.
- Mobile authority in V1 without separate authentication, pairing, wake, and incident proofs.
- Mandatory bounty, bond, salvage, and dispute escrow for inert, safety, emergency, or no-settlement work.
- Payment or agent ownership claims without identified legal principal, beneficiary, custodian, jurisdiction, and tax treatment.
- A narrow one-run wedge that simultaneously ships swarms, resurrection, economic settlement, mobile control, and a six-destination platform.

## Customer personas and outcome-oriented stories

### 1. Economic buyer and risk co-owner

VP Engineering, CTO, or Developer Platform leader working with security.

- As the accountable buyer, I want one bounded coding-agent run with explicit access, retry, capacity, and effect ceilings so I can evaluate adoption without accepting an open-ended local process.
- I want evidence exported into existing Git, CI, identity, audit, and SIEM systems so I do not have to purchase a replacement operational stack.
- I want native-control equivalence tested honestly so I can reject the product if it adds less value than its privileged deployment burden.

### 2. Platform governor

Platform or security engineer.

- I want reusable policies bound to exact repositories, bodies, tools, targets, and expiries so a valid approval cannot silently authorize a neighboring action.
- I want `UNKNOWN`, `STALE`, `AMBIGUOUS`, and `UNSETTLED` preserved so investigation begins from the real state rather than an aggregate green status.
- I want every enforcement claim linked to its enforcing component and witness class so I can distinguish source presence from actual containment.

### 3. Accountable delegator

Senior engineer or maintainer.

- I want to describe the outcome once and review a plain-language immutable proposal so I understand what may happen before anything starts.
- I want progress, diff, tests, capacity, effects, and evidence in one desktop path so I can intervene without following every command.
- I want Stop shown as delivery, acknowledgement, fence, process, lease, effect, and teardown stages so I am never told shutdown completed before it was witnessed.

### 4. Evidence consumer

Reviewer, incident responder, auditor, or compliance partner.

- I want to move from any summary to the exact admissible or unavailable evidence within two actions so I can verify a claim without accepting the UI’s conclusion.
- I want subordinate failures and witness disagreements preserved so one passing axis cannot conceal another unresolved axis.
- I want immutable exports that identify subject, proposition, witness, evaluation envelope, limitation, and freshness so later review does not depend on a live application.

### Anti-personas

- A developer who wants an unrestricted autonomous agent and accepts native provider controls.
- A team seeking a swarm platform, labor market, wallet, reputation economy, or general observability suite in V1.
- A buyer unwilling to permit the required independent containment and evidence boundary.
- A buyer whose native provider controls already satisfy the governance job.
- A user expecting mobile approval or instant hard shutdown before those paths are independently proved.

## Exact decisions

### Product

- Position the hypothesis as **one governed coding run**, not an autonomous-agent economy.
- Use **no-settlement profile**, not “zero-money,” because model/API spend may still exist.
- Treat provider neutrality as a contract goal until at least two adapters prove it.
- Keep Git host, CI, SIEM, IDE, identity, and audit systems as integrations, not replacement products.
- Require H1–H5 evidence at their earliest valid gates; praise or static enthusiasm is not deployment, payment, or renewal.
- Kill the wedge if native provider controls satisfy the job or privileged deployment costs exceed measured value.

### Engineering

- Use a small single-writer `AdmissionStore`; it may consume commits and append leases/intents but cannot schedule or forecast.
- Represent permanent ambiguity as terminal attempt state `QUARANTINED_UNRESOLVED` while the effect remains `AMBIGUOUS`.
- Permit only a separately reserved no-effect `ObserverLease` for reconciliation; it is not the admitted successor.
- Write effect closure and an idempotent settlement-outbox record in the effect boundary’s local transaction.
- Keep Stop and teardown independent of settlement availability.
- Preserve a typed result vector; never synthesize heterogeneous evidence into one green verdict.
- Replace universal exactly-once claims with provider-specific idempotency, durable intent identity, and explicit ambiguity.
- Limit authority-bearing implementation to two concurrent lanes before S1 passes. Read-only discovery may proceed separately.

### Design

- Put the `OperatorSemanticContract` in S0, not S6.
- V1 has one desktop Control Room path with three destinations: Proposal, Run/Intervene, Review/Evidence.
- FleetBar is projection and deep-link only.
- Observatory is read-only; Switchboard is the one visibly distinct command boundary.
- Every consequential command resolves to the same signed control API.
- Selection, filtering, repository changes, room changes, and evidence navigation emit no lifecycle or capability mutation.
- Show Stop as a staged lifecycle; mobile wording must say “request pending” until host acknowledgement and teardown evidence exist.
- Preserve separate visual and screen-reader semantics for `FAILED`, `UNKNOWN`, `STALE`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `FENCED`, `BLOCKED`, and `UNSETTLED`.
- Two actions is a navigation ceiling, not an accessibility or authorization result. A typed denial or unavailable-evidence record is a valid endpoint.
- Motion remains evidentiary and optional; no continuous activity animation.
