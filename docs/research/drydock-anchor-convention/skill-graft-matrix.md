# Skill graft matrix

This is the convention's explicit context-partitioning contract. A role receives
only the skills required to answer its authority question. A graft supplies
methods and vocabulary; it grants no runtime, repository, financial, or review
authority. Quarantined skills may be inspected as evidence but cannot establish
a safety conclusion until their replacement bundles pass the named audits.

## Cross-review graft

Every reciprocal reviewer also receives:

- `steel-man-argument` for the three-part strongest-case gate;
- `productive-discourse-facilitator` for bounded amendment and dissent;
- `agent-conversation-protocols` for attributable, sparse review messages; and
- the opposing paper, never the opposing author's private scratch context.

The reviewer must name one falsifier for its critique and one concession its own
orthodoxy must make. Neither author may edit the other's sealed position.

## Graft acceptance

A safety-critical graft is accepted only when:

1. its description and NOT-for boundary route correctly;
2. every local reference exists and every external claim has a primary source;
3. its output has a closed schema or an explicit reason a schema is impossible;
4. its validator fails known negative controls;
5. at least six positive and six negative activation cases pass;
6. its changelog identifies changed claims and compatibility; and
7. a graft cannot itself grant the authority it merely advises about.
