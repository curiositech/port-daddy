# Drydock + Anchor Mini-Convention

This packet is a bounded, static constitutional review of the Drydock and
Anchor designs at commit `6c2c30d74b889b4d8037ce5d43b265bfbf21a686`.

It exists to force materially different technical and product doctrines to
state their strongest cases independently, steel-man their conflicts, survive
fresh Engineering, Product, and Design review, and leave a decision ledger
that preserves dissent. It is not a running agent system and authorizes no
Port Daddy runtime, provider call, VM launch, local service, or consequential
effect.

## Truth labels

- `SOURCE_PRESENT`: inspectable at the exact anchor named above.
- `PROPOSED`: a design recommendation, not an implemented capability.
- `UNKNOWN`: evidence is absent, conflicting, or uses the wrong witness.
- `BLOCKED_BY_HALT`: dynamic evidence would require a locally prohibited
  runtime or effect.

No static validator, schema, fixture, diagram, signature, or polished UI may be
promoted to a dynamic claim.

## Convention shape

The convention is deliberately finite:

1. Ten sealed position papers, each owning one irreducible authority question.
2. A manager extraction that records convergence and conflicts without
   inventing consensus.
3. Reciprocal paired reviews. Every critique begins with an attributable
   three-part steel-man.
4. One bounded correction opportunity per paper.
5. Fresh Engineering Manager, Product Manager, and Product Designer reviews.
6. One decision ledger, roadmap, persona/story packet, visual system, and
   dissent register.

Authors cannot delegate. A role cannot create another role. A topology change
is a new, separately approved convention revision. Unresolved conflicts stop as
named alternatives rather than spawning an indefinite discussion tree.

## Steel-man gate

Every paired review begins with exactly three attributable points:

1. The opposing paper's strongest thesis and causal mechanism.
2. Its best evidence and strongest falsifier.
3. The invariant or operator outcome it protects.

Only then may the reviewer identify a tension, request a narrow amendment, or
register dissent. Empty praise, critique without an evidence identifier, and a
restatement of the reviewer's own paper fail the gate.

## Evidence record

Each consequential claim carries:

```text
claimId, evidenceId[], state, sourceClass, locator, contentDigest,
retrievedAt, scope, inferenceChain, falsifier, limitation, requiredWitness
```

Source classes:

- `C0`: immutable local source at an exact commit, path, range, and digest.
- `C1`: primary external specification or paper at an exact version and date.
- `C2`: bounded inference from named C0/C1 premises.
- `C3`: proposal, value judgment, or dissent; never represented as fact.

The manager cannot author a position, approve its own argument, waive a failed
gate, widen authority or budget, or upgrade `UNKNOWN` from plausibility alone.

## Hard limits

- Maximum ten position papers, ten reciprocal paired reviews, three fresh
  reality checks, one correction per position, and one final packet.
- No recursive births and no peer-created work.
- No local Port Daddy runtime or provider invocation.
- Stop after the final manager decision; preserve unresolved dissent.
- Safety-critical skill grafts fail closed. Optional, clearly non-normative
  context may fail open.
