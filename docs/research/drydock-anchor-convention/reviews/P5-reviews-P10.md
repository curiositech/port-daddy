# P5 reviews P10 — exact effect authority versus operator sovereignty

**Round 2 · reciprocal · sealed**

## Steel-man

1. **Strongest thesis — P10:** conversational intent, recommendation, and executable consent must be separate events. The safe sequence is capture, immutable review, and a distinct one-use approval; source-present pd-console currently couples capture and start in an ordinary chat turn.
2. **Best evidence/falsifier — P10:** selection should change only a projection, never lifecycle or authority. The proposed journey separates requested, verified, externally enforced, narrowed, quarantined, and blocked capabilities. A selector that wakes, retargets, or grants power falsifies noninterference.
3. **Protected invariant/operator outcome — P10:** sovereignty must be operable. Semantic controls, keyboard and screen-reader parity, comprehensible state, and externally witnessed stop let a person understand, refuse, narrow, inspect, and terminate delegated power without terminal ritual.

## Strongest unresolved tension

P10 says approval “grants that digest once.” P5 says approval is evidence rather
than executable authority: independent adjudication and controller-owned permit
redemption occur afterward. The operator owns purpose, consent, narrowing,
denial, policy change, and emergency stop. The UI must not therefore become
permit issuer, adjudicator, credential custodian, actuator, and witness.

A human click can authorize consideration. It cannot prove policy compliance or
complete mediation.

## Falsifier for this critique

An exact-build adversarial trace would falsify the critique if approval emits
only a digest-bound `ApprovalAssertion`; direct execution with it is rejected;
a separately keyed adjudicator may still deny; an external controller releases
the channel only after atomic redemption of a body-, tool-, repo-, harbor-, and
effect-bound permit; and independent provider readback closes the effect. One-
byte substitutions and direct bypasses must fail. This remains
`BLOCKED_BY_HALT`.

## Narrow amendment requested from P10

> Approval attests to that immutable digest once; it is not executable
> authority. A separately keyed adjudicator may still deny, and only an external
> controller that owns the named effect channel may accept and redeem the exact
> one-use permit.

## Revision required from P5

P5 concedes that technically exact authorization can still be constitutionally
invalid consent. “The human sees the blast radius” is insufficient. Approval
requires a separate gesture after intent capture, an unchanged review tuple,
semantic and accessible controls, non-color state cues, and comprehension tests
covering body, effect, capacity, budget, and stop. Repository/session selection
is an explicit noninterference falsifier.

## Retained dissent

P5 accepts P10's T0 HOLD and rejects promotion based only on UI separation,
signatures, policy verdicts, or receipts. The storyboard is an unconnected
fixture; ADR-0140 is a Phase-0 verifier, not a reference monitor; same-UID bodies
remain governed rather than contained. The existing adjudicator skill is
quarantined wherever it equates tool-call interception with complete mediation.
