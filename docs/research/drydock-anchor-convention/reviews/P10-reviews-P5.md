## Strongest unresolved tension

P5 achieves machine exactness without establishing human-semantic equivalence. A
permit can bind every byte while the operator sees either an exhausting field
dump or a friendly digest that omits the consequential difference. P5 names a
blast radius but supplies no normative presentation contract binding the view
the person understood to the action the controller may execute.

This is not security versus usability. It is whether those two actions are
provably the same without turning the UI into an authority.

substitution; correctly teach back the consequence; and fail to cross-redeem
approval against an altered permit. Dynamic evidence is `BLOCKED_BY_HALT`.

## Narrow amendment requested from P5

Every operator-dependent `ApprovalAssertion` binds both the `ActionProposal`
digest and an immutable `OperatorReviewEnvelopeV1` digest. The envelope states
the effect in plain language; names principal, body, actuator, target, blast
radius, cost/capacity, use count, and expiry; labels omissions and unknowns; and
zooms to primary evidence within two actions. It is evidence of what was
presented, not executable authority. Any mismatch fails closed.

to the bound review envelope; adjudication may still deny, and the protected

later push, PR, merge, compensation, provider-charge, or other consequential
permits. “No new skill” narrows to no new **human-factors** skill; P5's
consequential-effect authority remains a distinct domain.
