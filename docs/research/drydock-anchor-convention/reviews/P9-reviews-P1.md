## Strongest unresolved tension

P1 gives controller and broker complete power over admission, effects, export, cancellation, and teardown, but does not separate **containment authority** from **labor-settlement authority**.

Complete mediation creates complete leverage. A broker may correctly deny an effect while suppressing evidence needed to prove earned work, stranding a partial-payment claim, or making its cancellation decision dispositive. `HOST_OBSERVED` and `BROKER_OBSERVED` prove topology and requests; they do not prove entitlement, breach, fair grading, or payment. `PROVIDER_RECONCILED` proves charges, not compensation. A platform-owned broker cannot grade, slash, withhold earned bounty, or terminate appeal merely because it enforces capabilities.

“Implemented in Rust” supports memory-safety posture; it does not prove a small TCB, complete mediation, or freedom from semantic authorization bugs. Platform and cryptographic references establish available primitives, not P1's concrete configuration, key custody, or conformance.

## Falsifier for this critique

Admit a signed Float Plan, complete one independently checkable milestone, then crash or force broker refusal after the evidence checkpoint but before ordinary export.

This critique is falsified if an independent settlement component can reconstruct without controller discretion: plan and both principals; completed criterion and witness class; authorized and denied effects; quarantined artifact/evidence root; resource/provider reconciliation; and an atomic termination receipt that releases earned partial payment while closing effect authority.

## Narrow amendment requested from P1

> **Containment authority is not settlement authority.** Every admitted task receives an append-only, host-observed lifecycle record. Every success, denial, cancellation, crash, or teardown atomically closes effect authority and emits a `SETTLEMENT_HANDOFF` receipt accessible to both principals and an independent escrow/dispute authority. The broker may deny effects; it may not grade work, suppress evidence, slash collateral, or make the final payment decision.

## Revision required from P9

P9 must elevate containment from implementation detail to economic precondition:

- Signed or Merkleized agent records remain `GUEST_ASSERTED` until externally checkpointed.
- Every Float Plan criterion names its required witness class.
- Internal escrow reservation does not prove a hard financial ceiling; provider refusal and reconciliation are required.
- No bounty, reputation, or portable outcome settles from work executed with raw credentials, bypassable network, or uncontrolled export.

“Escrow before work” therefore becomes “escrow and externally witnessed containment readiness before work.”

## Retained dissent

`GUEST_ASSERTED` means insufficient alone, not inadmissible. Worker evidence remains relevant when corroborated, particularly for interrupted contributions. Dispute, reputation, and beneficial ownership do not enter the containment TCB; they remain separate governance and legal questions.
