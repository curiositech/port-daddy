# P3 reviews P7 — identity authority versus context authority

**Round 2 · reciprocal · sealed**

## Steel-man

1. **Strongest thesis — P7:** continuity requires an externally governed principal, an exact predecessor boundary, obligation-complete evidence, destination compatibility, and exclusive succession. Its Trust-Typed Context IR correctly treats history as evidence while admitting only verified guidance to authority-bearing channels.
2. **Best evidence/falsifier — P7:** omitted obligations, double redemption, foreign native resume, altered provenance, and compaction drift are sharp failures rather than illustrations. The double-redemption case crosses cognition, identity, and lifecycle authority.
3. **Protected invariant/operator outcome — P7:** remembered prose cannot impersonate the operator, compaction cannot silently erase an obligation, and incomplete translation must become visible discontinuity or blockage rather than a theatrical claim that this is the same agent.

## Strongest unresolved tension

P7 grants the Context Steward too much succession authority. Its `Redeem`
transaction consumes the capsule, mints a successor lease, appends lineage, and
fences the predecessor. Context compilation may establish what the successor
knows, what is omitted, and which capabilities are required. It cannot honestly
determine that an external effect settled, fence a predecessor, or activate a
successor. Those are lifecycle- and effect-authority mutations.

`effectState` must therefore be a projection from an independently issued
effect-reconciliation receipt, not a conclusion the steward may infer. Internal
method separation inside one steward is not independent authority.

## Falsifier for this critique

Place the Context Steward under complete adversarial control. The critique is
falsified if it still cannot fence a predecessor, resolve an unknown effect,
mint or redeem effect authority, activate a successor lease, or make its own
capsule admissible without independently issued lifecycle and broker receipts.

## Narrow amendment requested from P7

Split `Redeem` into two operations:

1. `PrepareContinuation`: the steward emits content-addressed Context IR,
   obligation-coverage proof, capability-translation report, omission manifest,
   and a capsule-consumption nonce.
2. `AdmitSuccessor`: the lifecycle writer consumes that nonce only after
   verifying a predecessor-fence receipt, an effect-broker reconciliation
   receipt bound to an exact high-water mark, and a capacity reservation. It
   alone creates the successor lease and lineage edge.

The steward cannot issue either prerequisite. Every projected effect state must
name issuer, receipt digest, observation time, and terminality.

## Revision required from P3

P3 concedes that saying “historical content is data” is insufficient if a prose
brief flattens obligations, evidence, tool output, and predecessor prompts into
one channel. No successor admission, including cognition-only admission, should
occur without a Context IR digest and obligation-coverage receipt whose channel
translation was checked for the chosen adapter.

P3 also adopts root regeneration: summaries may detect omission but cannot be
the factual substrate of the next generation. Effect receipts, identity
revisions, obligations, and accepted decisions are recompiled from primary
durable records.

## Retained dissent

An unresolved effect must block new effect authority but need not always block a
fenced cognition-only successor that can inspect evidence and help reconcile it.
Likewise, single use should mean at most one **activated** successor generation;
externally witnessed birth attempts that never obtained lease or capability may
remain attempts under the same rebody intent.
