# C-P9 — Make settlement witness classes and ambiguity terminally visible

## Steel-man

P1's paired critique is strongest where it shows that P9's signed plans, Merkle roots, panels, and atomic custody transitions can still manufacture finality unless consequential claims have typed independent witnesses, disagreement remains explicitly `UNSETTLED`, and no claimant, worker, or effect broker controls the settlement credential.

## Correction ledger

- **Disposition:** `ACCEPT`
- **Exact claim delta:** Each `FloatPlanV1` criterion names admissible evidence and required witness classes: `GUEST_ASSERTED` for claimant-, worker-, or subject-controlled reports; `HOST_OBSERVED` for host lifecycle/topology facts; `BROKER_OBSERVED` only for mediated requests and denials; `PROVIDER_RECONCILED` for external use, charges, or refusals; `MODEL_CHECKED` for abstract-state properties; and `REPLAYED` for sealed deterministic traces. Signatures, panels, or Merkle inclusion never upgrade the originating class.
- **Settlement rule:** Missing or conflicting required witnesses, stale/revoked authority, or an ambiguous consequential effect produces a durable nonterminal `UNSETTLED`. It authorizes no disputed payout, slash, or reputation mutation; preserves escrow and append-only evidence; and does not consume the single eventual terminal transition. Previously settled, independently witnessed milestone slices remain valid.
- **External key binding:** The immutable plan hash includes `settlementAuthorityRef`, binding an external controller/principal, key ID, key epoch, scope, revocation source, and conflict exclusions. A terminal receipt binds plan revision, criteria, evidence root, witness receipts, effect dispositions, monetary/reputation deltas, and nonce. The settlement key and escrow writer are inaccessible to and not commonly controlled by claimant, worker, worker runtime, broker, or compensated principal. Rotation without an externally witnessed epoch transition yields `UNSETTLED`.
- **Ambiguous effects:** Every plan-relevant effect remains `CONFIRMED_APPLIED`, `CONFIRMED_NOT_APPLIED`, or `AMBIGUOUS`; a claimant, broker, or worker cannot omit, relabel, or garbage-collect `AMBIGUOUS`. The broker may report mediation facts but cannot infer fault, grade completion, suppress evidence, or settle compensation.
- **Preserved invariants:** Economic principal remains distinct from operational lineage; agent ownership/personhood remains `UNKNOWN`; accepted milestones remain partially payable; noncompletion alone never slashes collateral; containment may stop, fence, quarantine, and preserve evidence without adjudicating blame; appeals remain conflict-excluded; cross-harbor custody still permits exactly one eventual `CLEAR` or `REFUSE/REFUND`.
- **Retained dissent:** `GUEST_ASSERTED` evidence remains admissible and may trigger investigation; it is merely non-decisive. Objective criteria may settle automatically through the pre-bound external authority; this does not require human adjudication for every payment or concede settlement authority to the containment TCB.

Final manager synthesis must accept or reject this addition.
