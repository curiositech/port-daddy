# C-P10 — Keep assent non-executable and preserve emergency containment

## Steel-man

P5 is right that P10's phrase “Approval grants that digest once” collapses accessible operator assent into effect authority: assent may only attest to an immutable review envelope, independent adjudication may still deny, controller-only redemption makes a separately issued permit effective, and Stop/freeze/quarantine may contain risk without manufacturing settlement.

## Correction ledger

- **Disposition:** `ACCEPT`
- **Exact claim delta:** Read P10's “second, explicit grant” as a separate accessible act emitting only a non-executable `ApprovalAssertion` bound to unchanged `ActionProposal` and `OperatorReviewEnvelopeV1` digests. Replace “Approval grants that digest once” with: “Approval attests to those digests once; it cannot execute. A separately keyed adjudicator may deny. Only an external controller owning the named effect channel may atomically redeem a separately issued, exact, one-use permit and release that channel.” Stop/freeze/quarantine remain operator-owned controls for withholding new authority, fencing or quiescing bodies, and imposing durable reconciliation holds. They cannot reclassify an effect, erase `AMBIGUOUS`, prove teardown, release held capacity, or unilaterally declare settlement.
