# Skill audit — `drydock-program-architecture`

**Disposition:** retain as the composition skill and amend its routing. The
focused skills remain normative for their own authorities.

## Steelman

The existing skill already gets the central architecture right: immutable
source provenance, no main-checkout writes, one effect-capable body generation,
external admission, typed effects, native-unit ledgers, fenced resurrection,
operator control outside the subject, witness-typed evidence, and a staged proof
ladder. It also carries a 17-view Mermaid atlas and validated 30-node hypertree
rather than using diagrams as decoration.

## Reconciliation finding

The program skill predated three focused contracts created by this convention.
It described their ideas, but composition prose is not a substitute for a
closed subordinate validator. The skill was amended to route:

- attempt admission, reservation, ambiguity, and settlement handoff to
  `conserved-capacity-admission-and-settlement`;
- context authority, obligation coverage, and backend translation to
  `trust-typed-context-compiler`;
- deterministic schedules, witnesses, replay, and result vectors to
  `trial-basin-deterministic-systems-evaluation`; and
- bonds, bounties, custody, slashing, and payment to the replaced
  `cryptoeconomic-protocol-security` contract.

A composition packet cannot upgrade a failed focused validator.

## Executed evidence

The integrated auditor passes with 24 required files, 17 Markdown files, 48
local links, and exactly 20 diagram views. The resurrection hypertree validates
30 nodes, 12 roles, 7 hyperedges, and 4 launchers. The review-loop fixture
validates 14 ordered events, one bounded rework round, distinct
producer/reviewer/manager identities, and identical HTML/Swift/Rust projection
truth.

## Residual limits

The skill remains a static design method. Its 30-node hypertree and observatory
fixture are not a scheduler, VM, effect broker, provider adapter, or live
operator surface. Under the runtime halt, all dynamic gates remain
`BLOCKED_BY_HALT`.
