## Principal findings

1. **The skill warns about rhetorical laundering but structurally enables it.**
   “Maximum strength” permits replacing evidence and adding expert sources
   without labeling whether a premise was explicit, inferred, supplemented, or
   rejected by the holder.
2. **Evidence and falsifiers are unbound.** Empirical and relational examples
   introduce quantitative, historical, physiological, and psychological claims
   without precise source locators or matching change conditions.
3. **The verifier may be the wrong witness.** “Does this feel fair?” is useful
   feedback, but the user need not be the position holder. Required states are
   `DRAFT`, `SOURCE_BOUND`, and `HOLDER_CONFIRMED`; anything less is not verified.
4. **Abuse handling is contradictory.** A disclaimer can unlock sophisticated
   advocacy under the root rule, while a reference says not to steelman abuse.
   Adopt the categorical boundary: neutral understanding may be bounded;
   persuasion, justification, victim-directed empathy, and optimization are not.
5. **Activation and delegation are not self-contained.** Five routed skill IDs
   do not exist and should not be “fixed” by creating thin overlapping skills.
6. **Privileges and visual proof are overstated.** Analytical work does not need
   write privileges by default. The hand-maintained scorecard misstates lines,
   diagrams, index, and validator readiness.

## Claims to delete or narrow

- Delete unsourced Finland, 5–15-percent, welfare-history, physiology, and
  psychological claims until claim-level primary sources exist.
- Replace “maximum strength” with the strongest reconstruction traceable to the
  holder; label every supplemented premise.
- Reserve `HOLDER_CONFIRMED` for the holder; source comparison yields only
  `SOURCE_BOUND`.
- Treat absent-person motives as hypotheses, not their actual reasons.
- Replace “strongest possible instantiation” with strongest support the holder
  or canonical source could reasonably endorse.
- Replace the disclaimer exception with categorical non-optimization of abuse,
  discrimination, atrocity, scams, or deceptive persuasion.
- Remove phantom delegations and the stale scorecard.

## Replacement contract

The primary output is a **Fidelity Ledger**. For every proposition it records:

- exact source locator;
- status `explicit`, `inferred`, or `supplemented`;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- strongest attributable evidence;
- strongest counterevidence or falsifier;
- source-holder confirmation state;
- correction history.

Thesis, audience, scope, values, and burden of proof lock before strengthening.
Any drift fails the reconstruction. Reciprocal reviews apply the same evidence,
uncertainty, falsifier, and confirmation standard to every side.

- fidelity-ledger schema;
