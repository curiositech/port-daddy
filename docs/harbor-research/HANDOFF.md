### 3.3 SHEAF STATISTICAL-HARNESS AUTOPSY (todo W8's real content; the working minimal proof is sheaf_mechanism_proof.py; the broken wrapper is wrong-turns/sheaf_verdict.py)
**Symptom:** in the wrapper (docs/harbor-research/wrong-turns/sheaf_verdict.py), full-visibility scenario showed 0 redundant detections (should be ~200: pairwise must fire when an equivocator perturbs compared edges) and 0 cohomology-only detections everywhere — coh signal wasn't tracking injected inconsistency at all.
**Diagnosed defects (in my head only until now):**
- **D1 — restriction maps kill coker.** Per-edge random orthonormal S×D projections (S=3, D=6) make the coboundary δ generically SURJECTIVE once edges are plentiful ⇒ coker(δ)=0 ⇒ harmonic space empty ⇒ signal ≡ 0 regardless of data. The scalar minimal case works precisely because identity restrictions give coker = cycle space. **Fix:** restrictions must be coordinate-subset selections (shared-prefix coordinates), or scalar/low-d stalks, so coker(δ) ≅ (cycle space) ⊗ (shared coords) and carries the cocycle constraints.
- **D2 — masking applied inconsistently.** `pairwise` was computed from the FULL disagreement cochain g (before removing uncompared edges) in some paths, and the coh signal never modeled the uncompared entries as unknown — it projected a g that silently contained the "hidden" edge's value. **Fix:** compute g only on compared edges; treat uncompared blocks as free variables.
