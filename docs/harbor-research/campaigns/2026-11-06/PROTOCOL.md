Owner: codex research program. Canonical priorities remain in the Omni ledger.

## Roles and gates

Red constructs reproducible attacks on named claims. White answers the sealed attacks with proofs, narrowed claims or defenses. Blue implements fixes and independent acceptance, rejection and regression checks. The coordinator adjudicates evidence and publishes unresolved obligations; no team may declare its own fix independently verified.

Gate A freezes the target packet and hypotheses. Red's report is sealed before Gate B delivers it to White/Blue. Gate C records each attack, response, independent rerun, verdict and remaining limitation. A further round uses the repaired packet and carries forward every unresolved attack. Research-round identifiers do not impose manuscript version prose or page quotas.

## Isolation truth

The historical launchers are not reused: their isolation failure handling and local Port Daddy calls are unsuitable. Local Port Daddy remains halted. Tool-native agents currently share filesystem/tool authority. Fresh contexts and separate directories provide procedural blindness only. They are not private hostile tenants. Strict inter-team confinement remains an explicit unpassed gate until independent execution boundaries and denied cross-read/write/network probes are recorded.

Reviewed pure formal models may run as host-reviewed processes with that exact label. Arbitrary hostile code and agent runtimes require separate guests with absent home/repository/credential mounts and constrained I/O. A passing symbolic secrecy model does not establish the host's actual process or key distribution boundary.

## Evidence and transcript contract

Preserve visible task assignments, inter-agent messages, tool command arguments, stdout/stderr, exit status, duration, source/configuration hashes, model bounds, negative controls, each team's write-up, and the coordinator's conclusions. Exclude hidden reasoning, credentials, system/developer prompts and unrelated chats. Distinguish raw capture from a reconstructed message record or summary. Hash chains provide tamper evidence relative to a retained anchor, not actor authentication or guaranteed completeness. Missing capture intervals remain explicit.

Screen recording should cover the dedicated research evidence viewer or terminal replay, not unrelated desktop applications. Video supplements exact machine-readable logs; it cannot substitute for them. No recording is claimed until a real file has been captured and checked.
