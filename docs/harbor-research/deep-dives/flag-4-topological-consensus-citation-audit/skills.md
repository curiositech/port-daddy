# Skills — flag 4

## Primary

**`make_copy_and_media_human`** — an unexpected fit, and the sharpest tool here.
Its shibboleth section names the exact failure mode Job 1 is investigating:

> **A quote nobody said is not a pull quote.** […] manufactured gravitas:
> attribute it or kill it.

The skill's discipline generalizes from quotations to citations. A citation with
no verifiable source is manufactured authority, and the remedy is identical:
resolve it or delete it. Its warning about suspiciously round numbers as a
generation tell is precisely why the finding scout was right to flag the round
performance figures in the suspect source.

Note the skill's own boundary, which applies: it edits, it does not accuse. The
finding here is "this citation does not resolve, do not use it," never a claim
about how it came to be reported.

## Secondary

**`harbor-exposition`** — for Q3 and Q8. The positioning sentence against
Herlihy–Shavit is a Move-3 structural analogy problem: state what relation
transfers (topology as the language for what distributed systems can know) and

minimizes it nor treats it as a crisis.

## Standard to hold

This dive's output feeds directly into a real paper's bibliography, and its
whole subject is citation reliability. The bar is therefore: **nothing enters
`findings.md` as a citation unless a URL was fetched and returned the source.**

A reference-list appearance in another paper is not verification. A search-result
snippet is not verification. A remembered bibliographic detail is not
verification. If the PDF was not obtained, the entry is `probable` at best and
says which secondary sources support it.
