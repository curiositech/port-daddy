# Evidence-driven continuation, acquisition, and review

This bounded research run develops three independent studies from the [corpus reading](corpus-reading-20260928.md). The artifacts concern explicit models and prospective experiments. They do not demonstrate a deployed agent system.

## Shared evidence contract

- A model's hidden state may be inspected by the verifier to assess safety, but cannot enter the modeled agent's decision unless an admitted observation reveals it.
- A receipt records a claim or an observed outcome under a declared source, operation, snapshot, and schema. Its presence alone does not establish authenticity, completeness, or truth. The finite models state trust assumptions; they do not implement cryptographic admission.
- Reusing an operation identity and copying an evidence packet have different semantics. Remote deduplication requires the adapter's atomic contract. Copying a packet creates no independent observation.
- Competing policies receive the same authorized evidence and acquisition costs. Additional state, raw claims, trusted readback, deterministic tests, or human adjudication must be explicitly available to every baseline that uses them.
- Each study freezes its hypothesis or outcome contract before checking results. No success rate is inferred from a synthetic arithmetic check, and a planned reviewer case is not an adjudicated held-out sample.
- Safety, progress, diagnostic separation, and statistical reviewer effectiveness are separate properties. Passing one study does not certify the others.
