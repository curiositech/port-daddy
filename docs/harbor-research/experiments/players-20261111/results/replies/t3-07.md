How I decided: the partner punishes a DEFECT with three cycles of mutual DEFECT. During those cycles our best move is also DEFECT, because filing COOPERATE earns 0 and restarts the count. So each engagement comes down to two plans. One is to cooperate forever, worth R/(1-d). The other is to defect and repeat, worth (T + P(d+d²+d³))/(1-d⁴). Here R is what each side earns when both cooperate, T is what we earn when we defect and the partner cooperates, P is what each earns when both defect, and d is the chance of another cycle. DEFECT wins when T−R > (R−P)(d+d²+d³). I worked the cut-offs out by hand and a script gave the same answers. The cut-off sits at a different chance for each payoff table:
