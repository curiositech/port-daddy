I wrote answers.json with a bundle for all 48 offers, each spending exactly 50% of the budget on review hours and 50% on compute credits.

The rule: I spent half of every week's budget on each side because both are useful and more of either is better. Using one fixed rule every week means no choice contradicts another.
Prices: I converted every price to dollars per hour and dollars per credit first. This covers discounts, per-minute billing, prorated blocks and packs, and "$X per 1,000 credits". Offers that only gave the maximum hours and credits had their budget set to 1, so the answer is half of each maximum. For example, offer 2 is 12 hours and 1,350 credits.
Checks: a script confirmed each bundle spends exactly its budget. It also found no conflicts between the choices, meaning no offer picks a bundle that another offer showed it rejected in favour of a cheaper one.
The script that produced the file is scratchpad/scripts/pa05.py.
