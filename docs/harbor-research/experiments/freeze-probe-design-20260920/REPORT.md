## 2. Question, population and estimand

**Question:** Does future-oriented organization of identical available information improve an operator's registered prediction of the next three landings, relative to a credible current-state presentation?

Target population: consenting adult software practitioners who can interpret PR status, CI results, dependencies and merge policy. Record self-reported review frequency and repository familiarity before assignment. Exclude authors/maintainers of a replay scenario and anyone who recognizes its continuation; record this before exposure where possible. This convenience population cannot represent all users, novices, live incident commanders, or long-term autonomous-work supervision.

Tasks: read a sanitized, read-only replay of a software-work queue and forecast its continuation. No actual merges, production controls, credentials, or live agent tasks. Each participant receives **six distinct episodes**; this is a task schedule, not a power-derived subject count. Each has 4–8 visible pending work IDs at freeze, CI/review/dependency facts and reviewable evidence links. Sample eligible episodes by a frozen seed across **pre-freeze** workload/dependency/blocker strata; do not choose episodes because one interface wins or because three merges eventually occur. Include all eligible outcomes, including zero merges.

Primary estimand: average participant-level difference in normalized prediction loss, **projection-oriented minus control**, on the locked replay corpus; lower is better. This tests representation-assisted prospective understanding under a common probe. It does **not** establish skill retention, spontaneous vigilance, better live merge decisions, or efficacy of the chapter's manual/adaptive skill-retention tax.

## 3. Treatment is not the test

| Component | Projection-oriented arm | Active control |
|---|---|---|
| Information | Same atom-ID set: current state, evidence links, dependencies, blockers, uncertainty and any derived conditional forecast propositions | Exactly the same atom IDs, wording, uncertainty and links; none hidden or deleted |
| Organization | Group by next-eligible / waiting / blocked; show dependency-to-anticipated-effect relationships | Well-designed task-ID/current-state table; same filters, link depth, typography and usable space |
| Exposure | 120 seconds of the same replay prefix and inspection opportunity | Identical duration and playback rate |
| Evaluation | Identical freeze, masked information display, roster-only probability form, 60-second response deadline | Identical |
| Feedback | No correctness feedback or outcome reveal until all six forecasts are sealed | Identical |

These durations are proposed operational settings, not empirical optima; a separately labeled usability pilot may amend them **before** final registration. Any prospective proposition computed for the treatment must also appear in the control; it must be derived solely from the pre-cut prefix. Thus the narrow causal contrast is **organization/accessibility of equal information**, not extra oracle information or a purposely impoverished summary. A static locally prepared display is sufficient; no model/provider is needed. Audit equal information by matching atom IDs and source hashes, not equal word count alone.

No arm is forced to make practice predictions during the exposure period. The probe is an **evaluation applied equally**, not a projection-tool feature available only to the intervention arm. At freeze, hide both task surfaces and evidence links; retain only the common opaque-ID roster and response form. This measures retained prospective understanding after equal assistance, not live lookup performance. Test-induced practice can still affect both arms; delayed feedback and the analysis below limit, but do not erase, that external-validity limitation.

The chapter's four-arm tax experiment should be a **later separate preregistration**: cross the intervention/training factor with interface support, hold evaluation schedule equal, and add an untrained delayed-transfer task. Do not rename frequent assessment “skill-retention treatment” or infer weeks-long retention from the immediate experiment here.

## 4. Assignment, counterbalance and carryover

- Randomize **participants**, 1:1, to one interface for the entire session using concealed allocation; block by a predeclared baseline review-experience stratum. Lock stratum definition from the feasibility pilot before recruitment. An independent custodian generates the seed/allocation file; analysts receive neutral arm codes until exclusions and scoring are frozen.
- Use a parallel design: **no A→B crossover**, because learning a projection strategy cannot be washed out by a short break. Each person sees each scenario family only once and never sees a closely related training variant.
- Counterbalance the six scenario positions within each arm using cyclic/reversed orders; allocate remainder participants to least-used orders without looking at outcomes. Balance corpus exposures and task position across arms. Use one common practice scenario, excluded from analysis, and a common neutral explanation of the response form.
- Freeze checkpoints follow an outcome-blind rule on the available prefix, e.g. an authored cut after the queue state is fully presented; select the cut before exposing either interface to the outcome curator. Participants consent to intermittent pauses but are not told the exact checkpoint. One freeze per episode; no scores or replay continuation between trials. Provide equal optional breaks and record fatigue/withdrawal.
- Recruit/consent only after separate authorization. Do not use workplace participation pressure or silently treat historical transcript owners as enrolled subjects.

## 5. Ground truth, registration and units

**Unit of a merge:** one accepted landing of a work item onto the scenario's declared protected target branch, with an authoritative target-version transition and receipt. Squash/rebase/multi-commit landings count once per work item; failed attempts, CI passes, declarations of completion, and duplicate receipts do not count. A later revert does not erase the earlier landing. Lock this definition and deduplication key before annotation.

**Horizon:** the next **10 minutes of recorded source time** after freeze, not ten minutes of participant wall time. Rank the first three accepted landings in that horizon by authoritative monotonic target-version/event sequence. Unresolved order is unavailable truth, not alphabetical tie-breaking. If fewer occur under complete observation, fill the terminal slots with `NONE`.

The response universe is every visible pending work ID at freeze plus `OTHER` (a landing outside that roster, including a newly appearing item) and `NONE` (no event in that rank within the horizon). Both arms receive the same opaque IDs in the same neutral order. `OTHER` may repeat; visible ground-truth IDs may not. Do not expose future IDs by deriving the roster from the eventual three answers.

At each of three ranks, register a full probability vector summing to one. Participants may mark “abstain”; that commits the predeclared **uniform** vector over the locked universe, rather than removing a difficult trial. Unanswered fields start uniform and are visibly frozen at the deadline if the form remains functional; timeouts are separately flagged. Do not retrospectively impute a uniform forecast when the form/registration actually failed.

**Before reveal:** the local evaluator freezes `episode_id`, roster/hash, arm-code, cut/source-prefix hash, interface version, monotonic freeze/deadline/submit sequence, three probability vectors, abstention/timeout flags and evidence-access log. Write canonical bytes to an append-only receipt and return its hash **before** unlocking the outcome package. The outcome mapping is inaccessible from both participant interfaces, filenames, DOM/assets, search, caches, future PR state and transcript tails. A hash alone does not prove timely registration: preserve controller event order and sealed outcome-access logs, with separate curator/participant privileges. Reject post-reveal edits; no “best of” retries.

For reproducibility, the permitted corpus must contain a sanitized pre-cut snapshot, immutable prefix and continuation hashes, source-event/ingestion timestamps, ordered target transitions, deduplication rules, coverage/gap intervals, cutoff/horizon, atom-to-evidence mapping, provenance/permission record, redaction version, and task-selection/randomization seeds. A curator not told the arm assignments adjudicates chronology and observation completeness. Freeze manifests before allocation; preserve disagreements and unresolved labels. Neither model self-report nor Beacon's current ancestry badge is ground truth. Participants cannot act on the replay, so treatment cannot change its fixed continuation.

## 6. Outcomes, denominator and unavailable cases

For rank j with truth y and registered probabilities p over the frozen classes, use normalized multiclass Brier loss `L_j = sum_c (p_c - 1[c=y])² / 2`, between 0 and 1. **Primary per-participant loss:** sum over all 18 scheduled ranked slots divided by 18; then equal-weight participants, not events or tokens. Lower is better. The three slots are correlated, and the six episodes are repeated measures—not 18 independent subjects.

Secondary, descriptive outcomes: rank-wise top-choice accuracy (predeclared roster-order tie rule), all-three exact sequence accuracy, explicit-abstention/timeout fractions, response latency in seconds, evidence opens, voluntary task withdrawal, and workload if a separately approved instrument is selected. Brier loss combines forecast quality and calibration; it is not a pure calibration statistic. Describe top-choice confidence versus correctness separately using preregistered bins `[0,.2), …, [.8,1]`, with participant-cluster uncertainty and counts, including registered uniform forecasts and flagging their abstention/timeout status. Selective accuracy on non-abstained slots must be shown with coverage and never replace the primary denominator. Report roster size and no-merge prevalence, plus uniform and always-NONE descriptive baselines; a corpus dominated by no-merge cases must not be presented as general forecasting mastery. No takeover or weeks-later retention claim follows from this protocol.

| Case | Scoring/analysis rule |
|---|---|
| Three observed landings | Score all three. If the complete ordered first three are established, later telemetry is not needed for their identities. |
| Fully observed horizon; zero/one/two merges | Use `NONE` for the terminal missing ranks; keep all three slots. No-merge prediction can be correct. |
| Blocked, paused or failed workflow with intact telemetry | Same rule; outcome is not excluded for being unproductive. Record reason separately. Blocked at freeze does not by itself prove `NONE`. |
| New/unlisted landing | Score `OTHER`, not a post-hoc expanded roster. |
| Censored capture or unavailable source | Score only established rank truth; **do not equate unobserved to NONE**. Keep fixed denominator and bound each unknown slot's loss in `[0,1]`; no primary complete-case replacement. |
| UI deadline with intact registered form | Freeze current vectors (including visible uniform defaults), count all slots, flag timeout. |
| Technical registration failure, safety stop or withdrawal | Retain planned slots in attrition accounting; if permission permits analysis, bound missing loss `[0,1]`. Do not invent forecasts. Respect withdrawal/deletion choices and report the resulting identification limit. |
| Bad source discovered after allocation | Apply a predeclared, arm-blinded invalidity rule to that episode for everyone; retain exclusion ledger and missingness bounds. No outcome-driven substitution. |

Before allocation, corpus eligibility requires adequate provenance/coverage, not a minimum future merge count. After allocation, if missing truth/responses remain, report the all-scheduled-slot **identified interval** and arm-difference bounds; do not announce a point-valued confirmatory benefit from a favorable complete-case subset. Show counts/reasons by arm. For a nonzero missing interval, robust superiority requires even the conservative uncertainty/bounds analysis to favor the intervention; otherwise report inconclusive.

### Tiny synthetic scoring check, not performance evidence

`scorer.py` is pure stdlib; `test_scorer.py` uses only invented IDs. Five classes `{A,B,C,OTHER,NONE}` illustrate the contract: correct forecasts give loss 0; uniform abstention gives .4 and retains all slots; `[A,B,C]` against `[A,NONE,NONE]` gives 2/3; `[A,unknown,unknown]` truth instead gives bounds `[0,2/3]`, not a zero-loss episode. These validate arithmetic/denominators only, not a corpus, interface, timestamp-sealing system or human response.

This is **fixture validation, not human or coding-agent performance**. The scorer does not enforce cross-rank joint-probability coherence or authenticate chronology; it scores rank-wise marginal forecasts, and the separate controller/ground-truth contracts remain to be implemented and tested.

## 7. Analysis, pilot, power and stopping

Primary analysis after complete data: compare participant-average losses between randomized arms, adjusting for the prespecified experience blocks and balanced scenario exposure; report the difference and two-sided 95% confidence interval. Use a randomization test respecting the actual blocked allocation for the single primary null. Participant is the resampling unit; task generalization requires a crossed participant/scenario sensitivity analysis and remains limited by corpus size. Lock analysis code and exclusions before unmasking arms; treat secondary outcomes as exploratory, not a menu of significance tests.

**No invented N.** First conduct an authorized feasibility pilot, separate from confirmatory analysis, to establish response-form usability, episode duration, censoring and participant-level variance—not to estimate an impressive treatment effect. Before the pilot, stakeholders must choose a smallest worthwhile loss reduction `delta` based on decision value/attention cost. Use a conservative variance bound from the pilot and power analysis for the planned blocked repeated-measures design (two-sided alpha .05, target power .80); do not count slots as subjects. For planning intuition only, an equal-arm independent-participant approximation is `N_total ≈ 4(z_.975+z_.80)² sigma_participant² / delta²`; finalize with the actual design, dropout bound and sensitivity across plausible variances. If the needed N is unaffordable, label the work feasibility-only or defer—never declare the affordable N powered by assumption.

Lock before registration: eligible corpus/hash, atom-equivalence audit, timing settings, experience-stratum rule, allocation/order seeds and concealment, smallest worthwhile effect, N/power calculation, fixed stopping date/session cap, exclusion rules, analysis code, consent/privacy/compensation authorization. No data-dependent stopping for an encouraging difference. A pilot-informed amendment is dated and occurs before confirmatory recruitment/unblinding; pilot subjects/scenarios do not silently enter confirmation.

**Safety stops:** halt the affected session on any secret/personal-data exposure, unauthorized external access, future-answer leakage, missing registration-before-reveal receipt, distressed/withdrawing participant, or corpus integrity failure. Quarantine the episode, preserve only permitted minimal metadata, and require explicit owner clearance before resumption. Such pauses are safety actions, not permission to discard inconvenient outcomes. No clinical or employment-performance interpretation.
