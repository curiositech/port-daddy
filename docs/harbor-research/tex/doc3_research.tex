\noindent\textbf{What this consolidates.} The prior portfolio enumerated eighteen results I judged provable end-to-end (tiers A/B/C). The three reviews then sharpened several, dissolved one, and added three genuinely new theorem candidates. The tooling survey fixed the formal substrate (Lean 4 + Mathlib for algebra/economics, Isabelle/HOL + AFP for noninterference, PRISM-games for inspection equilibria, Z3/cvc5/Gambit as the refutation layer). The sheaf assessment produced a commit-or-cut experiment. This document is the integrated program: what to prove, in what order, with which tool, gated by which experiment, and with an explicit \emph{falsification-first} discipline so the program stays honest.

\section{The discipline: refute before you prove}
Adopt one rule across every result: \textbf{no bound is formalized until it survives an automated counterexample search.} Concretely, for each claimed inequality or equilibrium, first run a randomized parameter sweep through Z3/cvc5 (algebraic bounds), Gambit (finite-game equilibria), or PRISM-games (stochastic-game equilibria); only a claim with zero surviving counterexamples across the sweep is promoted to a Lean/Isabelle proof. This inverts the usual order and catches the errors the reviews caught by hand (the non-PD payoff matrix, the wrong $\delta$ threshold) \emph{mechanically and early}. LLM provers (Goedel-Prover-V2 self-hosted, or the Aristotle API) sit \emph{behind} the kernel as proof-search accelerators; accept only kernel-checked output, and human-review every Lean \emph{statement} (autoformalization can produce a valid proof of the wrong theorem).

\section{Tier A --- days each (the legibility package + quick wins)}
These are short proofs where the care is in the definitions. The first three assemble into one paper (Document 4, Paper 1).

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0em,style=unboxed]
\item[A1. Split-Digest Theorem.] \emph{Lift: 1--2 days. Tool: Lean (order theory) + hand proof.} A single scoring head serves two readers iff their induced preference orders are comonotone; the successor reader (continuation value) and the operator reader (regret-if-ignored) are provably non-comonotone via two constructible items, so no shared head exists. The quantitative half: applying the information floor per reader, a \emph{joint} zero-miss digest pays the sum of the two floors when the critical sets are disjoint. \emph{Gives:} converts the SLM-sidecar architecture from opinion to necessity; upgrades the original split-ranker theorem from example to characterization. \emph{Review impact:} the rigor review independently argues ``the operator digest and the context compaction must be the same provenance-preserving projection'' --- this theorem says \emph{when} that is possible (comonotone readers) and when it is not, sharpening their claim.

\item[A2. Deriving the regret head.] \emph{Lift: 1--2 days. Tool: Lean (real analysis) + hand proof.} $\mathrm{regret}=\text{stakes}\times\text{irreversibility}\times\text{anomaly}$ is not a design choice but the exact expected miss-cost \emph{iff} anomaly is a calibrated posterior; the surfacing rule is then the classical likelihood-ratio criterion, and reputation $r$ enters cleanly as a shift of the class-conditional danger probability. \emph{Gives:} repairs the SDT error (Document 1, Change 4) constructively --- the corrected Chapter I gets a derived optimal alerting policy with a calibration obligation, not a patched heuristic. \emph{This is the single highest-value-per-day item}: it turns a flagged error into a theorem.

\item[A3. $\varepsilon$-conservation for the release ledger.] \emph{Lift: 1 day. Tool: Lean or TLA\textsuperscript{+}/Apalache (state-machine invariant).} Induction on the append-only release transitions gives cumulative $\varepsilon$-spend $=\sum$ recorded releases, identical proof shape to bond conservation, plus imported DP composition. \emph{Gives:} the Sealed Harbor's headline invariant; the structural rhyme (the harbor conserves one more scarce quantity through buckets) that makes the airlock auditable.

\item[A4. Canary detection power + sequential leak testing.] \emph{Lift: 1 day. Tool: hand proof + numpy simulation.} $1-\beta^k$ detection for a $k$-canary leak, plus a Wald SPRT giving expected time-to-detection. \emph{Gives:} assurance-as-a-quantity on the leakage side; re-scopes the exfiltration bond to funding detection (breach damage being unbounded, per the product reviews' own $c=\infty$ admission).

\item[A6. No-mint reputation inheritance.] \emph{Lift: 2--3 days. Tool: Lean (potential-function / supermartingale argument).} Per-outcome derivation budgets split across all derivations make total inherited prior $\le$ total witnessed value, with distillation cycles safe. \emph{Gives:} closes the skill-versioning cold-start gap safely and puts the product reviews' ``Darwinian prompt Elo'' on a sound footing (Document 2 hardening).

\item[A7. The information-floor falsification experiment.] \emph{Lift: hours to first figure; runnable in-container now. Tool: numpy/scipy.} Sweep digest token budget through the floor $\log_2\binom{N}{k}-\log_2\binom{m}{k}$; overlay empirical miss rate; second panel corrupts a feature fraction $\phi$ to trace the mimicry/inflation attack; third panel measures the joint-digest penalty (A1) for disjoint vs.\ overlapping reader sets. \emph{Gives:} the volume's first datum and best figure, and a genuine stress-test --- if real digests beat the floor, the critical formalization is wrong, and finding that out cheaply is the point.
\end{description}

\section{Tier B --- roughly a week each}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0em,style=unboxed]
\item[B1. The digest--zoom Pareto frontier.] \emph{Lift: 1 week. Tool: Lean/hand for the convex program + numpy.} A two-constraint rate--distortion program $R(\delta,f)$ (false negatives priced by $\delta$, false positives by open-budget $f$) with the original information floor as its worst-case corner; plus a \emph{zoom-advantage theorem} quantifying what adaptive second-stage inspection (Hwang-style splitting inside the flagged set) buys over a flat digest. \emph{Gives:} Chapter I's \S I.6 grows from one bound into a small theory with an operator-time knob; the strongest standalone-paper candidate.

\item[B2. The inspection tower.] \emph{Lift: 1--1.5 weeks. Tool: PRISM-games (equilibria) + Gambit (numeric) + Lean/Isabelle (closed form).} Stage result $\rho^\star=G/(dB)$; contraction under sealed sampling from $q$ disjoint principal-cliques giving $\lambda=O(1/q)$, so finite bond capital certifies an infinite tower; and a declining audit schedule proving \emph{reputation is amortized verification} ($O(1)$ lifetime audit spend per honest judge vs.\ $\Theta(T)$ flat). \emph{Review synthesis (the crown jewel):} this is exactly where the two reviews' rate-the-raters proposals fuse --- the product reviews' \emph{model heterogeneity} supplies the disjoint cliques the contraction needs, and their \emph{VRF honeypots} are the randomized audit realizing $\rho^\star$. B2 is the theorem behind both. Converts Conjecture III.11.1 into a parameterized result plus a measurement obligation (does the deployed judge pool satisfy sealed multi-clique sampling?).

\item[B3. Hypervisor enforceability = Ramadge--Wonham controllability.] \emph{Lift: 1 week. Tool: Isabelle/Coq (meta-theorem) + Supremica (concrete synthesis).} Port Daddy's hypervisor can \emph{regiment} policy $P$ iff $P$ is a prefix-closed safety language controllable w.r.t.\ the uncontrollable event set (model-internal steps, in-context content); everything else is detect-and-compensate. \emph{Review synthesis:} this is the formal core of the product reviews' OP-2 (``regimentable iff a pure function of committed local state'') stated correctly and generally, and it is the theorem the Sealed Harbor (C1) depends on. Yields a table re-grading every ledger row against the controllable set: force-push (network event) regimentable; ``confident lie in a report'' (semantic content) forever detect-only.

\item[B4. A tractable deontic-conflict fragment.] \emph{Lift: 1 week. Tool: Z3/cvc5 (Horn + difference logic) + hand proof.} Consistency of a commitment language of ground Horn clauses + interval resource claims + difference constraints is polynomial and \emph{incremental} (unit propagation + interval trees + negative-cycle detection); one step outside (disjunctive obligations) is NP-complete by a SAT reduction. \emph{Review synthesis:} the rigor review's Alice/Bob/Carl chapter needs exactly this --- ``who decides what is a conflict'' splits into mechanical detection (this fragment) and chartered resolution, and the schema designer gets a theorem stating which expressive conveniences forfeit tractability. Composes with B3: since acceptance is a mediated write, conflicting acceptances become \emph{regimentable} pre-commit.

\item[B5. Engine substitution / resurrection soundness.] \emph{Lift: 4--6 days. Tool: Lean (two-period lemons model).} Akerlof unraveling \emph{inside one identity} (build reputation on a strong engine, spend on a weak one); closed by engine attestation, giving reputation on (principal, engine) pairs; resurrection soundness then preserves the sanction-respecting property across provider migration. \emph{Gives:} the Phase-4 flagship demo ships with its own security theorem and names its own attack; one rule closes both engine-substitution and skill-versioning.

\item[B6. The front-loaded probation theorem.] \emph{Lift: 3--5 days. Tool: Lean (exchange argument).} The optimal newcomer-ceiling schedule is maximally front-loaded --- a probation \emph{cliff}, not a gradual ramp --- because the deterrence-to-honest-cost ratio $(\delta_f/\delta_h)^t$ strictly decreases in $t$. Mildly counterintuitive against deferred-compensation instincts, and the resolution is instructive. \emph{Gives:} pins down \emph{which} newcomer schedule repairs the (corrected) identity theorem, with a behavioral prediction a deployment can A/B against churn.

\item[B7. Costly-escalation signaling.] \emph{Lift: 4--6 days. Tool: Lean (single-crossing) + Gambit.} A separating threshold equilibrium for the attention-queue alarm channel, with a computable tuning band $[\delta_\text{min},\delta_\text{max}]$ for the dismissal debit. \emph{Gives:} the ``crying wolf is costly'' mechanism gets existence + an operating band fit from measured validation rates, not vibes.

\item[B8. The specialization boundary.] \emph{Lift: 3--4 days. Tool: Lean/numeric (Erlang-C).} Sole-responsibility roles beat pooling iff the skill-rate premium clears an explicit queueing threshold; includes the single-point-of-failure corner (M/M/1 with breakdowns) that quantifies the succession-rule requirement. \emph{Gives:} the role-lease pattern (Phase 2) gets an honest boundary instead of an aesthetic.

\item[B9. Context paging with an untrusted pin oracle.] \emph{Lift: 4--5 days. Tool: Lean/hand (competitive analysis).} Landlord/weighted-caching competitiveness on the unpinned region plus a linear $\phi$-degradation term for a corrupted pin oracle --- sharing one adversarial knob $\phi$ with the digest theory. \emph{Gives:} the buffered-input layer (Phase 1--2) gets a competitive guarantee and the formal restatement of the effective-context trap.
\end{description}

\section{Tier C --- multi-week}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0em,style=unboxed]
\item[C1. Sealed-Harbor noninterference via unwinding.] \emph{Lift: 2--3 weeks. Tool: Isabelle/HOL + AFP (Rushby intransitive-noninterference is already formalized there).} Domains $\{D,E,G_{\to E},G_{\to D},\text{pub}\}$ with an intransitive flow policy; discharge Rushby's three unwinding conditions per transition class; complete mediation (B3) discharges the ``no other edges'' premise; gate correctness is the named residual oracle. Possibilistic, honestly scoped (timing/termination channels out of model; the theorem bounds channels, not minds). \emph{Review synthesis:} this is the rigor review's ``formal claim worth attempting'' (observational noninterference modulo declassification) made into a mechanized obligation, and it is the airlock's central promise. \emph{Depends on B3.}

\item[C2. Minimum viable take-rate.] \emph{Lift: 2 weeks. Tool: Lean (VCG deficit in a uniform-type model).} Closed-form expected VCG deficit for efficient bilateral trade $\Rightarrow$ the minimum platform fee that buys ex-post efficiency out of the Myerson--Satterthwaite corner; plus the scoped comparison that under common-value contamination (shared oracle) the same budget is better spent on disclosure than subsidy. \emph{Gives:} Chapter 7's most-cited impossibility becomes a priced budget line.

\item[C0 (new). The work-unit transition system.] \emph{Lift: 2--3 weeks. Tool: TLA\textsuperscript{+}/Apalache (bounded model check) $\to$ Isabelle (unbounded safety).} The rigor review's most productive formal object: one work unit plus principals, roles, capabilities, effects, claims, receipts, with transitions (propose, authorize, assign-role, request-effect, admit-effect, checkpoint, assert-claim, verify, contest, reassign, revoke, settle) and six safety properties (every admitted effect has an active grant/correct principal/role epoch/resource mapping; each idempotency key settles at most once; a verified completion has a path to acceptance policy and an admitted verifier result; no child capability more permissive than its parent; role reassignment invalidates stale epochs; every settlement is correctly funded). \emph{Why it leads C-tier:} it is the formal spine of the re-spined volume (Document 1, Move 1) and the substrate every other theorem plugs into --- prove its safety once and A-, B-tier results become properties \emph{of} it rather than free-floating lemmas. Liveness is separated honestly (fair service, reachable approvers, bounded partitions).
\end{description}

\section{The sheaf question: a bounded, decisive experiment}
The sheaf-cohomology assessment reached a conditional verdict, and the decisive test is cheap. \emph{Do this before writing a single sheaf sentence for the volume.} Build a hand-rolled numpy/scipy prototype (PySheaf cannot compute cohomology): gossip graph as a 1-complex with the peer-overlap cover, stalks as delta-vector embeddings of log states, coboundary $\delta$, harmonic residuals via $\ker L_1$. Run honest baseline (must give $H^1 = $ topological $\beta_1$ only) and single-equivocator cases on ring + partition topologies.
\begin{verdict}\textbf{Commit-or-cut gate.} If dim $H^1$ (net of $\beta_1$) does not equal the number of injected equivocations, or if the \emph{partition} regime yields no cohomology-only detection that pairwise digest comparison misses, \textbf{cut} the sheaf chapter to an appendix remark --- cohomology would be renamed equivocation-detection. If both hold, the value-add is real (detection under partial visibility, where pairwise comparison is impossible but cocycle conditions over triangles still bind --- structurally identical to Abramsky--Brandenburger contextuality), and the chapter commits with three theorem candidates (independent-equivocation counting, harmonic-support localization, consistency-radius = max prefix divergence). The consistency-radius result is low-risk and publishable regardless. \emph{Honest note:} the abelianization gap (Car\`u's counterexamples) means vanishing $H^1$ over $\mathbb{R}$ certifies consistency only up to that gap; non-vanishing is a sufficient alarm, never treat vanishing as an all-clear without a pairwise sweep.\end{verdict}

\section{Experiments that matter (adversarial differential, not demos)}
The rigor review's experiment list is the right empirical program; pair each with the theorem it tests.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Raw chat vs.\ typed acts} (duplicate work, hidden commitments, intervention time) --- tests the messaging-layer read bound and the value of deontic typing.
\item \emph{Digest vs.\ claim-graph} (decision accuracy, false belief, time, tokens, source coverage) --- tests A1/A2/B1.
\item \emph{Sidecar attacks} (injection, poisoned evidence, omission, conflicting sources, summary recursion) --- tests the projection-sidecar soundness boundary and the $\phi$-parameter shared with B9.
\item \emph{Hook mode vs.\ confined mode} (bypass success across fs/git/net/credentials/logs/child processes) --- tests B3 and the assurance-level claims empirically; this is also the red-team gate for the Phase-2 ``Confined (level 4)'' claim.
\item \emph{Cross-provider resurrection} (time to useful next action, duplicated effects, obligation loss, final verifier outcome) --- tests B5 and the capsule fidelity metric.
\item \emph{Role failover under kill/partition} (stale effects, mailbox continuity, SLO recovery) --- tests the exclusive-role epoch safety and the CAP trade.
\item \emph{Blind review fleet} (seeded real defects, false-block rate, joint-miss correlation, post-merge rollback) --- tests the independence assumption directly; measures the pairwise/higher-order joint-miss rates that the $(1-d)^k$ story needs.
\item \emph{Lookout/Unspider} (future incidents only; precision, recall, Brier calibration, warning lead time) --- tests prospective-assurance soundness within the declared abstract domain.
\item \emph{Sealed-Harbor red team} (canary exfiltration through network, logs, errors, filenames, length, timing, CPU load, receipt status) --- tests A4 and the C1 side-channel scope honestly.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Week 2:} B3 (hypervisor controllability) --- highest leverage; re-grades the ledger, earns the Phase-2 product claim, and unblocks C1.

\item \textbf{Parallel track (whenever):} the sheaf commit-or-cut experiment (2--3 days, decisive); A3/A4 (Sealed-Harbor invariants, cheap); A6 (skill inheritance).
\item \textbf{The long arcs:} C0 (work-unit transition system --- start early, it is the spine everything plugs into), then C1 (Sealed-Harbor noninterference, gated on B3), then C2.
\item \textbf{The Chapter III pair:} B5 + B6 together (engine substitution + probation cliff).
\item \textbf{The Alice/Bob/Carl core:} B4 (tractable conflict fragment).
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{What I still decline to claim} (in the ledger's spirit): gossip convergence under real partitions (an empirical distribution, correctly graded by the volume); cross-operator attestation closed as a \emph{proof} (a hardware/PKI deployment problem --- specify the TEE / key-transparency evaluation, do not prove the gap shut); folk-theorem cartel bounds at federation (provable only in monitoring models stylized past usefulness); and the sheaf program \emph{beyond} the committed experiment. Every result touching deployment parameters ($d$, $\beta$, $\phi$, calibration maps, validation rates) is a theorem \emph{conditional on measurement} --- the instrumentation is the co-requisite, not the afterthought.
