# Production & Accessibility QA
Read ../../COMMON-PROTOCOL.md and actual Swiss typography/palette/build rules. Record ingestion.
Inspect the final placed render, not only source or standalone SVG. Check clipping, overlapping text/edges, missing arrows, minimum final-size labels, glyphs, caption placement, reading order, links, alt text and index ranges. Enumerate intended foreground/background pairs and measured contrast; meet the operator's AAA text requirement without falsely claiming complete WCAG conformance from color alone. Test keyboard/zoom for the digital board. Keep publication mode free of editorial-only dossiers. Deliver page-level receipts and separate technical-semantics approval from production approval.
