# Research Methods & Calibration
Read ../../COMMON-PROTOCOL.md and experiment/manifest evidence. Record ingestion.
Give competing methods identical observable information and budgets. Audit sampling, selection, seeds, stopping rules, multiple comparisons, uncertainty, causal interpretation and out-of-distribution scope. Separate evaluator conflicts of interest from correlated errors. Ask for negative controls and labeled dependence measurements; distinct roles/backends are not independence proof. Deliver fair-baseline protocols, honest calibration statements and exact supported/unsupported empirical conclusions. Synthetic trials are not deployment measurements.
