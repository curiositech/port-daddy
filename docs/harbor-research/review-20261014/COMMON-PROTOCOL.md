# Shared specialist protocol

Read PROGRAM.md and your own skills/<role>/SKILL.md, then the current repository AGENTS.md and required checked-in references. Pin the manuscript/source revision. Record your actual instance, backend/version when exposed (otherwise unknown), and each skill's content digest after its contents enter your context. Availability is not ingestion. These contracts confer no professional credentials and create no running agents.

Respect the local-runtime halt. Use ordinary isolated Git/worktrees and authorized hosted services only. Reconcile the existing Omni/critique/epistemic authorities; the work register is occupancy, not the research ledger. No credentials, automatic provider spend, force-push, deployment, or bypassed checks.

Report coverage honestly: chapter, sections/pages visited, omitted ranges, sources, commands and retained artifacts. A partial chapter pass remains partial. Empty findings mean only no finding within the stated inspection. Workflow state never upgrades evidence status. First-wave reviews are seeded; fresh cold readers must not receive downstream material or prior verdicts. Shared sources/backend errors are disclosed, not assumed independent.

A finding needs exact source anchor and revision, claim, evidence class, severity, minimal repair, acceptance test, dependencies and limitations. Verify cited sources against primary records; failed search is not proof of nonexistence, and the nearest real title is not automatically the intended source. Preserve distinctions among analogy, formal theorem, finite check, measured result and implementation status.

Work in your assigned exclusive report/proposal directory. Append a new attempt or supersession rather than silently rewriting a receipt. Propose patches with exact context; no simultaneous edits to canonical manuscript/bibliography files. The steward integrates briefly after an independent reviewer checks the repair. Authors do not approve themselves. An adversarial instance has no merge vote and ends after its report; its evidence and dissent remain.

After a shared definition changes, reopen affected summaries, figures, captions, exercises, answer keys, indexes, public excerpts and skills. Reproduce named scripts in isolated fixtures and retain input/output hashes, seeds, environment and limits. Deliberate mutants must fail for the named reason. Do not weaken tests to make a build green.

Figure meaning is checked before beauty. Keep precise text and equations editable; preserve semantic spec, sources, alternatives and selection rationale. Inspect final placed pages at intended size. Essential assumptions belong in the reading path; margins contain concise explanations, citations or reminders. Attribution, bibliography and analytical index are separate deliverables.

Each receipt includes: task/request URL; actual instance/session acknowledgment; role and chapter; source SHA; covered/omitted anchors; ingested skill paths+digests; consulted sources; proposed changes; tests and output hashes; limitations; dependencies; and disposition. Unknown values remain explicitly unknown. Stop after your bounded task; do not simulate a background service.
