## Adopt the visual mechanisms

The strongest features are hierarchy through line and fill, visible arrow
shafts, deliberate routes, explicit grouping, restrained grids, and geometric
markers that remain distinguishable without color. Quantities get axes;
protocol sequences get directed paths; containment gets bounded regions;
lineage gets a tree. A caption does not repair an ambiguous relation.

Book type stays Suisse. Titles remain sentence case. Do not copy the guide's
large all-cap headings, tiny example labels, full-page figure sheets, or long
instruction panels into the Book. Those serve the guide, not the reader.

## Reusable implementation

Both copies of figures/pd-figure-language.tex now define:

- pd guide step: consistent padding, explicit text width and structural outline.
- pd guide arrow: a 7-point Stealth head, a distinct shaft, and endpoint clearance.
- pdflowstep and pdflowlink: place a named check and connect named endpoints.
- pd evidence axis: stronger axes, quiet major grid and native Book labels.
- pd evidence measured / comparison: hollow circles with a solid line versus
  filled squares with a dashed line. The first style is also used for analytic
  examples; the caption, not the style name, must state the evidence status.
- pdindependentmisses(d, count) and pdreviewcost(count): integer-sampled analytic
  curves, with no smoothing that invents fractional reviewers.

These styles are opt-in. Applying bigger arrowheads globally before widening
gaps would recreate the original invisible-shaft defect.

Three figures were revised with these primitives:

1. Boot recovery: equal-width checks, 26-point inter-box gaps before shortening,
   one enclosure and one nearby margin caption. Its status is still partial;
   visual polish does not establish a unified recovery invariant.
2. Purchased assurance: matched reviewer-count axes for log-risk and linear
   cost, shared-error comparison, a 1% target, and the sixth reviewer. At d=.6,
   five reviewers leave 1.024%; six leave .4096%.
3. Deterrence conventions: aligned bars retain four-digit rates; shared input
   parameters move into a short margin caption. The detached note and ambiguous
   percentage comparison are removed, freeing the adjacent warning's anchor.

## The margin also draws

The shared pdmarginexhibit API combines title, native-size content and caption
in one measured outside-column object. The new finite-horizon, joint-miss,
recipient-ledger and tree-residual exhibits apply the guide's compact evidence
approach. See MARGINALIA-PLACEMENT.md for exact owners and approved artwork.

Generated physical analogies never substitute for a numerical chart or protocol
proof. Six of eight candidates were selected after inspection. Drawings carry
a concise explanation of what they illustrate and where the comparison stops.
Keep generation provenance in asset sidecars, not repeated caption labels.

## Do not copy the guide's claims as facts

A positive graph-Laplacian gap does not by itself guarantee a cycle alarm.
A routing curve does not measure latency. A conceptual state diagram does not
prove a deployed enforcement path. A grid cannot make qualitative quantities
into measurements. The manuscript's definitions and proofs decide what a
figure may assert.
