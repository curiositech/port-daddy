Source: [`CriticalAnalysis-2026-09-08.md`](CriticalAnalysis-2026-09-08.md), an
outside review of the whole Book (*The Harbor, the Person, and the Economy*,
Textbook Edition, September 2026) by a reader who reworked the mathematics.
Adjudicated 2026-09-08 against the chapter sources at commit `daf24d62e` and
the local maritime build of the same commit.

## How to read this file

One row per claim the review makes. Verdicts are the four the Book uses for
outside critique:

- **ACCEPT** — the review is right; the change, the chapter and section, and
  what would verify it are named.
- **ACCEPT-IN-PART** — what is taken, what is left, and why.
- **DECLINE** — with a one-sentence reason that is never "that would be work".
- **REFUTED** — the review is mistaken, and the receipt (a file and line, a
  theorem statement, a run) is given. Several premises are older than the
  manuscript; saying so with the receipt is the point of the row.

Receipts cite source files by line (`file:line`) and the Book by section
number. Page numbers, where given, are from the local build (`lb p.`) and are
one line behind the CI build from chapter 1 §1.8 onward, per the plan; they
locate, they do not certify. A verdict without a receipt is an opinion and has
no place below.

Ids are `CA-NNN`, numbered in the order the item appears in the review, so the
rows can be appended to
[`CRITIQUE-LEDGER.md`](../../CRITIQUE-LEDGER.md) as a `CA` source (with the
matching objects in `critique-ledger.json` and a run of
`check_critique_ledger.py`) once the plan owner accepts them. Nothing here
edits the plan; the rows the plan should gain are proposed in the last
section.

The review's theorem numbers all match the current build (1.8.3, 1.11.2,
3.5.1, 4.4.1, 4.6.2, 4.6.3, 5.6.3, 5.6.4, 5.11.1, 6.5.1, 6.5.2, 6.7.1, 6.7.2,
7.7.2, 7.10.1, 8.5.2, 8.5.4 all resolve to the statements the review
describes). Its reading of several *bodies* is older than the source: the
functorial-conservation objection, the Kani-scope objection, the
delegation-chain conflation, the two-equivocator demand and the exfiltration
explanation are each already in the text, and the receipts are in the rows.
