# Marginalia portrait candidates

Research notes for the Book's margin portraits and title pages (plan Wave 12 §12.2:
one per section at most, public-domain or CC-BY/CC-BY-SA only, each committed under
`website-v2/public/whitepaper/plates/marginalia/` with a sidecar naming source, author,
licence and retrieval date, and listed on a generated credits page). **Nothing is
downloaded or committed yet**: the sandbox this branch is built in cannot reach
commons.wikimedia.org, so every row below was gathered from indexed search snippets and
must be confirmed against the file page by whoever fetches the image. Rows marked
"verified" carry a literal on-page licence quote; "indexed" rows are inferred from a
category or mirror page. The machine-readable form is `marginalia-candidates.json`
(same directory), whose `attribution_rules` quote the CC attribution clauses verbatim.

Two rows need a decision before use: Coase's photographs carry the University of
Chicago Law School's bespoke free-use-with-attribution terms, not a CC badge, so the
credits page must quote those terms rather than label them CC BY; and `Wonham.jpg` may
be a group photograph and needs a visual check and a crop. No usable portrait of Peter
Ramadge was found.

## Rejected candidates (and why)

- **File:Leslie Lamport.jpg** (393×500, the Wikipedia-infobox version) — every search returned only Commons' generic site-wide footer text ("Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License; additional terms may apply"), which is boilerplate on *every* Commons page, not this file's own licence tag. Could not verify → excluded.
- **File:Abraham Wald. The spirit of the Ghetto.1902.jpg** and **File:Abraham Wald in his youth.jpg** — false-lead/unrelated-or-different-photo matches from keyword overlap; excluded in favor of the confirmed Oberwolfach MFO photo.
- Group/press-conference photos of Ostrom, Aumann, and Coase (e.g. "Nobel Prize 2009-Press Conference KVA-30.jpg") — multi-person shots, not usable as solo margin portraits; the tighter cropped derivatives were used instead where available.
- Peter Ramadge's Princeton faculty-bio photo, LinkedIn photo — university/platform-copyrighted, not freely licensed. No Commons file exists for him. Reported as **none**, per the task's own fallback instruction.
