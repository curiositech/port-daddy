## Exact falsifiers and review of the root's work

I read both `skills/harbor-results/scripts/scout_claim_counterexamples.py` and `docs/harbor-research/research/scout-claim-counterexamples.md`, independently checked their equations and schedules, and ran the script. All three counterexamples are valid for their explicitly constructed models. These examples refute the scout's overbroad formulations, not the cited primary authors or a measured production implementation.

**D14.** With U(x)=min(x,1), an original balance 2 yields payoff 1. Two children each holding 9/10 have conserved discounted total 9/5 but combined payoff 9/5. For superadditive nondecreasing U, finite induction gives `sum U(b_i) <= U(sum b_i) <= U(x)`. The root's note correctly restricts necessity to unrestricted zero-loss splitting; it does not claim an iff for fixed lossy transfers. Discounting makes the redistribution weakly loss-making in reputation mass, so the scout's phrase “zero-sum internal redistribution” is itself too strong. A step threshold is not automatically a convex function.

**D8.** Four equiprobable states, digest cells `{0,1}` and `{2,3}`, opening budget 2: covering loss is zero. Guessing the exact state from the digest has Bayes error 1/2, versus zero with the full observation. With total variation defined as half the L1 distance, reconstruction deficiency from this digest to the exact-state experiment is also exactly 1/2: a uniform reconstruction in each pair attains it; two indistinguishable states in a pair force at least 1/2 error. The root script checks the decision-risk witness, not a general LP or arbitrary experiment. **Additional scope caution:** if opening the pair reveals the exact state, digest-plus-inspection can recover full information. The experiment must specify whether those later observations are included.

**D29.** Each reservation actor has read then grant, with one initial unit. The six order-respecting interleavings contain four with both reads before both grants, hence two reservations. Both serial orders of atomic check-and-decrement are safe. The root's implementation uses a stale write `free = saw[actor]-1`, making this a coherent lost-update model. A still simpler variant can decrement live free twice and instead reach -1; neither variant proves a real kernel race. The note correctly identifies the missing environment compatibility rather than claiming safety itself lacks intersection closure.

No mathematical correction to those root artifacts is needed. Their evidence boundary is appropriate. The bounded positive utility enumeration illustrates the proof but does not replace it.

## D25: exact binary witness and decoding boundary

Use K4 with ordered edges `01,02,03,12,13,23`. Consistency means an edge label equals `x_u XOR x_v` for some vertex potentials. The code has eight words and minimum edge weight 3. Its 56 received words at edge distance at most one from a codeword are distinct; therefore one arbitrary edge error is correctable.

A single corrupt vertex 0 may introduce

```text
error = (1,1,1,0,0,0) = delta(1_vertex0)
fundamental-cycle syndrome = (0,0,0)
```

This changes the all-zero consistent word to another consistent word. The received evidence is equally compatible with a different honest potential assignment. Thus a syndrome alone cannot even detect every one-vertex alteration. Under the error metric “minimum number of vertices incident to every changed edge,” the minimum nonzero codeword weight is 1 on every connected graph with at least two vertices: every nonempty vertex star is a cut. This is a direct algebraic proof; the finite checker is supplementary.

Pinning one global reference potential removes the global additive ambiguity of potentials, but it does **not** prevent a different unpinned vertex's star attack. Additional trusted semantic facts must constrain the relevant potentials, or the protocol must bind mutually contradictory signed statements to the same subject, proposition, time/epoch and origin. Ordinary edge signatures alone do not establish truth or malicious intent. A pure graph consistency model should not call every changed potential “equivocation.”

The scout also proposes “minimum-weight decoding as T-join/min-cut.” For the cut code and received all-ones vector, distance to the cut indexed by S equals `m - |delta(S)|`. Exact nearest-codeword decoding therefore computes a maximum cut. The ordinary minimum-cut distance computation and arbitrary nearest-cut decoding are different problems. On K4 the minimum cut is 3, maximum cut is 4, and nearest all-ones residual is 2. A T-join decoder belongs to a different incidence/parity formulation; it cannot be imported without identifying that formulation. The finite graph script does not implement a general efficient decoder.

The new checker independently constructs fundamental cycles and enumerates all binary received words on all connected simple labeled graphs with 2–4 vertices. It tests kernel equality, every vertex star, K4 edge-error correction, an omitted-parity-check negative control, the pinned-root limitation, and the all-ones decoding identity.

## Additional theorem repairs

**D1 notation and control boundary.** Basin et al., Section 2.1 and Theorem 3.8, use C for controllable actions, O for only-observable actions, and U for the trace universe. The scout's “lease expiry in U” is a type error. The theorem additionally requires decidability conditions and acceptance of the empty trace. A timeout cannot repair a bad commit that an adversary can perform first while it remains uncontrollable. A lease policy must make the dangerous operation synchronously controllable, constrain the plant so it cannot occur, or accept a weaker post-hoc guarantee. Under delayed partial information the supervisor's observation constraints must be preserved in any reduction. [Primary paper](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/basin/pubs/tissec-enforce2013.pdf), [delay-augmented synthesis, Sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.11273).

**D3 semantics.** For fixed restriction c, meet-with-c is monotone, idempotent and deflationary. Deflationarity alone does not imply idempotence: decrementing a numeric allowance by one is a counterexample. If one instead orders restrictions by reverse authority inclusion, closure terminology may be used, but the whole order and inequalities must reverse consistently. RFC 2693 already defines authorization intersection and validity interval intersection; a claim that no algebraic attenuation law exists is not established. [RFC 2693, Sections 6.3.1–6.3.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2693.txt).

**D6 dependence.** For events M_i with P(M_i)=beta, `max(0,k*beta-k+1) <= P(intersection M_i) <= beta`. If the miss indicators are positively associated, induction on increasing indicator products gives `P(intersection M_i) >= beta^k`, so power is at most `1-beta^k`. Perfectly shared misses attain power `1-beta`. The proposed latent-variable formula is correct under conditional independence with the stated conditional marginal, not under correlation estimates alone. These are direct probability derivations; the agent-specific paper only supplies a concrete application context. [Credential-exfiltration study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04141).

**D13 equilibrium strength.** Qiu et al. Section 3.3 Theorem 1 states Bayesian Nash equilibrium under common prior and the specified score payoffs. It does not provide the scout's new strict coalition theorem. Gao et al. analyze costly information and low-cost coordination signals, directly warning against treating peer agreement as ground truth. A bonded judge also needs audit accountability, enforceable funds and individual rationality. The previously implemented bonded-trigger audit is a finite public repeated-game check with exogenous replenishment, not this new judge mechanism or a finite-bankroll certificate. [Qiu et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20299), [Gao et al. primary manuscript](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~kevinlb/papers/2016-ECDS-PeerPrediction.pdf).

**D26 quantifiers.** A useful conditional statement is: if each of two exposed conflicting statements reaches an honest detector by R rounds with failure probability at most epsilon/2, then a union bound gives detection by R with failure probability at most epsilon. If affected operations in any W-time interval are bounded by B+rW and reaction adds d time, damage after exposure is at most `B+r(R+d)` on that event. A per-operation harm cap is additionally needed to bound monetary or social damage. An average empirical rate is not a deterministic rate envelope. Karp's random phone-call model does not cover arbitrary partitions, and Sheng's quorum-protocol culpability theorem does not automatically apply to a graph of pairwise witnesses. [Karp et al. primary manuscript](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=ad41da70eae0a81c3df258e2eaa281f75faf3687&repid=rep1&type=pdf), [BFT Protocol Forensics, Definition 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.06785).
