Each gets one section, placed in the chapter whose argument it instantiates, written in the book's register (claim labelled by kind, runtime claim labelled by assurance mode, boundary as prominent as the claim). ## Order and cost

1. Front matter method section (short, no build risk beyond a page).
2. Chapter 1: the harness paragraph, then the authority-transfer section (the largest new argument; it also answers Porthole decision 6).
3. Chapter 4: the operator's instrument, then the modes of cooperation (needs the binder read closely; the modes' names are taken from it, not invented).
4. Cross-references from Chapters 3 and 5.

Each section is written by the lead, not a worker, and each lands with its own interim PDF. Names are the repository's current names; where a document uses an older name, the section says so once.
