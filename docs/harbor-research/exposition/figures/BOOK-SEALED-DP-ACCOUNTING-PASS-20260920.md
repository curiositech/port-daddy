## What changed and why

The chapter now distinguishes a conserved ledger from the premises needed to
apply a privacy composition theorem. The conditional corollary names the
neighboring-record relation, conditional pure-DP releases, admissible adaptive
selection, complete observer-visible interaction (including refusals, metadata
and timing), and the prospective spending guard. The bookkeeping induction
proves neither the release-privacy nor complete-mediation premise.

Fixed caps and a maximum horizon do not forbid adaptive queries or mechanisms.
Early stopping retains the maximum-horizon bound; substituting realized
adaptive costs or a realized stopping count into the fixed-parameter formula
does not establish a valid filter. Whitehouse et al.'s result matches advanced
composition rates and leading constants under its stopping rule, rather than
having the previously asserted worse constants. It is a distinct approximate-DP
filter, not implemented by A3's sum-only ledger. Large k alone need not make
advanced composition preferable.

The worked comparison retains its inputs and values (32/.1/3.2/3.31 and
128/.05/6.4/3.30), but explicitly trades a smaller epsilon for positive delta.
It no longer calls that a uniformly stronger guarantee. The repeated
related-work discussion receives the same correction. The module docstring
and printed explanatory prose in A3 match this scope; no computation changes.

Primary sources consulted for these specific boundaries, not a claim to have
independently verified every proof:

- Dwork, Rothblum and Vadhan, [Boosting and Differential Privacy](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2010/01/DworkRV10.pdf), adaptive composition definitions and fixed-parameter bounds.
- Rogers et al., [Privacy Odometers and Filters](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/6170-privacy-odometers-and-filters-pay-as-you-go-composition.pdf), adaptive composition game, filter definitions and basic filter theorem.
- Whitehouse et al., [Fully-Adaptive Composition in Differential Privacy](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/whitehouse23a/whitehouse23a.pdf), conditional privacy, approximate-DP filter and leading-constant comparison.
