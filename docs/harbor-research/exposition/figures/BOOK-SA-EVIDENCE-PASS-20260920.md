regardless of its zoom. That universal claim was not supported by the cited study.
The primary navigation experiment reports a comprehension difference between manual
and full automation, but no significant perception difference. Its measured task
does not establish a universal effect of present-focused coding-agent digests.
The revised prose retains both citation keys, narrows the study claim, and identifies
projection support and the next-three-merges probe as proposals, not completed validation.
