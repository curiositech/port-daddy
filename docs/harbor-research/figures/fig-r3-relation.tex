\caption{The relation-map for Theorem 3. Three relations transfer, and they are the section in order: expected loss is magnitude times probability, so the rule must weigh what being wrong costs and not merely how unusual the reading is; raising the miss cost \emph{lowers} the threshold at which to act (drawn in row 2, $a^\star$ moving from $0.057$ to $0.006$ as $C_{\mathrm{miss}}$ goes $100\to1000$ [verified]); and a rule that surfaces too much is miscalibrated in probability rather than over-sensitive, which is why $\mathrm{anomaly}=\Pr(\mathsf{bad}\mid\text{evidence})$ is an obligation and not a naming convention.}
