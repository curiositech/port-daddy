\caption{The relation-map for Theorem 2. Row 3 draws the crossing pair the proof constructs --- a routine working state (high continuation value, negligible regret) against an abandoned dead-end with irreversible side effects (zero continuation value, maximal regret) --- which is what makes the impossibility bite in real fleets rather than merely in principle.}
