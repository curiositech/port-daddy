\caption{The relation-map for Theorem 2. The transferred relation is not that a summary resembles a temperature; it is that one scalar ranking serves two readers with strict total orders iff their orders coincide. A tie in one reader's order may be refined to serve both. Row 3 draws the crossing pair the proof constructs --- a routine working state (high continuation value, negligible regret) against an abandoned dead-end with irreversible side effects (zero continuation value, maximal regret) --- which is what makes the impossibility bite in real fleets rather than merely in principle.}
