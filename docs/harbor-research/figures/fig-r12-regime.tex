\caption{Regime diagram for Theorem 1: total inherited credit from one unit-value episode along a depth-3 full-weight chain, as a
function of the discount $\gamma$. Two thresholds, kept apart. \emph{This depth-3 chain} crosses the mint line at
$\gamma\approx0.5437$, the root of $\gamma+\gamma^2+\gamma^3=1$ --- the dotted rule, bracketed by the plotted points
$(0.5,0.875)$ and $(0.5833,1.1221)$ --- and reaches $2.439$ at $\gamma=0.9$, the refuting counterexample [internal,
\texttt{a6\_no\_mint.py}]. The familiar $\gamma=\tfrac12$ is the threshold for the budget-only \emph{scheme}, not for this chain:
it is where the depth-limit bound $\sum_{d\ge1}\gamma^d=\gamma/(1-\gamma)$ first exceeds $1$, so above it some chain of sufficient
depth mints, with the required depth growing as $\gamma$ falls. Transfer semantics holds the live total at the witnessed value for
every $\gamma$, which is why one line carries both labels.}
