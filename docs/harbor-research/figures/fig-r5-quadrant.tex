% Conceptual control/observation grid for this paper's example policies.
% It is not a universal iff test for arbitrary policy languages. The
% controllability checker supplies the declared-plant control verdicts;
% the observation premise is separate. Missing control or observation
% does not itself establish detectability. Exact realization differs
% from safety obtained by rejecting additional legal histories.

\caption{Control and observation for the stated examples. The upper-right
policies meet both requirements for exact realization; arbitrary policies
still require the full language conditions in \S\ref{sec:theorem} and
\S\ref{sec:partial}. A hidden trigger can prevent an exact guard even when
the effect is controllable. Blocking that effect on additional histories can
still preserve safety. Widening control and improving observations can change
these classifications; neither establishes a detector by itself. The lower-left
policy is a constructed illustration. [internal]}
