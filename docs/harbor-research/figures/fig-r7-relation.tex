\caption{The relation map for the stage game. Left: traffic enforcement --- a driver obeys when expected fine $F\cdot p$ beats the time saved $T$, and the city need not catch every car. Right: agent honesty --- a judge is honest when $\rho\, d\, B \ge G$, giving the critical audit rate $\rho^\star = G/(dB)$. The arrows carry the load: expected fine $\Leftrightarrow$ expected slash, patrol budget $\Leftrightarrow$ audit rate. The analogy maps \emph{relations}, not surface features: both systems price deterrence, neither buys omniscience.}
