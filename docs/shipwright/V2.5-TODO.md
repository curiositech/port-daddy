### A4 · Email magic-link race (Bonded §7.x) [HIGH]
- **Smell origin:** v2.0→v2.1, staged
- **Paper says:** single-use TTL token with bounded lifetime
- **Missing:** ProVerif state-machine model (issued → consumed →

- **Artifact target:** `proofs/bonded/recovery/magic-link.pv`

  tight; v2.3 federated proof assumes single-use
