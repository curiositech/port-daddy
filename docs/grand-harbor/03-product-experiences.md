# Product experiences

**Status:** PROPOSED ACCEPTANCE SCENARIOS  
**Authority:** product projection; these scenarios do not amend contracts  
**Target dates:** planning hypotheses from the 2026-09-02 synthesis, not delivery commitments

Architecture is useful only when it changes what Erich, collaborators, reviewers, owners, and buyers can truthfully do. These stable experience IDs are the acceptance layer between the contracts and the implementation program.

## GH-E-001 — Mediated development day

**Proposed target:** 2026-10-15

Erich spends one complete development day using only Port Daddy-launched coding actors. No actor receives a reusable GitHub or provider secret. Every external write is attributable to a typed request, authorization decision, effect observation, and receipt. Direct provider CLI sessions are not required.

**Proof:** a bounded event interval; process and secret-custody inventory; external-effect receipts; no unexplained privileged process.

## GH-E-002 — Durable person across bodies

**Proposed target:** 2026-10-31

During a real feature, Erich kills Builder's provider-A body and creates a provider-B body for the same durable `AgentNode`. Builder resumes with accountable identity, obligations, intention lineage, relevant context, claims, grants, and evidence without Erich retelling the feature.

The promise is observable continuity, not recovery of hidden provider activations. A signed, content-addressed portable task capsule records the last proven event cursor, goals, acceptance criteria, actor lineage, tool I/O, hypotheses, claims, grants, pending operations, idempotency keys, and provenance. Every inherited, revalidated, omitted, unavailable, or unknown item is exposed.

**Proof:** one `AgentNode`, two bodies, credential verification, continuity manifest, obligation acknowledgement, uncertain external-state reconciliation, uninterrupted attribution.

## GH-E-003 — Abrupt ideas without erasure

**Proposed target:** 2026-10-31

Erich introduces ten abrupt ideas while work continues. Each source utterance remains durable and is proposed as `refines`, `adds`, `interrupts`, `contradicts`, `supersedes`, `questions`, or `parks`. Existing commitments do not silently disappear, and ambiguity may remain explicit.

**Proof:** raw utterances, interpretation proposals, signed/authorized transitions, rejection history, surviving intention graph.

## GH-E-004 — Interrupt, handle urgency, resume

**Proposed target:** 2026-11-15

Erich interrupts a long-running feature with an urgent bug, finishes the bug, and sees the earlier intention preserved with resume conditions and drift. He explicitly resumes, replans, supersedes, or abandons it; the actor does not restart from generic context.

**Proof:** active → interrupted → explicit terminal/resume transition; claims, plan lineage, and evidence retained.

## GH-E-005 — Disagreement becomes protocol

**Proposed target:** 2026-11-15

At least three genuine architecture contradictions become structured Parleys with a literal disputed proposition, protocol roles, evidence, deadlines, failure/cancellation semantics, and an honest terminal state. Agreed, rejected, and unresolved propositions remain separate. Any Chartroom mutation is still proposed through its own authority path.

**Proof:** three protocol transcripts plus downstream proposals and unresolved residue.

## GH-E-006 — Effects pass through one choke point

**Proposed target:** 2026-11-30

GitHub writes, filesystem mutation, process spawn, agent-body spawn, and browser/native embodiment actions use typed actuator contracts. A significant assertion such as “published” links to what was requested, what was authorized, what was attempted, what was observed, and what remains unknown.

**Proof:** complete-mediation inventory; denial tests; GitHub read-after-write vertical proof; no sanctioned bypass.

## GH-E-007 — One truthful workstation

**Proposed target:** 2026-12-31

Erich ships one useful browser or native application from pd-console without using Codex or Claude Code as the primary development UI. Chartroom shows why, Claim Forest shows where, Actors shows who, action review shows what is authorized, and Porthole shows what happened.

**Proof:** the full flow in [GH-CUT-001](program/CUT-001-first-vertical-proof.md), including embodiment evidence and publication receipt.

## GH-E-008 — Claims plus stigmergic sensing

**Proposed target:** 2026-12-15

Erich watches two actors and himself work on overlapping repository scopes. Solid, addressable claims expose hard coordination state. Separately inspectable, decaying pheromones expose attention, freshness, contention, uncertainty, risk, novelty, abandonment, and opportunity. Removing every pheromone changes ranking and suggestions but cannot change ownership, authority, or completion.

**Proof:** combined projection; conflict/salvage flow; no-signal invariance test.

## GH-E-009 — Cooperative sovereign Harbors

**Proposed target:** 2027-01-31

One feature crosses Erich's devices and one crosses to another person. Each Harbor remains sovereign. Resource access, action requests, disclosure, budget, and evidence acceptance are directional and explicitly granted. A conventional GitHub collaborator can participate through an adapter whose weaker guarantees are labeled.

**Proof:** native Relay flow plus adapter flow; revocation and divergence scenario; no ambient authority transfer.

## GH-E-010 — Experience changes selection

**Proposed target:** 2027-02-28

Two competing implementations of a decomposition, matching, review, or scheduling function are evaluated on stratified tasks. Port Daddy records evidence available at selection, candidate modules, chosen module, policy version, outcome vector, uncertainty, and regret. At least one policy change improves held-out performance rather than merely incrementing an Elo counter.

**Proof:** predeclared experiment, control/treatment or shadow evaluation, held-out result, rollback criterion, provenance-bound graft versions.

## GH-E-011 — Capability becomes livelihood and legacy

**Proposed target:** 2027-03-31

Erich receives the first dollar for a skill, evaluator, router, or protocol implementation under explicit price, data, execution-cost, evidence, failure, refund, and settlement contracts. Buyer revenue, provider COGS authority, platform compensation, and settlement holds remain separate. Separately, one coordination-paper claim becomes an executable policy/skill/evaluator with a research-grade provenance dataset.

The illustrative `$10 revenue → at most $8 execution COGS → at least $2 retained margin` split is a configurable policy example, not a constitutional constant.

**Proof:** economic authority lineage `RevenueReceipt → EconomicAuthority → SpendGrant`; reserve/measure/commit/release accounting; witnessed outcome; disclosure contract; exportable evidence corpus.

## Acceptance discipline

- “Implemented” is not “experienced.”
- “Receipt exists” is not “good outcome.”
- Aggregate green status cannot hide a stale-head or failed child proof.
- A target date can move without weakening the scenario.
- If a scenario cannot be proved with named evidence, it remains proposed.
