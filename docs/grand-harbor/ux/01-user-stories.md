# Personas and user stories

**Status:** UX PROPOSAL

## Personas

| Persona | Desired outcome | Failure the product removes |
|---|---|---|
| Erich, owner/operator/developer | resume quickly, redirect without erasure, control cost, ship, preserve research/product legacy | reconstructing truth from chats, terminals, PR comments and stale roadmaps |
| Native collaborator | work concurrently with explicit intent, claims, grants and proof | ambiguous ownership and ambient credentials |
| Ordinary collaborator | participate through GitHub or another adapter | being required to install Port Daddy, or being shown stronger semantics than the adapter has |
| Maintainer/reviewer | inspect exact-head architecture and proof | polished aggregates hiding missing/stale evidence |
| Convoy developer | package actors, protocols, embodiments, budgets, outcomes | bespoke orchestration scripts per app |
| Skill publisher | offer a versioned cognitive module with measured evidence | reputation/payment without identity or outcomes |
| Skill buyer | bound price, COGS, data, evidence and failure | opaque execution and uncontrolled API liability |

Actors are represented participants, not personas. The interface can be personable without anthropomorphizing away body, role, authority, or evidence distinctions.

## Orientation and intent

- **BR-01:** As Erich, I return after several days and see active, interrupted, blocked, next, and parked work without reading transcripts.
- **BR-02:** Every blocker names the missing decision, grant, claim, dependency, budget, observation, or proof.
- **BR-03:** I can follow any status to the exact source sequence and governing contract.
- **CH-01:** A material instruction is durably captured before semantic interpretation finishes.
- **CH-02:** I review exactly what an interpretation proposes to add, refine, interrupt, contradict, supersede, question, or park.
- **CH-03:** Rejecting a proposal preserves source utterance, evidence, and prior state.
- **CH-04:** Ambiguity can remain a question instead of becoming a false decision.
- **CH-05:** Interrupting preserves the prior intention, resume conditions, claims, notes, and evidence.
- **CH-06:** A constitutional contradiction leads to an amendment path, not an ordinary mutation button.

## Claims and stigmergy

- **WK-01:** Before editing, a human or actor sees parent, child, peer, file, symbol, or range overlap.
- **WK-02:** Compatible and incompatible overlap use shape/labels as well as color.
- **WK-03:** A stale claim held by a ghost ship starts an explicit salvage flow; it is never silently reassigned.
- **WK-04:** Every pheromone exposes source, scope, evidence, strength, creation time, half-life, and dispute state.
- **WK-05:** Disabling all signals changes suggestions and visualization but cannot change authority.
- **WK-06:** Conflicting advisory signals remain inspectable rather than becoming one unexplained risk score.

## Actor continuity

- **AC-01:** I distinguish a durable person from current body, provider, session, office, and protocol role.
- **AC-02:** Body loss preserves obligations, claims, grants, intention lineage, and outcomes under their own lifecycles.
- **AC-03:** Resurrection classifies state as inherited, revalidated, selectively restored, omitted, unavailable, or unknown.
- **AC-04:** A replacement body proves its right to embody the person.
- **AC-05:** External state that may have changed during loss is observed before retry/resume.
- **AC-06:** An existing permit is never assumed to transfer while policy is unresolved.

## Governed effects

- **PA-01:** Before authorizing, I see actor/body, Harbor, typed target/payload, preconditions, grant, budget, expiry, expected state, idempotency and irreversibility.
- **PA-02:** The control says `Authorize exact action once`, not `Confirm` or `Approve`.
- **PA-03:** Scope revision creates a new ActionIntent rather than mutating an issued permit.
- **PA-04:** submitted, denied, permitted, executing, observed, received, and outcome-assessed remain separate.
- **PA-05:** provider timeout after dispatch becomes indeterminate, not automatically failed or successful.
- **PA-06:** rollback is a new governed action.
- **PA-07:** batch review retains independent permits, receipts, costs, and failures per row.
- **PA-08:** automatic decisions expose predicate evidence and policy epoch.

## Evidence and Porthole

- **EV-01:** From “this shipped,” I traverse requirement → implementation → test → authorization → external observation → outcome.
- **EV-02:** The first missing, stale, conflicting, redacted, or untrusted link is named.
- **EV-03:** replay cannot repeat live actions.
- **EV-04:** event and observation times are separate.
- **EV-05:** later external mutation cannot rewrite what the receipt observed.
- **EV-06:** terminal output, browser frames, and screenshots are evidence attachments, not the ledger itself.

## Parley

- **PY-01:** A Parley begins with a literal disputed proposition.
- **PY-02:** protocol roles are shown before bound actors.
- **PY-03:** delivered, understood, accepted, and agreed are distinct.
- **PY-04:** resolution separates agreed, rejected, and unresolved propositions.
- **PY-05:** terminal Parley proposes downstream Chartroom changes; it never silently applies them.
- **PY-06:** timeout, cancellation, `not-understood`, impasse, and protocol violation remain visible.

## Relay, budgets, and future economy

- **RL-01:** each cross-Harbor capability is directional, scoped, expiring, attributable, and inspectable.
- **RL-02:** transport connectivity cannot make an expired/revoked grant usable.
- **RL-03:** remote assertion and locally acceptable evidence are labeled separately.
- **RL-04:** adapters expose weaker identity/delivery/evidence semantics.
- **BD-01:** buyer revenue and provider COGS envelope are separate.
- **BD-02:** cost truth comes from the boundary holding the credential/observing provider usage.
- **BD-03:** a denial names the exhausted budget and smallest needed grant.
- **BD-04:** failure does not erase genuine provider COGS.
- **BD-05:** delayed, unattributed, or disputed usage remains an explicit liability.
- **AN-01:** a future buyer sees price, data, evidence, failure, refund, and settlement terms before contracting.

## Research legacy

- **LG-01:** Erich can trace a deployed policy/evaluator back to the paper/skill/decision that motivated it.
- **LG-02:** An experiment exports task strata, versions, evidence, outcomes, uncertainty, and exclusions without private data leakage.
- **LG-03:** A later paper can cite a stable evidence corpus rather than reconstructed chat history.
