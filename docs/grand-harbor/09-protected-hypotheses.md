# Protected hypotheses

**Status:** EXPERIMENTS; NEITHER ACCEPTED ARCHITECTURE NOR TRASH

Skepticism must be able to narrow a hypothesis without deleting its motivation. Each experiment inherits the constitution and must declare what it is forbidden to decide.

## GH-H-001 — Stigmergic coordination sensing

Decaying, provenance-bearing signals improve task discovery, contention avoidance, abandoned-work detection, novelty discovery, and routing priors.

**Must preserve:** no capability, claim, assignment, completion, or factual authority; bounded strength and decay; signals separately inspectable.  
**Current evidence:** local entity pheromones, resolution damping, file heat, read-time/background decay, and tests are implemented. Full graph-Laplacian diffusion and planner replacement are not.

## GH-H-002 — Sheaf inconsistency localization

Typed local views and overlap constraints may identify the smallest inconsistent region of a multi-actor program graph more usefully than a global “conflict” flag.

**Must preserve:** output is diagnostic evidence, not a decision; unresolved mathematical/modeling assumptions stay explicit.

## GH-H-003 — Adversarial steelman

A reviewer that must first reconstruct the strongest compatible interpretation of a proposal may reduce reflexive rejection and sycophantic replacement.

**Must preserve:** the steelman cannot commit a mutation or hide a contradiction.

## GH-H-004 — Replaceable selection functions

Task decomposition, skill matching, actor matching, plan selection, intention scheduling, context selection, memory promotion, merge ordering, and evaluation can be replaceable modules selected from witnessed experience.

**Must preserve:** pre-selection evidence, uncertainty, credit-assignment limits, shadow/control comparison, rollback, and held-out evaluation.

## GH-H-005 — Organizational offices

Stable offices with obligations and protocol roles may outlive the particular actor filling them, enabling cooperation without confusing person and role.

**Must preserve:** office is not identity, credential, or ambient authority.

## GH-H-006 — Forever-backwards Harbor search

A semantic query can traverse event → actor → intention → action → receipt → observation → artifact → decision while preserving exact provenance and temporal state.

**Must preserve:** similarity is not causality; replay is inert; first missing/stale/untrusted link is named.

## GH-H-007 — Confidential skill market

Remote confidential execution plus evidence commitments may permit buyers to invoke protected cognitive modules without disclosing unnecessary task state or implementation.

**Must preserve:** identity and witnessed outcomes precede reputation; data/evidence/settlement contracts are explicit; the economic layer remains downstream of authority truth.

## GH-H-008 — Formalized action policy

Some high-value invariants may be compiled or proved with Cedar, Datalog/Soufflé, generated Rust, Lean, TLA⁺, or a composition.

**Must preserve:** the policy engine is not the state store, authenticator, secret broker, actuator, receipt verifier, or complete-mediation proof. Candidate selection remains open.

## GH-H-009 — Embodied cooperative development

Browser, terminal, iOS Simulator, and macOS adapters implementing a common observation/action/evidence contract can let humans and actors inhabit the product under construction.

**Must preserve:** every mutating action still requires the normal authority path; screenshots are attachments, not outcome truth.
