## Art-preservation contract

The supplied jacket, inside-jacket photograph, Chapter VII plate, and four selected coda plates are copied byte-for-byte into `website-v2/public/whitepaper/art/collected-volume/`. The LaTeX plate macros use `keepaspectratio`; they do not trim, clip, recolor, retouch, or overlay the artwork. White space is intentional and protects the original composition. Two otherwise plausible extras were deliberately excluded because their embedded lettering was malformed and the available replacements conflicted with the no-old-book visual direction.

The analytical figures remain distinct from the editorial plates. Their structural palette is restrained to ink, warm paper neutrals, and a single cobalt accent; warning states retain redundant labels and line styles so color never carries meaning by itself.
