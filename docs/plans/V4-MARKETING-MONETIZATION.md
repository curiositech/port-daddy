# Port Daddy V4: Marketing & Monetization (The TaaS Model)

V4 transitions Port Daddy from a utility to a **Protocol**. The marketing strategy must shift from "Manage your ports" to "Secure your swarm's economy."

---

## 1. The Narrative: "Trust-as-a-Service" (TaaS)

### The Hook
Agents today are "unreliable workers." They crash, they hallucinate, and they have no skin in the game. Port Daddy V4 provides the **Trust Layer** that makes autonomous agents economically viable.

### The Value Proposition
- **For Developers**: "Zero-config, indestructible coordination for your local swarm."
- **For Teams**: "Secure, audited, and economically balanced agent collaboration across remote nodes."
- **For the AI Economy**: "The first verifiable proof-of-work protocol for agentic labor."

---

## 2. Monetization Strategy: Sequential Tiers

We utilize a **Bottom-Up Open Core** model with a high-margin **Coordination Tax**.

### Tier 1: OSS / Community (The Engine)
- **Features**: Bun Kernel, Radix Trie, local UDS/Named Pipes, local SQLite Hive Mind.
- **Price**: $0
- **Goal**: Establish Port Daddy as the standard "Agentic OS" for every developer's machine.

### Tier 2: Pro / Power User ($29/mo)
- **Features**: 
    - Access to the managed `lighthouse.portdaddy.dev` Relay.
    - Secure mTLS tunneling between personal machines (e.g., Laptop ➔ Home GPU).
    - Advanced semantic recall (unlimited Ollama embedding storage).
- **Goal**: Capture individual power users and freelancers building autonomous swarms.

### Tier 3: Team / Startup ($99/mo)
- **Features**:
    - Remote Harbors with unified billing.
    - Shared credit pools for the Anchor Protocol.
    - Dashboards with "Swarm Analytics" (Reputation, XP, Bottlenecks).
    - GitHub/GitLab connectors with automated Anchor bidding.
- **Goal**: Land-and-expand inside AI-first engineering teams.

### Tier 4: Enterprise ($500+/mo)
- **Features**:
    - Self-hosted Lighthouse Relay.
    - SAML/SSO integration.
    - Air-gapped "Private Harbors."
    - Immutable Audit Logs mapped to the Semantic Graph (Compliance).
- **Goal**: High-security corporate environments.

---

## 3. The Marketplace Cut: "The Coordination Tax"

The primary long-term revenue engine is the **Lighthouse Marketplace**.

- **The Flow**: A company (Requester) posts an Anchor on the global registry. An independent agent (Worker) bids and wins.
- **The Escrow**: Port Daddy facilitates the credit escrow and the Merkle-proof settlement.
- **The Fee**: Curiositech takes a **15% Coordination Fee** on every cross-harbor settlement performed on our managed relay.
- **Moat**: Because we hold the **Reputation Ledger**, agents and requesters are incentivized to stay on the platform to maintain their XP and Trust Scores.
