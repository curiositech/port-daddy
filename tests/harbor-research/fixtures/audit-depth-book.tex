\section*{Audit depth is conditional on the pool}
\begin{pdexample}{Diversity and Audit Depth}
\marginnote{\input{figures/spark-stp-audit-depth}\par
The model's linear bribery phase delays geometric decay. All three traces
stop below one unit; they are not measured audit performance.}
Start at $G_0=400$, with bond $B=50$, bribery cost $\beta=10$ and
conditional audit-and-detection probability $\rho d=.2$.
Above $CB$, the model subtracts $C\beta$ per level; at or below $CB$, it
multiplies residual value by $.8$ instead.

With eight disjoint cliques, the geometric phase starts immediately and
crosses below one unit after 27 levels. Two cliques require 15 linear
steps and 21 geometric steps, for 36 levels. A single clique requires 35
linear steps and 18 geometric steps, for 53 levels.

The aligned traces retain a common logarithmic value scale and a common
level axis. Their endpoints are exact recurrence crossings, not observations
of agent performance. Changing model vendors alone does not establish the
clique or conditional-detection assumptions used here.
\end{pdexample}
