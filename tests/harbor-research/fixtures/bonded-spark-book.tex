\section*{The cost of three punishment rounds}
\marginnote{\input{figures/spark-bonded-deviation}\par
Three-round model: net gain from deviation, in payoff units.
Above $\delta\approx.343$, deviation loses.}
The one-shot gain is 1. Each of three subsequent punishment rounds loses 2
relative to cooperation. At discount factor $\delta$, the net gain is
\[U(\delta)=1-2(\delta+\delta^2+\delta^3).\]
At $\delta=0$, future punishment has no present cost. At $\delta=1$, the
three losses total 6, leaving a net loss of 5. The zero at approximately
$0.343$ separates profitable from unprofitable deviation in this model.
