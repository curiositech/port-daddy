\input{figures/fig-correlating-device}
\noindent The figure exhibits the regime where $\mu$ has non-trivial correlation: on
a symmetric tie between $\alpha$ and $\beta$ over a single resource, the daemon's
recommendation is a randomized joint draw, not a deterministic function of the public
conflict graph. The marginals are uniform, but the joint is supported on the off-diagonal,

graph-augmented game~\cite{aumann1987correlated}. Outside the symmetric-tie regime,

recommended profile.
