\caption{The abdication gradient (\S\ref{sec:abdication}). As the automation takes
longer plans end-to-end, the operator's role slides through four stations --- doer,
approver, rubber-stamp, absent --- and situation awareness slides with it. The
Bainbridge--Scott convergence is that this is not operator weakness but a
\emph{property of good automation}: the digest erases the swarm's m\^etis and the
operator's alike. The design's one active defense is the Inception Canary
(Prot.~\ref{prot:canary}), which arrests rather than reverses the slide: a
consented, blinded replay of a known-outcome defect keeps the supervisor in practice
without synthetic busywork. \textbf{Both curves are schematic.} No
situation-awareness measurements exist for this system; the freeze-probe study of
\S\ref{sec:sa} is the experiment that would replace the shape with data, and
until it runs this figure is an argument drawn, not a result plotted.}
