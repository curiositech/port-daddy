\caption{The split ranker (Thm.~\ref{thm:split-ranker}). Discovery and the operator's
attention queue share candidate generation and recency decay
(Prop.~\ref{prop:decay}) over the same signals, but apply distinct scoring heads:
discovery maximizes \emph{fit} (Eq.~\ref{eq:fit}) while attention maximizes
\emph{regret-if-ignored} (Eq.~\ref{eq:regret}). The aligned example gives
$\operatorname{fit}(A)>\operatorname{fit}(B)$ and
$\operatorname{regret}(B)>\operatorname{regret}(A)$, so the two rankings reverse
the same pair. They are incompatible loss functions, not one objective with
swapped weights.}
