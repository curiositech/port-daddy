\caption{The split ranker (Thm.~\ref{thm:split-ranker}). The discovery ranker and the
operator's attention queue \emph{share} candidate generation and recency-decay
(Prop.~\ref{prop:decay}) over the same signals --- claims, skills, notes, episodes,
presence --- but apply distinct scoring heads: discovery maximizes \emph{fit}
(capability match, Eq.~\ref{eq:fit}); the attention queue maximizes
\emph{regret-if-ignored} (Eq.~\ref{eq:regret}). The operator wants the most dangerous
item surfaced, the agent wants the most capable collaborator --- incompatible loss
functions, not one objective with swapped weights, so the elegant ``one ranker''
claim is false-as-stated and demoted to ``shared substrate, distinct heads.''}
