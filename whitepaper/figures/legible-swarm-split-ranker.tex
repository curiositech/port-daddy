the discovery route asks which collaborator is most capable, while the attention
route asks which item is most dangerous to ignore.  The paths split because
capability fit (Eq.~\ref{eq:fit}) and regret-if-ignored (Eq.~\ref{eq:regret}) can
reverse the same pair of items.  They are distinct objectives, not one score with
weights renamed.}
