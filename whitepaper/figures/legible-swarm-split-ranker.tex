\caption{The rankers share candidates and recency decay but reverse the same
two-item ordering.  Discovery sends the capable, on-topic candidate $A$ ahead of
$B$ because $\mathrm{fit}(A)>\mathrm{fit}(B)$.  The operator attention lane sends
the high-stakes, irreversible anomaly $B$ ahead of $A$ because
$\mathrm{regret}(B)>\mathrm{regret}(A)$.  The reversal is the theorem's evidence:
the two heads are distinct objectives, not one score with renamed weights.}
