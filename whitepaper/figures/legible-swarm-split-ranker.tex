\caption{The split ranker (Thm.~\ref{thm:split-ranker}). Discovery's \texttt{whois}
and the operator's Attention Queue \emph{share} candidate generation and recency-decay
(Prop.~\ref{prop:decay}) but apply distinct scoring heads: \texttt{whois} maximizes
\emph{fit} (capability match, Eq.~\ref{eq:whois}); the Attention Queue maximizes
\emph{regret-if-ignored} (Eq.~\ref{eq:regret}). The operator wants the most dangerous
item surfaced, the agent wants the most capable collaborator --- incompatible loss
functions, so the elegant ``one ranker'' claim is false-as-stated and demoted to
``shared substrate, distinct heads.''}
