\caption{The Harbor Volume stack-map. Each rung serves a different \emph{whom}

(tube, pheromones, commitments, the cryptographic \texttt{auth-chain}, the
Arbiter). The L1 \emph{semantics} (protocol FSMs, GPGP-enriched commitments, the

what L0 \emph{assumes} from the machine; patina shows what the kernel
\emph{provides} upward. Papers 1, 3, and 4 all cite this one --- it is the
load-bearing substrate the rest of the volume stands on.}
