\caption{The four-layer coordination stack, drawn over the host floor it stands
on. Each rung names the audience it serves; the margin names the part and
chapters of this book that own it; every gap carries the dependency in both
directions, the lower rung \emph{provides} and the upper rung \emph{assumes}.
The filled rung is this chapter --- the transactional substrate, whose
one-writer record, claims, and sessions everything above rests on. Coordination
is outlined in the same accent but left unfilled, because this chapter specifies
only its implemented half and Chapters~2--3 carry the rest. The floor is dotted
and unbadged because the book builds none of it: a file lock, a clock, a socket,
and a supervisor are assumed of the host. Chapter~5 owns no rung --- the durable
identity it builds leans on this substrate and is priced on the market above.
