\caption{The four-layer stack. Each layer serves a different \emph{whom}; chapters
may own or cross-cut more than one rung. \textbf{This chapter owns L2 --- the legibility \&
authority ``wedge'' --- plus the L1 directory substrate} (the existence layer that a
message must address). Each layer \emph{assumes} the one below it and \emph{provides}
to the one above: this paper assumes the single-writer kernel (the companion protocol
chapter, \emph{The Single-Writer Kernel}) and provides the legible,
checkpointed, outcome-bearing identity that the reputation bridge (\emph{From Spawn
to Person}) and the market (\emph{The Harbor Economy}) consume. Chapters V--VII
cross-cut the ladder with protocol, incentive, and federation deep dives.}
