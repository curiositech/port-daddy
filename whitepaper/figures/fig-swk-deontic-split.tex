   {\color{hhcobalt}\textbf{Regimented}} (pre-commit, truly unreachable):\\

% ─── Obligation ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\node[col,fill=hhsand!30,right=5mm of prohib] (oblig)
  {{\hdr Obligation}\\ \textit{``you ought''}\\[4pt]
   \textbf{Enforced} by durable, \emph{violable} commitments
   (Cohen \& Levesque persistent goals):\\[4pt]
   \textbf{Law 1} --- the deadline is daemon-derived, \emph{never} agent-supplied (Goodhart-resistance, I5).\\[3pt]
   \textbf{Law 2} --- closing as `done' is refused without a reference from a finite
   oracle vocabulary (a released claim, a merged commit, a passing test, a satisfied
   policy sub-check). Free text cannot close (I6).\\[3pt]
   State machine: open $\to$ done $\mid$ abandoned $\mid$ superseded.};

% ─── Permission ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\node[col,fill=hhsand!30,right=5mm of oblig] (perm)
  {{\hdr Permission}\\ \textit{``you may''}\\[4pt]
   A recorded \textbf{capability} attached to an actor.\\[5pt]
   {\color{hhcobalt}\textbf{The split that must not collapse:}}\\[3pt]
   \textbf{capability} = \emph{ability} (what the sandbox makes possible).\\[3pt]
   \textbf{permission} = \emph{normative status} (what the deontic layer allows).\\[5pt]
   ``Able but forbidden'' must stay representable --- e.g.\ a dangerous override flag
   that \emph{exists} (capable) but \emph{must not be used} (forbidden).};

% ─── The unifying caption strip ───────────────────────────────────────────

  {\textbf{ The deontic split of the kernel (Jones \& Sergot 1993):}\quad
   \emph{prohibition} is regimented \emph{or} detected; \emph{obligation} is enforced
   by oracle-bound commitments; \emph{permission} is a recorded capability ---
   and the coordination layer's deontic binding is a direct lift of this split.};

\caption{The kernel's deontic split, the formal core of its
obligation/enforcement organ. Following Jones \& Sergot, the daemon realizes three
deontic modalities with three distinct mechanisms. \emph{Prohibition} is handled two
ways depending on the rule: a small regimented set is rejected pre-commit by a
uniqueness constraint or a fail-closed boot gate (truly unreachable); everything the
policy monitor \emph{subscribes} to is detected post-commit and compensated.
\emph{Obligation} is enforced by durable, violable commitments whose deadline the
agent cannot set (Law~1) and whose closure demands external evidence (Law~2).
\emph{Permission} is a recorded capability --- and, crucially, capability (ability)
is kept distinct from permission (normative status) so that ``able but forbidden''
remains expressible, the very state a guardrail-bypass discipline depends on.}
\label{fig:swk-deontic-split}
\end{figure}
