\caption{The kernel's deontic split, the formal core of its
obligation/enforcement organ. Following Jones \& Sergot, the daemon realizes
three deontic modalities with three distinct mechanisms. \emph{Prohibition} is
handled two ways depending on the rule (the C1 correction): a small regimented
set is rejected pre-commit by a \texttt{PRIMARY KEY} constraint or a fail-closed
boot gate (truly unreachable); everything the Arbiter \emph{subscribes} to is
detected post-commit and compensated. \emph{Obligation} is enforced by durable,
violable commitments whose deadline the agent cannot set (Law~1) and whose
closure demands external evidence (Law~2). \emph{Permission} is a recorded
capability --- and, crucially, capability (ability) is kept distinct from
permission (normative status) so that ``able but forbidden'' remains expressible,
the very state the project's own bypass-flag discipline depends on.}
