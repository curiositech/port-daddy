\caption{Context degradation is a causal chain: rot pressures compaction, naive
compaction loses the middle, recursive summaries fabricate, and the resulting digest
replaces its source.  A labelled intervention at any stage terminates the chain.
The load-bearing intervention is stage~3: \emph{compact from durable artifacts}, so
each new reading can be checked against an addressable claim, note, episode, or diff
rather than a prior summary.  Cost and truth fail together on this surface.}
\label{fig:cascade}
