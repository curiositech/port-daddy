\caption{The context-degradation cascade (\S\ref{sec:cascade}): rot makes you
compact, naive compaction loses the middle, recursion fabricates, and the
operator-facing digest over-flattens what's left --- the failures compound, hence
``cascade.'' Each rung (left column, joined by blue arrows) has a cure (right
column, joined by teal arrows), and breaking \emph{any} rung
stops the cascade; the load-bearing cure is \emph{compact-from-artifacts} (rung~3),
which a system with durable, addressable state (claims, notes, episodes) makes
cheap. Cost and legibility share this surface: when compaction fails, the bill
\emph{and} the truth degrade together.}
