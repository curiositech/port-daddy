\caption{Context degradation becomes a cascade only when each stage is left untreated.
Rot pressures compaction; naive compaction loses the
middle; recursive summaries fabricate; and the operator-facing digest finally
replaces its source. Any intervention stops the run, but the load-bearing one
is station~3: \emph{compact from durable artifacts}, so every new reading can
be checked against an addressable claim, note, episode, or diff rather than a
prior summary.  Cost and truth fail together on this surface.}
