\caption{The context-degradation cascade (\S\ref{sec:cascade}): rot makes you
compact, naive compaction loses the middle, recursion fabricates, and the
operator-facing digest over-flattens what remains. The amber ladder encodes the
causal order; the parallel teal rail places one countermeasure at each rung, so
breaking any rung stops the cascade. The load-bearing intervention is
\emph{compact-from-artifacts} (rung~3): durable, addressable claims, notes, and
episodes make it possible to reconstruct a digest from evidence rather than from a
previous summary. When compaction fails, the bill \emph{and} the truth degrade
together.}
