# Figure brief — II.2 Kernel contracts

- **Reader question:** What does each kernel contract own and enforce, and what
  is shared underneath all of them?
- **Claim:** Six non-equivalent contracts expose different durable records,
  mechanisms, controls, and maturities over one shared transactional substrate.
- **Objects:** six contracts, four comparison fields, three maturity states, and
  one non-peer substrate.
- **Reader action:** compare a row across fields, then recognize the common
  substrate as a dependency rather than a seventh equivalent object.
- **Must distinguish:** contract-specific state and enforcement; implemented
  versus partial maturity; the self-asserted identity caveat; shared substrate
  versus peer contract.
- **Chosen grammar:** contract matrix plus a separate substrate band.
- **Rejected grammar:** a 2x3 grid of equal cards makes the contracts look
  homogeneous and forces their differences into prose.
- **Five-second acceptance test:** a reader can identify an organ's record,
  mechanism, control, and maturity, then see that all six use one transaction
  floor.
