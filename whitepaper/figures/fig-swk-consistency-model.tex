\caption{The consistency-model theorem --- the formal property the single-writer
choice buys, made explicit here. Because all mutations serialize through one writer
over one WAL file, transactions are \textbf{serializable}; because that writer is
the sole decider, the observable history of claims and locks is
\textbf{linearizable}: every operation has a single linearization point at its
commit, and the commit order coincides with real time. The trace shows A winning a
port, B losing cleanly to the holder, A releasing, and C then winning --- one
unambiguous order with no agreement protocol in sight. This linearizability is
exactly what lets the coordination layer treat ``who holds this'' as ground truth.}
