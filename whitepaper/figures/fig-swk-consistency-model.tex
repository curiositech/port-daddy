\caption{The consistency-model theorem --- the formal property the single-writer
choice buys, made visible. Because all mutations serialize through one writer over
one WAL file, transactions are \textbf{serializable}; because that writer is the
sole decider, the observable history of claims and locks is \textbf{linearizable}:
every operation takes effect atomically at its commit (its \emph{linearization
point}, the cobalt dots), and the commit order coincides with real time. The trace
shows A winning a port, B losing cleanly to the holder, A releasing, and C then
winning --- one unambiguous order with no agreement protocol in sight. This
linearizability is exactly what lets the coordination layer treat ``who holds
this'' as ground truth.}
\label{fig:swk-consistency-model}
