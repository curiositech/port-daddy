\caption{The one law (Def.~\ref{def:zoom}) made concrete. \emph{Left}: a legible
digest line summarizes only administrative \textbf{state} and exposes a total zoom
$\zeta$ --- every claim reaches an operator-verifiable artifact (diff, test log,
commit) --- m\^etis preserved, the lie catchable. \emph{Right}: the Potemkin digest
paraphrases the work away; $\zeta$ is partial, the summary became a
\emph{replacement} rather than a \emph{lens}, and the tile asserts a reality with no
zoom path back to ground truth. This is Scott's \emph{Normalbaum}: gloriously
legible to the yield table, fatal because it erased what it could not see.}
