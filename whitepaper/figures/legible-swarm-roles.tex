\caption{Role assignment (\S\ref{sec:roles}) in Hobbes' author/actor distinction
(\emph{Leviathan} ch.~16). The operator is the author who owns authority, issues
scoped consent grants (Def.~\ref{def:consent}), and retains the inalienable
override (Prop.~\ref{prop:override}). The daemon is the sovereign-as-actor: it may
block, land, escalate, or gate only under the recorded grant, and its acts must be
legible through named receipts and artifact links. The agents are the multitude
whose covenant is each-with-all; the daemon enforces that covenant without becoming
one of its peer parties.}
