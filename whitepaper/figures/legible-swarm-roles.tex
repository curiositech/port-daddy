\caption{Role assignment (\S\ref{sec:roles}) in Hobbes' author/actor distinction
(\emph{Leviathan} ch.~16). The \emph{agents} are the multitude who covenant
each-with-all; the \emph{daemon} is the sovereign-as-actor that exercises the
instituted authority --- blocking, landing, escalating --- but, by the
legible-sovereign amendment (Prop.~\ref{prop:legible-sovereign}), is the most
legible actor, not the least --- the one relation drawn in the accent color,
because it is the inversion this chapter is arguing for; the \emph{operator} is the \emph{author} who owns the
authority, issues scoped consent grants (Def.~\ref{def:consent}), and holds the
inalienable override (Prop.~\ref{prop:override}). Naming the operator ``the
sovereign'' would break the covenant; the author slot is the one Hobbes leaves for
the one who authorizes without personally acting.}
