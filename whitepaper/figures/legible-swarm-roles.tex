\caption{Role assignment (\S\ref{sec:roles}) in Hobbes' author/actor distinction
(\emph{Leviathan} ch.~16). The \emph{agents} are the multitude who covenant
each-with-all; the \emph{daemon} is the sovereign-as-actor that exercises the
instituted authority --- but, by the legible-sovereign amendment
(Prop.~\ref{prop:legible-sovereign}), is the most legible actor, not the least; the
\emph{operator} is the \emph{author} who owns the authority, issues scoped consent
grants (Def.~\ref{def:consent}), and holds the inalienable override
(Prop.~\ref{prop:override}). Naming the operator ``the sovereign'' would break the
covenant; the author slot is the one Hobbes leaves for the one who authorizes without
personally acting.}
\label{fig:roles}
