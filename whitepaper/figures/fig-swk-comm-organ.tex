\caption{The communication organ. The \textbf{bus} envelope --- durable, ordered,
at-least-once, history-cursored (\textsf{implemented}) --- is the carrier
\emph{into which} the typed speech act is layered; the kernel ships the envelope,
the coordination layer ships the semantics that ride inside it. \textbf{Stigmergic
markers} implement coordination with per-kind exponential decay computed both on a
background tick and \emph{at read time}, so the anti-blackboard-rot property is
provided by the substrate rather than left to discipline. Finally, the figure fixes
a dangerous naming collision: the \emph{authorization chain} is the cryptographic,
mechanically verified chain of signatures that proves \emph{who authorized whom};
the \emph{coordination lineage} is the coordination object that prevents ping-pong
--- a different thing entirely, carried \emph{inside} the authorization chain.}
