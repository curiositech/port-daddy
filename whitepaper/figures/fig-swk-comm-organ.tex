\caption{The communication organ. The L0 \textbf{tube} envelope --- durable,
ordered, at-least-once, history-cursored (\textsf{BUILT}) --- is the carrier
\emph{into which} the L1 typed performative is layered; the kernel ships the
envelope, Paper~1 ships the semantics that ride inside it. \textbf{Pheromones}
implement stigmergic coordination with per-kind exponential decay computed both
on a background tick and \emph{at read time}, so the anti-blackboard-rot property
is provided by the substrate rather than left to discipline. Finally, the figure
fixes the volume's most dangerous naming collision (C4): \texttt{auth-chain} is
the cryptographic, ProVerif-verified Ed25519 chain that proves \emph{who
authorized whom}; \texttt{delegation-trace} is the coordination object that
prevents ping-pong --- a different thing entirely, carried \emph{inside} the
auth-chain.}
