% Brief (tikz-diagram-craft), register row ch4-22.
% Reader question: at a given attention budget, does any dismissal debit
%   exist that keeps the distress lane both uncrowded and low-miss, or is
%   the feasible band empty?
% Claim: the feasible debit interval [delta_min,delta_max] is bounded below
%   by the alarm-load wall (decreasing in A) and above by a fixed miss-loss
%   wall; the band is empty below A*~3.108 and opens above it, exactly as
%   at the chapter's own A=2.0 (empty) and A=3.3 ([0.035,0.072]) points.
% Evidence: delta_max=0.071975 (constant, R3 constants); delta_min(2.0)=
%   0.395644, delta_min(3.3)=0.034993 [verified closed forms,
%   b7_escalation_band.py, run this pass].
% Must distinguish: the empty-band regime (delta_min>delta_max) from the
%   feasible band; that delta_max does not move with A while delta_min does.
% Grammar chosen: threshold/regime plot, feasible band shaded between the
%   two walls (fillbetween naturally splits at their crossing), two worked
%   points marked.
% Grammar rejected: a bar chart of the two named A values' band widths would
%   state the two cases but hide that emptiness is a real, continuous
%   threshold in A, not a property of the two examples alone -- the
%   chapter's own point that the fix is capacity, not tuning.
% Five-second test: "at A=2.0, does the alarm-load wall sit above or below
%   the miss-loss wall, and what does that mean for delta?"

\caption{The escalation threshold feasible dismissal-debit band of
Theorem~\ref{thm:escalation-threshold}, on attention budget $A$ (R3
constants $C_{\mathrm{miss}}{=}100$, $c_{\mathrm{att}}{=}1$,
$C_{\mathrm{fa}}{=}5$). The miss-loss wall $\delta_{\max}$ is fixed at
$0.071975$; the alarm-load wall $\delta_{\min}(A)$ falls as the budget
widens and reaches $0$ at $A{=}3.5$. The two walls cross at
$A^\star{\approx}3.11$: below it $\delta_{\min}>\delta_{\max}$ and no debit
satisfies both walls at once (the point named in the chapter text at
$A{=}2.0$, where $\delta_{\min}{=}0.396$), and above it the tinted band is
the feasible interval (the point named at $A{=}3.3$,
$[0.035,0.072]$). An empty band is the honest diagnosis that the fix is
capacity or evidence quality, not a better $\delta$. [verified closed
forms, \texttt{b7\_escalation\_band.py}]}
