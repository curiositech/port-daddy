\caption{The continuity spine, ascending. The volume's through-line --- memory
$\to$ checkpoint $\to$ witnessed ledger $\to$ identity $\to$ reputation $\to$ price
--- bottoms out \emph{here}, in the kernel's three continuity organs (the lower
three treads), and the maturity mark on each tread is the honest part. \textbf{Memory}
(journal) is built: the kernel even forbids amnesia, a monitor rule keeping an active
session's note count from regressing. \textbf{Checkpoint} (snapshot) is built but the
weakest link --- drawn half-faded and amber-edged --- because recovery (heartbeat
$\to$ stale/dead $\to$ queue $\to$ salvage handoff) passes the durable notes yet does
\emph{not} capture execution state; a checkpoint with teeth (a real execution-state
snapshot, OP-4) is the literal foundation of any cross-machine reputation economy.
\textbf{The witnessed-outcome ledger} (sealed receipt), on which reputation actually
keys, is designed only --- it does not yet exist; the personhood paper builds it.
Identity, reputation, and price are the Floors above that lean on this slab. One
``the foundation is soft here'' claim, marked once on the tread that earns it --- not
three boxes in a row.}
