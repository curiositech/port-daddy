\caption{One durable record becomes five reader-specific instruments, not one
general-purpose summary.  The same raw trajectory yields an arrival briefing,
an operator attention queue, episodic recall, a decaying swarm trace, and a
successor handoff; each compacts for its reader while preserving a zoom path to
the underlying artifact.  Thus the coordination layer is a \emph{distributed
compaction engine}: its cost, recency-decay (Prop.~\ref{prop:decay}), and
legibility obligations are shared even though its readings differ.}
