\caption{The transition this paper argues for. \emph{Left}: an ungoverned swarm is
Hobbes' state of nature --- rational agents colliding because none can trust the
others (\S\ref{sec:taxonomy}). \emph{Right}: the operator institutes a rule of the
road and consents (a covenant \emph{among the subject agents}, enforced by the
daemon, not a bargain with it) to a \emph{local} Leviathan that renders the swarm
legible. The arrow is the covenant; the right-hand spokes are legibility, not
chains. Empirically, LLM societies traverse this arrow on their
own~\cite{dai2024}; Port Daddy's wager is to build the destination
\emph{deliberately and legibly}.}
