\caption{The transition from concurrent work to consented order. \emph{Left}: an ungoverned swarm is
Hobbes' state of nature --- overlapping writers may each act on the same work, so a
collision has no common order or inspectable return. \emph{Right}: the operator
institutes a rule of the road and consents (a covenant \emph{among the subject
agents}, enforced by the daemon, not a bargain with it) to a \emph{local} Leviathan.
The same work is admitted through one ordered commit spine, and each act returns an
actor, claim, artifact, and named reason. That is the legibility the earlier swarm
lacked: the covenant turns concurrent work into an auditable order.}
