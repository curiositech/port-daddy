\caption{The transition this paper argues for. \emph{Left}: an ungoverned swarm is
Hobbes' state of nature --- rational agents colliding because none can trust the
others (\S\ref{sec:taxonomy}): double-claims, illegible PRs, confident lies,
footguns. \emph{Right}: the operator institutes a rule of the road and consents (a
covenant \emph{among the subject agents}, enforced by the daemon, not a bargain with
it) to a \emph{local} Leviathan that renders the swarm legible --- claims, the
Arbiter, digest-with-zoom, consent grants, the override. The arrow is the covenant;
the right-hand spokes are legibility, not chains. Empirically, LLM societies
traverse this arrow on their own~\cite{dai2024}; Port Daddy's wager is to build the
destination \emph{deliberately and legibly}.}
