% Super-additive split penalty (Thm.~\ref{thm:split-penalty}): the joint

% than twice the single-reader floor, and the gap widens with the critical-set

% domain (2k<=m); the m=8 series reproduces the four values the theorem states
% in prose (1.029, 1.067, 1.122, 1.224) and the m=20 endpoint reproduces its
% stated 1.392 at k=10 -- no simulated or invented points, only the closed
% form the chapter already commits to. [internal; analytic]
