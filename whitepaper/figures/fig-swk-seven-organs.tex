constellation of equivalent ``organs.''  The matrix separates each contract's owned
record, operative mechanism, and inspectable control.  The substrate band is deliberately
not a peer: every row persists through its one SQLite write-ahead log and its atomic write
turn.  Identity is implemented only as a local, self-asserted record; the missing
