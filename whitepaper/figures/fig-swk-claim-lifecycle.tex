\caption{The guarded claim lifecycle. Acquire is one atomic uniqueness decision;
re-claim and release are idempotent; expiry is removed lazily on the next read.
The interrupted amber rail is intentional negative space: the kernel has no
queued state and therefore no fairness or bounded-wait guarantee. A reactive
ticket mechanism may later occupy that gap for cooperatively released claims, but
lazy expiry would still bypass it, so starvation remains an open problem.}
