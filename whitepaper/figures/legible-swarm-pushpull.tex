\caption{Push versus pull signals in the discovery ranker (Eq.~\ref{eq:fit},
Table~\ref{tab:fit-signals}). \emph{Push} signals are what a candidate declares
about itself; \emph{pull} signals are residue of work it actually did, recorded for
some other purpose. The design weights pull over push not because declarations are
usually false but because they are \emph{free} --- an agent that wants to be routed
work can inflate a skill tag at no cost and cannot inflate a merged episode without
merging something. This is the discovery-side instance of the same discipline
digest-with-zoom (Def.~\ref{def:zoom}) applies to summaries: trust the claim that
reaches an artifact.}
