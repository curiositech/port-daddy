\caption{The discovery ranker weights \emph{pull} signals --- residue of work a
candidate actually did, recorded for some other purpose --- over \emph{push}
signals, what a candidate merely declares about itself (Eq.~\ref{eq:fit},
Table~\ref{tab:fit-signals}). The paired rows hold the candidate capability claim
constant and expose the discriminating evidence: declarations are cheap to make,
but a merged episode, owned artifact, and current workload are expensive to forge.
The selection therefore follows evidence weight, not confidence in self-report.
This is the discovery-side instance of the same discipline digest-with-zoom
(Def.~\ref{def:zoom}) applies to summaries: trust the claim that reaches an artifact.}
