\caption{\textbf{Authority is an observable route from evidence to a named decision, not a generic hub.}
The six control functions map plan state, proposed changes, acceptance evidence, dependency constraints, high-risk operations, and exceptions to their respective records: roadmap consistency, independent review, completion verification, integration sequencing, safety response, and human escalation. Together they make the consented daemon's acts inspectable and reviewable; read-surfaces (Figure~\ref{fig:read-surfaces}) expose state, while these functions decide, block, integrate, and escalate. The engineering status of each function is recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:honest}.}
