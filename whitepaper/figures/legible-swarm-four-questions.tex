\caption{The four design questions (\S\ref{sec:four-questions}) as a decision flow

obligation (force-zoom, verbatim-link, artifact-target, named-reason) the surface
must satisfy before it may ship. The procedure is the operational form of the one
law (Def.~\ref{def:zoom}) and the legible-sovereign rule
(Prop.~\ref{prop:legible-sovereign}).}
