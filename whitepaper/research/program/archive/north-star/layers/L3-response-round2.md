## Point-by-point responses

### Critique A — Mechanism design / game theory

**A1. "PoA when reputation is for sale" is a category error — the action space changes, so it's a signal-existence question, not a ratio.**
**CONCEDE.** This is the sharpest finding and the graft confirms it (PoA is defined "for a fixed game"; once "buy/sell reputation" is in the action space the type/information structure changed). **Fix:** rewrite Open Problem #1. New statement: *"Does a market with tradeable/leasable reputation admit any informative (separating) equilibrium, or does decoupling the signal from the type it signals force pooling collapse? This is Spence-signaling-with-resale, not price-of-anarchy."* Cite Spence (1973) and the rating-shopping / issuer-pays literature (Bolton–Freixas–Shapiro 2012; Skreta–Veldkamp 2009). Delete the "bound PoA at 1:1" framing for the third side; keep PoA only for the *labor* side where the game is fixed.

**A2. "Reputation never ends" (no decay) is mechanism-design-hostile; no-decay is what *enables* the bubble.**
**CONCEDE.** The reviewer is right that Gap #4 and Open Problem #8 are symptoms of this single root error, and that the canonical result (Liu–Skrzypacz 2014; Mailath–Samuelson) is that *unbounded* records create reputation cycles/bubbles. **Fix:** retract the §1.C non-property "**never ends (no decay window)**." Replace with: *"Reputation is monotone in **witnessed, un-revoked** outcomes; the design parameter is not 'decay vs. no-decay' but the **records window**, which is tuned to defeat whitewashing (lower bound from Friedman–Resnick) without enabling reputation bubbles (upper bound from Liu–Skrzypacz)."* This single edit also dissolves the contradiction the crypto and category reviewers both hit (no-decay vs. required revocation).

**A3. The grading oracle's IC is *assumed, not proven* — and it's the keystone of every folk-theorem claim. Gap #3 is mis-classified as a side-gap; it's a hole in the core theorem.**
**CONCEDE — this is the most important concession.** The claim-signaling folk theorem (claim #17) conditions on a *public signal*; imperfect-monitoring folk theorems (Green–Porter 1984; Abreu–Pearce–Stacchetti 1990) require the signal distribution to be exogenous and common knowledge. If the grade is produced by a strategic, capturable evaluator, the folk theorem does not apply — it becomes mechanism design with a strategic mediator. **Fix:** promote "who pays the judges / judge-capture" from Gap #3 to a **named theorem-or-assumption in §0**. Two-part: (a) *Assumption O (Oracle Exogeneity):* the daemon-derived grade for **machine-checkable** acceptance criteria is strategy-proof by construction (the requester signs the predicates; the daemon evaluates them; neither party authors the grade) — this is the *bulk* of the work and it genuinely is exogenous. (b) For **subjective** axes (aesthetics) that need an LLM/human judge, declare it a *bonded sub-mechanism*: judges post their own bond, hold their own reputation, and are slashed when a later re-audit overturns them — and **prove the rate-the-raters recursion terminates** by grounding the base case in (a) (any subjective rating is periodically sampled against a machine-checkable proxy or a higher-bonded human panel; the recursion bottoms out at the exogenous machine grade, so it is a finite tower, not an infinite regress). I cannot yet *prove* convergence — I commit to stating it as a starred open problem (new Open #10, kept) but with the termination argument sketched, not hand-waved.

**A4. Cleanup Lower Bound (worst-case) and competitive underwriting (expected-loss pricing) are jointly inconsistent per-transaction; this is closer to a contradiction than an "open problem."**
**CONCEDE on the form of the answer; keep one genuinely-open sub-question.** The reviewer is right: a competitive premium prices to *expected* loss, which sits below worst-case for sub-worst-case agents, so per-transaction bond ≥ c and market-discovered premium cannot both hold. **Fix:** reframe the Cleanup Lower Bound at the *system* level, not per-transaction. New formulation: *"Per-transaction, the bond covers expected loss; the **worst-case (tail) coverage moves to the insurer's capital-adequacy constraint** — reserve ratio + reinsurance for the tail (a Solvency-II framing)."* The Cleanup Lower Bound becomes: *the insurer's reserves + reinsurance must dominate the federation-wide worst-case cleanup cost, not each bond.* Cite the catastrophe-insurance / reinsurance literature. **Genuinely open (keep as Open #6):** the exact reserve formula and whether competitive entry drives reserves *below* adequacy (a race-to-the-bottom underwriting cycle) — that is a real open question, but the *shape* is now determined, as the reviewer said.

**A5. Strict conservation (budget balance) + IC + efficiency violates Myerson–Satterthwaite; the paper never names which corner it gives up.**
**CONCEDE.** A three-sided market trading under two-sided private quality information is squarely in MS territory, and the conservation invariant *guarantees* budget balance, so something else must yield. The graft's "Budget Balance Blindness" failure mode is the same point. **Fix:** add an explicit MS clause to §0/§4: *"By Myerson–Satterthwaite (1983), efficiency + IC + IR + budget-balance are jointly unattainable under two-sided private information. Port Daddy fixes **budget balance** (the conservation invariant is non-negotiable) and **IC** (the escrow/slash mechanism), and therefore sacrifices **first-best efficiency**: the slashing-to-commons buffer is the budget-balancing wedge, and it makes the realized allocation strictly worse than first-best (the dead-weight is the commons accretion on partial/sabotage settlements)."* Position against **AGV / d'Aspremont–Gérard-Varet** as the baseline (the graft names it): AGV buys budget-balance + Bayesian-IC at the cost of dominant-strategy IC and IR — note that PD's slash mechanism is *closer to AGV than VCG* and say why interim-IR still holds (the bond is posted voluntarily ex-ante).

**A6. Wash-trading defense is circular: "cost-per-settled-outcome must exceed reputation's marginal value," but that marginal value is endogenous (the resale price from A1).**
**CONCEDE.** You cannot set a fee to dominate a quantity the fee helps determine. **Fix:** replace the circular fee argument with a **structural concavity break**: *"Reputation returns to settled outcomes between a **fixed counterparty pair** are sharply concave (diminishing) — the Nth settled trade between the same two principals contributes ε→0 reputation. This caps the wash-trade payoff **independent of price**, removing the fixed-point."* Categorically (anticipating critique C) this is *breaking the automorphism* that wash-trading exploits: collusion is a self-edge in the feedback graph, and concavity-on-self-edges kills it. Pair with sampled adversarial re-audit of high-velocity counterparty pairs. This also resolves the A1 endogeneity for the wash-trade case specifically (the concavity is a fee-free defense).

**A's "what an expert would add" (formal type/info structure; equilibrium concept per claim; collusion-proofness tooling; reserve framing; VCG/AGV baseline; one worked equilibrium):**
**CONCEDE all six as the §0 + appendix work order.** Concrete: (i) add **§0 Formal Model** — type space (each side's private quality), message space (the signed FloatPlan), allocation+transfer rule (escrow→settlement states), equilibrium concept (public-perfect equilibrium under imperfect-public monitoring for the labor side; note imperfect-*private* monitoring — Ely–Hörner–Olszewski 2005 — for the strategic-grader case where folk theorems are weaker).
