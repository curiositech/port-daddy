**Paper**: 5, *Continuity Without Metaphysics* (`whitepaper/research/tex/paper5.tex`)

**Dive run**: 2026-08-26. Source read in full plus its three verification
scripts and two figure sources. Direct dive (Skeleton B) — no separate planning
brief; see the parent `README.md`'s "The two dive patterns."

**Verdict**: **NARROW**.

No prior work proves any of the paper's four theorems — nothing is SUBSUMED or
CONTRADICTED by the literature — but every claim needs a narrower scope stated
up front, and the falsification pass alone found ten internal defects (D1–D10),
two of them contradicted by the paper's own arithmetic (D1: the plotted figure
data puts the γ crossing at ≈0.5437, not the 1/2 the prose and caption claim).
The headline literature finding: Theorem 2a is misnamed — it concludes pooling
and collapse (Akerlof's lemons result, which the paper already cites correctly
elsewhere), not full disclosure (Grossman–Milgrom "unraveling," the name the
paper actually gives it). Grossman 1981 §2/§3 turns out to *be* the paper's
Theorem 2b/2a pair, with disclosure cost as the crossover variable the paper
doesn't model. All eight of the paper's existing bibliographic records verify
exactly against Crossref — no fabrications, better than papers 2, 3, and 7 —
but two have usage problems (Spence is about sorting, not deterrence; the true
ancestor of the deterrence instrument is Becker & Stigler 1974, uncited).
