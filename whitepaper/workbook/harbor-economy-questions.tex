\section*{Part IV --- The Harbor Economy}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Part IV --- The Harbor Economy}

\emph{Floor 4 of the Harbor Volume: the trading floor. You don't sell crypto, you
sell hosted trust --- a three-sided agent labor market. These questions track the
paper section by section; worked solutions are in the Solutions part under the same
numbering.}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{IV.\arabic*},leftmargin=2.8em,itemsep=6pt,topsep=4pt]

% ── from §The thesis ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck Give a one-sentence reason the market cannot ship before reputation
  does, using only the word ``clone.''

\item \wbcheck Side~1 (operator-for-hire) does not require durable identity in the
  same way sides 2 and 3 do. Why? (Who is the counterparty, and who bears the
  consequence?)

\item \wbopen\wbstar Construct a market design in which sides 2 and 3 exist
  \emph{without} a non-forgeable identity. Argue informally why every such design
  collapses to a Sybil attack.

% ── from §What a side is ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck State the operational test for ``how many sides'' in one sentence,
  then apply it to a ride-share platform: how many sides, and why?

\item \wbtrace Side~3 (skill licensing) keys reputation on a \emph{content hash},
  not a person. What breaks when a licensor ships v2 of a skill?

\item \wbopen\wbstar A buyer reads a three-axis reputation vector
  $(\text{accuracy},\text{aesthetics},\text{efficiency})$. Specify a disclosure rule
  that lets the buyer weight the axes without re-introducing a single Goodhart
  target. Argue why every fixed scalar collapse re-creates the bandit framing the
  design rejects.

% ── from §The float plan ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbtrace Trace the flow of funds for a \textbf{Partial} settlement where the
  worker completed 40\% of the evidence chain. Confirm Conservation holds across the
  split, then redo it for a bond of $250$\,\textsc{cr} and $70\%$ completion.

\item \wbcheck Why does closure bind to an oracle rather than a self-reported note?
  Name the attack a free-text ``Result:'' note enables.

\item \wbopen\wbstar The four states assume the acceptance criteria were
  \emph{right}. Design a float-plan amendment protocol for the case where the
  criteria turn out wrong mid-flight (re-sign by both parties, escrow
  top-up/refund delta), preserving Conservation.

% ── from §Three sides on one escrow ───────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck Map each of Grossman--Hart's two parties (residual-control-right
  holder vs.\ reputational-consequence bearer) onto the rental side's roles. Which
  one is the runtime jail?

\item \wbtrace Show that adding a lease line item and a metered-license line item to
  a float plan cannot violate Conservation, no matter the amounts.

\item \wbopen\wbstar A skill's portfolio proof for v1 says nothing about v2. Specify
  a version-binding for the Merkle portfolio proof so a new version starts with a
  discounted inherited prior --- the newcomer policy, but for code.

% ── from §The keystone ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck In one sentence, state why a reputation system layered on forgeable
  pseudonyms is \emph{no} mechanism, not merely a weak one (cite Douceur).

\item \wbcheck Local non-forgeable identity has a non-goal: ``no defense against a
  hostile operator.'' Name the exact word in the market's definition
  (``mutually-distrusting,'' ``boundary neither controls'') that this non-goal
  contradicts.

\item \wbopen\wbstar Specify a cryptographic binding tying $N$ agents to one
  principal such that spawning fresh agents does not reset the bond floor, and that
  survives into the cross-operator world (where the single-operator trusted-credential
  shortcut is unavailable). This is the hard half of cross-operator attestation.

% ── from §Conservation as a functor ───────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck State Myerson--Satterthwaite in one sentence, then identify which of
  its four desiderata the harbor sacrifices and why.

\item \wbtrace The single-unit-federation theorem requires a single unit of account.
  Give a concrete two-harbor example where heterogeneous units make $F$ fail to
  preserve composition (the source of the lax functor's slack $\varphi$).

\item \wbopen\wbstar Write the coherence 2-cells of the lax functor explicitly and
  bound them by $\varphi$. Determine whether the pentagon/triangle coherence holds.

% ── from §Cross-harbor capability transfer ────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck Why is the transfer ceremony four messages and not two? Identify what
  message (3) buys (who must counter-sign, and under whose key does $C_B'$ later
  verify?).

\item \wbtrace Trace what happens to an in-flight $C_B'$ when Alice rotates her
  signing key between message (3) and a later presentation at $B$.

\item \wbopen\wbstar Specify replay protection across the boundary: is the
  \emph{anchor id} a nonce? Does $B$ reject a re-presented message (3)? State the
  freshness invariant.

% ── from §Federation / revocation gossip ──────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck The convergence bound is $\Delta(1+\ln m)$ expected. Name the two
  assumptions that make it false in the adversarial setting, and the partial-synchrony
  assumption that rescues a weaker version.

\item \wbtrace Trace a revoked capability through the revocation-gossip figure: at
  which harbor, and at what time, can it still be spent? That window is what the
  Equivocation Lower Bound must price.

\item \wbopen\wbstar State and (attempt to) prove the Equivocation Lower Bound for a
  three-harbor mesh under a fixed gossip period $\Delta$ and a single equivocating
  witness.

% ── from §The grading-oracle keystone ─────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck State the exact assumption the folk theorem needs about the public
  signal, and explain why a capturable grader violates it.

\item \wbcheck Classify three settlement criteria as machine-checkable (Fix A) or
  judge-dependent (Fix B): a unit test passing; a merged SHA; ``the refactor is
  tasteful.''

\item \wbopen\wbstar Prove the rate-the-raters recursion terminates: define a
  well-founded order on ``levels of judge,'' grounded at machine-checkable oracles,
  and show no infinite ascending chain of ``judges judging judges'' is needed.

% ── from §Cold start / the auction question ───────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck Which side should Phase~1 subsidize, and why? Apply the ``scarcest
  cross-side externality'' rule.

\item \wbtrace Use the cartel repeated-game figure to identify the grim-trigger
  condition under which the cartel is self-sustaining. What raises the critical
  discount factor $\delta$ enough to break it?

\item \wbopen\wbstar ``Price of anarchy when reputation is for sale'' is the wrong
  tool: once reputation is tradeable you have changed the game, so PoA (a ratio for a
  fixed game) does not apply. Reframe it as a signal-existence question
  (Spence-signaling with a resale market): does any informative separating
  equilibrium survive reputation resale?

% ── from §The sheaf-gluing problem ────────────────────────────────────────────
\item \wbcheck In one sentence each, restate the trustless-settlement conjecture, the
  Equivocation Lower Bound, and the lax-functor theorem as facets of the gluing
  obstruction.

\item \wbcheck Why is double-spend ``$H^1 \ne 0$'' and not ``$H^0 \ne 0$''? ($H^0$ is
  global sections; $H^1$ is the obstruction to building one.)

\item \wbopen\wbstar Either exhibit a defense that forces $H^1 = 0$ for a bounded
  gossip topology, or prove no such defense exists without consensus (which would
  refute the federation thesis's no-consensus stance).

\end{enumerate}
