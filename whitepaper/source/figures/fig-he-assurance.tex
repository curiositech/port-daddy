\caption{\textbf{Purchased assurance is one risk--cost trajectory.} Each teal
point adds one independently bonded reviewer: cumulative cost rises linearly while
residual defect risk falls geometrically. At $d=.6$, three reviewers leave $6.4\%$
residual risk and five leave about $1\%$. The dashed amber counterfactual charges
for five correlated reviewers but delivers roughly one reviewer's protection.
Independence and Becker deterrence $\rho dB\geq G$ are therefore conditions of the
frontier, not footnotes to headcount.}
