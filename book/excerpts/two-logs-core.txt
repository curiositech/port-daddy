                 -> each now holds evidence that acme equivocated

                 -> evidence that acme signed two roots for it,
                    3c657ada and 38936ba2. Both signatures verify
step 7   staging audits acme's epoch 8 at log 2
                 -> evidence that acme signed two roots for it,
                    38936ba2 and 3c657ada. Both signatures verify
