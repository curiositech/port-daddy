                -> each now holds evidence that alice equivocated

                -> evidence that alice signed two roots for it,
                   408dc067 and e88dca6c. Both signatures verify
step 7   carol  audits alice's epoch 8 at log 2
                -> evidence that alice signed two roots for it,
                   e88dca6c and 408dc067. Both signatures verify
