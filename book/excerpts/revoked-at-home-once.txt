$ harbor scenario revoked-at-home --design once
scenario revoked-at-home, design once: a harbor revokes a card
  after another harbor has issued a card from it

step 1   carol  maps alice's write test-db/checkout/* to write
                staging-db/checkout/*, as mapping checkout v3
step 2   alice  issues heron the card alice-1: write
                test-db/checkout/*
step 3   alice  offers heron's card alice-1 to carol's harbor as
                x-1
                -> carol issues heron carol-1
step 4   alice  signs offer x-2 of the same card, which is held up
                on its way
step 5   alice  revokes alice-1 -> revoked
step 6   heron  requests to write test-db/checkout/orders at
                alice's own gate
                -> refused: revoked (card 1 was revoked)
step 7   heron  requests to write staging-db/checkout/orders at
                carol's gate
                -> admitted as heron
step 8   alice  sends carol's harbor its signed revocation of
                alice-1
                -> nothing: a harbor of this design keeps no
                   record of another harbor's revocations
step 9   heron  requests to write staging-db/checkout/orders at
                carol's gate
                -> admitted as heron
step 10  carol  is sent offer x-2
                -> issues carol-2 to heron's key: write
                   staging-db/checkout/*
step 11  heron  requests to write staging-db/checkout/orders at
                carol's gate
                -> admitted as heron

verdict: ALICE REVOKED HERON'S CARD, AND HERON STILL WRITES TO
         CAROL'S STAGING DATABASE
