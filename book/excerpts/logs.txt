$ harbor logs
every sequence of events for three logs and five signed roots. The
  roots: one that acme signs for epoch 7, two different ones it
  signs for epoch 8, one that beta signs for its own epoch 8, and
  one that wren signs and that names acme. Any root may be
  published to any log, any number of times and in any order. Logs
  of the design core may also exchange the roots they hold, any
  two at a time

dropped: states in which a log no longer holds a root it should:
  the first to reach it for a harbor and an epoch, or a second
  one, which it should hold as evidence
forged: states in which a log holds a root that the harbor it
  names did not sign
accused: states in which a log holds evidence against a harbor for
  an epoch, and two roots that harbor signed for it have not
  reached the log
split: states in which two logs hold different roots for one
  harbor and epoch
hidden: of those, the states in which the two logs would not both
  hold evidence after an exchange. Logs that exchange nothing are
  counted as they stand

in every state, each log that asks the core holds exactly the
  roots and the evidence that the reference model says it should
