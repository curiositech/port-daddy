scenario wrong-card, design core: the revocation of one card is
  sent again with another card

t=0      harbor issues alice a card: read shop/*, write shop/*;
                valid until t=600000
t=0      alice  delegates to heron: write shop/checkout.ts; valid
                until t=600000
t=0      heron  delegates to finch: write shop/checkout.ts; valid
                until t=600000
t=0      heron  delegates to wren: write shop/checkout.ts; valid
                until t=600000
t=60000  heron  signs a revocation of wren's card, and sends it
                with the cards
                -> revoked
t=60000  wren   requests to write shop/checkout.ts
                -> refused: revoked (card 3 was revoked)
t=120000 wren   has a copy of what heron sent the gate, and of the
                cards he and finch present
t=120000 wren   sends the gate what heron sent it, with finch's
                cards in place of her own
                -> refused: bad-revocation (the revocation is not
                   a signature for this card at this harbor)
t=120000 wren   sends it once more, with heron's cards
                -> refused: bad-revocation (the revocation is not
                   a signature for this card at this harbor)
t=120000 finch  requests to write shop/checkout.ts
                -> admitted as finch
t=120000 heron  requests to write shop/checkout.ts
                -> admitted as heron

verdict: no other card was revoked: what heron signed revoked
         wren's card, and no other
