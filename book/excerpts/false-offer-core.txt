scenario false-offer, design core: an outsider with a copy of
  every message tries to get a card of her own

step 1   carol  maps alice's write test-db/checkout/* to write
                staging-db/checkout/*, as mapping checkout v3
step 2   bob    maps alice's write test-db/checkout/* to write
                shop-db/checkout/*, as mapping checkout v3 too
step 3   alice  issues heron the card alice-1: write
                test-db/checkout/*
step 4   alice  offers heron's card alice-1 to carol's harbor as
                x-1
                -> carol issues heron carol-1
step 5   wren   sends bob's harbor a copy of offer x-1
                -> refused: other-recipient (the offer names
                   another harbor)
step 6   wren   sends carol's harbor an offer in alice's name for
                her own key, signed with her own key
                -> refused: offer-not-signed (the offer is not
                   signed with the key of the harbor it is from)
step 7   wren   sends carol's harbor offer x-1 with the holder's
                key changed to her own
                -> refused: offer-not-signed (the offer is not
                   signed with the key of the harbor it is from)
step 8   wren   sends carol's harbor offer x-1 again
                -> known: nothing new is issued for x-1
step 9   wren   presents carol-1 at carol's gate, and signs the
                challenge with her own key
                -> refused: not-proved (the proof is not signed
                   with the last card's key for this presentation)
step 10  wren   sends carol's harbor a revocation of alice-1,
                signed with her own key
                -> refused: not-its-harbor (the revocation is not
                   signed with the key of the harbor that issued
                   the card)
step 11  wren   sends alice's harbor an acknowledgment of x-1 that
                she signed
                -> refused: ack-not-signed (the acknowledgment is
                   not signed with the key of the harbor it is
                   from)
step 12  heron  requests to write staging-db/checkout/orders at
                carol's gate
                -> admitted as heron

verdict: nothing wren sent got her a card, and heron still has its
         own
