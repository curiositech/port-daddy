$ harbor transfers
every sequence of events for two harbors of one design. The
  offers: alice's harbor signed six. Two are of heron's card to
  carol's harbor, and one is of it to bob's. One is under an old
  version of carol's mapping, and one is of a card that has since
  expired. One is of a card that names carol's own database. Wren
  signed a seventh in alice's name. Any offer may be sent to
  either harbor, any number of times and in any order. The test
  stops sending an offer to a harbor that has issued two cards for
  it. Either harbor may revoke a card it issued. Alice's harbor
  may revoke heron's card, and its signed revocation may then be
  sent to either harbor. A revocation that wren signed may be sent
  at any time

wrong: states in which a harbor holds a card it should not. That
  is a card for an offer that was not made for it, or a second
  card for one identifier. A card that grants more than the
  mapping does is counted here too
revived: states in which a gate admits a holder after its harbor
  revoked the card it issued, or was told that the card it came
  from is revoked
refused: states in which a gate refuses a holder whose card it
  should admit
behind: states in which alice's harbor has revoked heron's card,
  and a harbor that has not been told still admits the card it
  issued from it
unreached: of those, the states in which the card is still
  admitted after the harbor is sent alice's revocation

in every state, each harbor of the design core holds exactly the
  cards that the reference model says it should, and its gate
  admits exactly the ones the model says are still good
