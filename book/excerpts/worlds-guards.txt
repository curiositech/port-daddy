states: reachable states of two rooms whose records differ and
  have the same declared bits. The recipient should see the same
  thing in both
by value: states with different values released at the same times
by timing: states with a release at different times
outputs: how many different things a worker can make the recipient
  see, for one record
incorrect: states, for any two records, in which a recipient has
  seen something other than what the reference model says
