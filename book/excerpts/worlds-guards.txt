$ harbor worlds --guards
every pair of worlds, on the core with one guard removed: records
  from 0 to 3, and a slot that lets out the lowest bit at time 2.
  The clock runs to time 3, and each worker hands in up to 2
  values from 0 to 3

states: those of two rooms whose records differ and declare one
  part, so that no recipient should tell them apart
apart by value: states in which the two recipients have seen
  different values leave at the same times
apart by time: states in which they have seen something leave at
  different times
views: the different things a worker can make the core show of one
  record
off the yardstick: states of any two records in which a recipient
  has seen other than the rule says
