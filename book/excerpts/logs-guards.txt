$ harbor logs --guards
every sequence of events for two logs and five signed roots, on
  the core with one guard removed. The roots: one that acme signs
  for epoch 7, two different ones it signs for epoch 8, one that
  beta signs for its own epoch 8, and one that wren signs and that
  names acme. Any root may be published to any log, any number of
  times and in any order, and the two logs may exchange the roots
  they hold

dropped: states in which a log no longer holds a root it should:
  the first to reach it for a harbor and an epoch, or a second
  one, which it should hold as evidence
forged: states in which a log holds a root that the harbor it
  names did not sign
accused: states in which a log holds evidence against a harbor for
  an epoch, and two roots that harbor signed for it have not
  reached the log
hidden: states in which the two logs hold different roots for one
  harbor and epoch, and would not both hold evidence after an
  exchange
pairs: of the 25 pairs of those roots, how many the core accepts
  as evidence. Two pairs are evidence
