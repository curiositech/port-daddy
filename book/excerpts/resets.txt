$ harbor resets
a worker gets a sanction, and its principal either keeps the
  account or resets by registering a new worker. Scores follow the
  Book's example: a newcomer starts at 50, and a sanction costs
  30. A pass adds 10, which is this build's own choice

design    passes  before  keep  reset  best  regained
named          0      50    20     50    50        30
named          2      70    40     50    50        10
named          4      90    60     50    60         0
minted         0      50    20     50    50        30
minted         2      70    40     50    50        10
minted         4      90    60     50    60         0
core           0      50    20     20    20         0
core           2      70    40     20    40         0
core           4      90    60     20    60         0

before: the score before the sanction
keep: the score after it, if the principal keeps the account
reset: the score of the new worker, if the principal resets
best: the higher of the two
regained: how many of the sanction's 30 points the reset won back
