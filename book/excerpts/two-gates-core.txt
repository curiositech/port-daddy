  presented at another

t=0      harbor issues alice a card: read shop/*, write shop/*;
                valid until t=600000

                until t=600000

                with it

                -> revoked

                gate, presenting heron's cards
                -> refused: revoked (card 2 was revoked)
t=180000 wren   requests to write shop/checkout.ts at the far
                gate, presenting heron's cards
                -> admitted as heron
t=240000 gates  exchange notices
                -> the far gate adds 1 card to its revocation list
t=240000 wren   requests to write shop/checkout.ts at the near
                gate, presenting heron's cards
                -> refused: revoked (card 2 was revoked)
t=240000 wren   requests to write shop/checkout.ts at the far
                gate, presenting heron's cards
                -> refused: revoked (card 2 was revoked)

verdict: refused at both gates: once the gates had exchanged
         notices, heron's card was refused at each. Before that
         the far gate admitted it once
