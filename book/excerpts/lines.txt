every sequence of events for one piece of work: the worker changes
  it up to 2 times, correctly or not, and reports on it up to 2
  times. The kernel runs the acceptance test at any point, and the
  store may return the wrong text once. The operator opens up to 3
  claims, and may try to land the work in any state

lines: states in which the operator is shown a line
overclaim: lines that say the work is good and have no claim that
  links to a passing run of it
landings: states in which the work can be landed
unverified: landings of work that no run of its revision had
  passed
unseen: landings of work whose diff the operator had not been
  shown
