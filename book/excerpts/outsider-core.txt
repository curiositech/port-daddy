scenario outsider, design core: someone who holds no card of a
  chain asks the gate to revoke its last card

t=0      harbor issues alice a card: read shop/*, write shop/*;
                valid until t=600000
t=0      alice  delegates to heron: write shop/checkout.ts; valid
                until t=600000
t=0      heron  delegates to finch: write shop/checkout.ts; valid
                until t=600000
t=0      finch  requests to write shop/checkout.ts
                -> admitted as finch
t=60000  wren   copies finch's cards as he sends them, and holds
                none of the keys they name
t=60000  wren   sends the gate finch's cards, and asks it to
                revoke his card
                -> refused: no-revocation (no signed revocation
                   came with the cards)
t=60000  wren   signs a revocation with her own key, and sends it
                with his cards
                -> refused: not-in-chain (the revocation's key is
                   not the harbor's, and no card of the chain
                   names it)
t=60000  wren   signs another, and names heron's key as the key
                that signed it
                -> refused: bad-revocation (the revocation is not
                   a signature for this card at this harbor)
t=60000  finch  requests to write shop/checkout.ts
                -> admitted as finch
t=120000 heron  signs a revocation of finch's card, and sends it
                with the cards
                -> revoked
t=120000 finch  requests to write shop/checkout.ts
                -> refused: revoked (card 3 was revoked)

verdict: no outsider's request was accepted: finch's card stayed
         valid until heron, who signed it, had it revoked
