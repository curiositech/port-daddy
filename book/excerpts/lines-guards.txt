$ harbor lines --guards
every afternoon of one piece of work, on the core with one guard
  removed: the worker changes it up to 2 times, well or badly, and
  says up to 2 things of it. The kernel runs the kept test when it
  gets to it, and the store may go wrong once. The operator opens
  up to 3 claims, and may land the work in any state

lines: states in which the operator is shown a line
say more than show: lines that say the work is good, with no claim
  that leads to a passing run of it
landings: states in which the work can be landed
not verified: landings of work that no run of its revision had
  passed
not read: landings of work whose diff the operator had not been
  shown
lead nowhere: lines with a claim that leads to nothing in the
  store
