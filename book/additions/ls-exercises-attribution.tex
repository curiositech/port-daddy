\begin{pdexercise}[kind=Check,rating=1]{ex:ls-fit-uplift-pair}
A router scores two skills for one task: $\mathrm{fit}(A)=0.9$ and
$\mathrm{fit}(B)=0.6$. Randomized availability estimates their uplift as
$0$ points for $A$ and $15$ points for $B$. Show that the two orders are not
comonotone, and say which head should set royalties.
\end{pdexercise}
\begin{pdsolution}{ex:ls-fit-uplift-pair}
The comonotonicity product is
$(0.9-0.6)(0-15)=0.3\times(-15)=-4.5<0$, a strict reversal, so by
Theorem~\ref{thm:comonotone} no single head serves both readers at a
one-skill budget. The router may still load $A$ first: fit predicts which
skill matches the task. Royalties and deranking should read uplift, because
a skill that matches the task but does not change its outcome has earned
nothing for the buyer (Theorem~\ref{thm:ls-fit-uplift}).
\end{pdsolution}
