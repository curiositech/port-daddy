- **A chapter's Reader's Map can no longer leak into the Book, and the two chapters that were leaking one are repaired.** The Book's generator strips a reader's map only where the heading is starred, on the reasoning that a numbered section is chapter content, so a chapter that wrote `\section{Reader's Map}` instead printed its per-audience routing table inside a book whose front matter already routes the reader — twice, found by hand both times. The Single-Writer Kernel and the Harbor Economy are now `\section*{...}` with an `\addcontentsline`, which keeps each map in its standalone paper and out of the Book, and a new test runs the generator over every chapter named in `whitepaper/textbook.json` and asserts against the generated body that none survived. The chapter list comes from the textbook rather than from the test, so a ninth chapter is covered the day it is added.
- **The Book generator no longer splices a chapter body at a commented-out `\begin{document}`.** `documentBody` took the first `\begin{document}` and the last `\end{document}` by text position alone, so a disabled draft opening or an `\end{document}` parked above an appendix while it was being cut would move the extent silently, swallowing a preamble or truncating a chapter into a body that still looked plausible. It now locates both markers outside TeX comments, reading `\%` as the literal percent sign it is rather than the start of one.
