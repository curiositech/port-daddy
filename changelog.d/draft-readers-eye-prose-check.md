- **The Book now has a check for prose a reader cannot follow, not just prose that is structurally complete.** Every existing linter over the eight chapters measures structure — `chapter_lint.py` asks whether a section has a worked example and whether a claim carries its epistemic kind, `margin_lint.py` asks whether a gloss sits at the term's first use, `check_style.py` asks whether the seven house moves appear — and none of them asks whether a competent reader could restate a paragraph after one read. `skills/textbook-craft/scripts/readers_eye.py` is the mechanical half of that question: six rules, stdlib only, chapter list read from `whitepaper/textbook.json`, every word list in `skills/textbook-craft/references/readers-eye-lexicon.json` rather than inline. The sharpest is `caption-carries-the-fact` — a float's caption states something concrete and checkable while the paragraph introducing it states nothing at all, which is the author knowing the fact and putting it where the reader arrives only after the prose has lost them. The specimen it was built against, §1.3 of `whitepaper/single-writer-kernel.tex`, trips five of the six rules and seven findings; the corpus baseline is 149 findings across all eight chapters, 92 of them the single systemic "this paper" register issue, and that baseline with its honest false-positive read is recorded in `skills/textbook-craft/references/readers-eye.md` §6.
  - The judge pass is a rubric, not a keyword list: `references/readers-eye.md` §5 puts a model in the chair of a systems engineer who knows what a kernel and a daemon are and none of this project's vocabulary, and asks only whether that reader could restate the paragraph in their own words — a finding names the sentence that lost them and carries a rewrite, because a finding without a fix is a complaint.
  - Advisory in `library-checks.yml`, exactly as `chapter_lint.py --strict` is, until the corpus clears rule by rule. The one required step is `--selftest`, which asserts the check still separates a clean fixture from a deliberately bad one; `tests/harbor-research/test_readers_eye.py` pins the specimen's findings so a future tuning pass cannot quietly stop flagging it.
