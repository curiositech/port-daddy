- **Chapter 5 now builds the registry its theorem is about.** The chapter proves that a penalty tied to one identity is lost wherever a fresh identity is free, and it tells the story of a worker that resets. A new build section runs both. The first registry files a record under the name a process gives. A second operator's process uses a worker's name and gets that worker's passes, and a sanctioned worker registered under a new name shows no sanction. The second registry issues account numbers and credentials, and the sanction is still lost, because a new account is free. The fix, in the Book's kernel, records a pass on the worker's account and a sanction against its principal. The chapter's table of what a reset wins back is then reproduced from runs. An exhaustive test of every short sequence of events finds a sanction missing in 1,042 of 3,405 states under account numbers, and in none of 3,717 under the kernel's registry. Kani checks each decision on any registry of a fixed size. One new figure and four exercises.
