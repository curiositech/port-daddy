- **Review-evidence media has a content-addressed home in Cloudflare R2, and a manifest that cannot drift because it is derived from the tree.** Of 900.1 MiB tracked, 765.9 MiB is media and a further 139.9 MiB sits behind git-lfs pointers; `scripts/r2-media-manifest.mjs` applies one mechanical rule — tracked, media extension, under one of eight review-evidence roots that no build step reads — and projects 818 assets / 782 distinct objects / 411.4 MiB into `media/r2-manifest.json`. Keys are `sha256/<ab>/<64hex><ext>`, so identical bytes always land on the same object: a redeploy issues HEADs and zero PUTs, a PR preview needs no bucket or promotion step of its own, and `If-None-Match: *` makes two racing CI runs safe. `scripts/sync-r2-media.mjs` is Node-stdlib-only, fails closed on every path that is not full success, and holds its secret on a non-enumerable property with a redacting `toJSON` so no log line or error can print it. `scripts/check-r2-media-manifest.mjs` regenerates the manifest in-process and compares bytes, catching an added, a deleted, or an edited asset — the third being the case a list-against-list check structurally cannot see. Design, bucket layout, cache headers, the custom-domain-over-`r2.dev` decision and the rollback story are in `docs/adr/0142-r2-media-offload.md`; no Cloudflare resource is created by this change.
