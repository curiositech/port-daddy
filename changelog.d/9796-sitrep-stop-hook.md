- **The SITREP contract is now verified at end of turn, not just compelled at the start.** A fourth Giant Squid tentacle, `bin/pd-hook-stop` (phase `PD CLOSE`, ADR-0092 L4 closeout gate), registers on each provider's end-of-turn event — Claude Code `Stop`, Gemini CLI `AfterAgent`, Codex `[[hooks.Stop]]`, Antigravity `Stop` — and checks the final assistant message for the SITREP table under the same per-repo `sitrep.endOfTurn` dial the prompt tentacle reads. Under `enforce` it blocks exactly once (exit 2 with the SITREP directive as the reason — the reason is the model's next prompt), loop-guarded twice over: the vendor's `stop_hook_active` signal plus a harness-owned one-shot per-session marker with a TTL, because Gemini and Codex document no consecutive-block cap of their own. Antigravity is observe-only (its camelCase Stop payload carries no final-message field and no loop guard), unverifiable final text never blocks, and everything fails open. Ships with the `skills/giant-squid-hooks` family skill documenting all four tentacles' per-vendor contracts.
