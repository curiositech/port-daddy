- **The Book's citations, provenance and proof pointers move into the margin.** Every eligible `\cite` in the eight chapters becomes a `\pdcite`, which sets a short form — surname, ampersand or "et al.", year — in the margin beside the sentence that leans on the work, while the back-matter bibliography stays exactly where it was; the margin is a convenience, not a replacement. The inline `[verified]` and `[internal]` tags that used to interrupt a worked number are gone from the running text and are set instead as a margin note naming the script, its seed and what it establishes. Three theorem promises now carry a "Proved on p. N" pointer to the chapter that discharges them. Citations inside a bibliography, a caption, a footnote, a heading or another margin macro's own argument are left alone, because margin material cannot nest.
- **The generated tables that back those macros are checked, not trusted.** `build_cite_shortforms.py`, `build_discharge_pointers.py`, `promote_cites.py` and `promote_provenance.py` each ship a `--check` mode, and the library-checks workflow runs all four: rename a `\label`, add a `\bibitem`, or edit a chapter's citations, and the build stops instead of quietly setting a blank margin note or a bare key. `margin_lint.py` gains an enforced rule closing the other direction — a `\pdprovedon` whose label has no entry in the generated table fails the run rather than rendering an empty pointer.
- **The parsing behind all of it has tests.** Author lists, name particles, year-versus-arXiv-id disambiguation, brace balance, protected regions, the backward paragraph search that matches a provenance tag to its number, the tie-break between several candidate scripts, and the discharge table's four refusal paths. Writing them found two real defects: a two-particle surname lost its head ("van der Waals" came out as "der Waals"), and an unterminated bibliography silently protected everything after it instead of raising.
