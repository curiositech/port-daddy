- **The Distress Register drill (ADR-0132 §5, phase 6): a halt procedure is now something you run, not something you hope.** `scripts/pd-distress-drill.sh` stands in a dummy daemon, supervisor, agent hook and guard as plain POSIX-sh subprocesses in a scratch `PD_HOME` and walks the whole sequence without the real daemon, `pd`, launchctl or the network: hoist `SECURITE HALT`, assert every entity answers `SEEN` then `COMPLIED` within two listening intervals and stops spending, hard-kill the supervised child and assert no relaunch, assert the guard exits 0 with its one calm OFF line, then prove an unsigned all-clear, a garbage-signed all-clear and a deleted sentinel all change nothing while a signed operator `ALL-CLEAR` lifts the halt and everything resumes. Steps that need the live daemon are reported as `SKIP: requires live daemon — post-halt`; real components present on the checkout (`bin/pd-distress`, the `pd-hook-pre-tool` halt gate) are exercised and any that depend on a higher tier than they claim are reported as `GAP`. `tests/unit/pd-distress-drill.test.ts` runs it under `sh` and `dash` and proves the drill fails on a rogue lift.
