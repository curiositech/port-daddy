type: changed

- **A chapter page links to the Book instead of embedding a copy of it.** `website-v2/src/pages/whitepaper/PaperDetailPage.tsx` carried an 82vh `<iframe>` of the whole collected PDF under a heading about one chapter, so every visitor to `/whitepaper/<chapter>` paid a book-sized download to render something they had not asked to read, and the page's `Format` row advertised "PDF, embedded below". The frame is gone; in its place a "Read the paper" block links to `/whitepaper`, which already carries the reader, and to `COLLECTED_VOLUME.pdfPath` labelled with the Book's real page count. The argument map and takeaways move out of the sidebar the frame displaced them into and onto a two-column grid, and the review-history and sibling-chapter links become a row of their own.
- **`llms.txt` and `sitemap.xml` derive their chapter list from the site's own data, which fixes a missing chapter and a 404.** `website-v2/scripts/generate-seo-artifacts.mjs` held a hand-typed list of seven chapter paths that shadowed `WHITE_PAPERS`; it never grew an eighth, so The Sealed Harbor had no entry point in `llms.txt` for its whole published life, and the chapters were in neither the Book's order nor any other. The list is now `WHITE_PAPERS.map((paper) => paper.readerHref)`. Regenerating also corrected `docs/cli/embed-text` to `docs/cli/embed-subcommand` in both artifacts — the sitemap had been advertising a route that does not exist.
