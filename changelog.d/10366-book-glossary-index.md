- **The Book gains a glossary, an index of defined terms, a define-before-use lint and status tags in the margin.** Every term a chapter defines with `\pdterm` is set bold where it is defined, entered in an index printed as the Book's last leaf (makeindex, run by latexmk or between the plain-engine fallback's passes, in every edition and in the extracted source archive), and listed in a glossary appendix after the lettered appendices: the term, its definition from the chapter's `book/glossary/<slug>.json`, and the page of its definition. `scripts/generate-book-glossary.mjs` renders the appendix on every build and `--check` validates the glossary files in library-checks. `scripts/lint/check-book-terms.mjs` walks the sources in reading order and reports, at file:line, a term used before its definition, a term defined twice, a `\pdterm` with no glossary entry and an entry with no `\pdterm`; library-checks runs it with `--strict`, so a new early use fails the build. A glossary entry may list `other_senses`, phrases that use its words in an everyday meaning ("ground truth" beside the logic term "ground"), which the lint does not count. `\pdstatus` now prints the grade word and its note as a compact tag in the margin column beside the heading or claim it qualifies instead of a line in the argument.
