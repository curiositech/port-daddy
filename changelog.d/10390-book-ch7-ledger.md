It is walked through all 6,902 states within the bounds of the chapter's TLA+ model, and Kani takes one step of the theorem's induction for any amounts. With the guard on a bond's standing removed, every unit is still there and the wrong party holds some of them, which is why settling a bond once is checked separately.
