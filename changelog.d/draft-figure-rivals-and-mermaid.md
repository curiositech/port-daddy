- **Every figure in the Book now has its rival variants rendered side by side, and a naive mermaid sketch of what it is trying to say.** Seven branches had been redrawing the same figures in parallel with no way to see them together, so the choice between them was being made from commit messages. `docs/harbor-research/exposition/figures/FIGURE-RIVALS.json` indexes each figure against every branch that draws it, and 123 rendered variants are committed under `docs/pr-assets/figure-rivals/` as Git LFS pointers (15,990 bytes in git for 123 images) so the comparison survives the branches that produced it. `NAIVE-MERMAID.json` records, for each figure, the plainest mermaid diagram that carries the same claim — the baseline a TikZ redraw has to beat to justify its cost. Six variants that won their comparison ship as drawable fragments (`fig-bc-graduated-trigger`, `fig-bc-oracle-audit-rate`, `fig-bc-delta-threshold`, `fig-he-succession-price`, `fig-anchor-handshake-ladder`, `tab-bc-settlement-rule`), with their plotted data generated by `whitepaper/figures/data/make-figure-data.py` into committed CSVs rather than typed into the picture.
