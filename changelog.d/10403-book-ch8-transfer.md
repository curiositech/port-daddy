- **Chapter 8 builds the transfer of a card to another harbor.** Section 8.4.1 gives the ceremony as four messages, and nothing in the chapter ran them. A new section, 8.4.4, builds the ceremony in the kernel for the chapter's own case: Heron holds a card from Alice's harbor, and has a migration to run on Carol's staging database. A gate that honors Alice's cards as they are admits whatever Alice's harbor signs. In the build, Alice's harbor signs an offer that describes the card, and Carol's harbor issues a card of its own for what its own mapping grants. It honors an offer once, and it revokes its card when Alice's harbor says, with its signature, that the old card is revoked. An exhaustive test of two harbors reaches 195 states and finds no card that should not be there. ProVerif proves that each card issued answers one offer, made for that harbor. The section also prints what it leaves out: the offer carries no epoch root, and a harbor that has not been told of a revocation still admits its card.
- **Chapter 8's witness log now runs as processes.** Every run in section 8.2.4 kept its logs and its harbor inside one process. The section now starts two logs and a harbor as processes of their own, each on its own socket, and prints the session. The harbor publishes one root for an epoch to each log, and two audits read different answers. The logs exchange what they hold, and each then holds the two roots as evidence. One log is killed and started again, and still holds it, because a log writes what it holds to a file before it answers. The Book is now 1,291 pages.
