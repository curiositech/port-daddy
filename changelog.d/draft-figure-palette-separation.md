- **The figure palette is now measured for whether two story colours can be told apart, not only for whether each is readable on its ground.** The existing lockstep guard proves every ink clears contrast against the paper it prints on, which says nothing about one ink against another: two colours of equal luminance have a contrast ratio of 1.00 against each other and the old check called that fine. `website-v2/scripts/check-figure-palette-separation.mjs` measures all 21 story-colour pairs as OKLab Delta E, under normal vision and under simulated protanopia and deuteranopia (`website-v2/scripts/oklab-cvd.mjs`), gating at 15 for normal vision and 8/6 for CVD. Run against the palette as it stood, it failed two pairs — `pdcobalt`/`pdindigo` at Delta E 9.1 normal-vision, and `pdcobalt`/`pdviolet` collapsing to 4.8 under protanopia, which is the same colour twice for those readers. Both are re-stepped on their own hue in `tokens.semantic.css` and mirrored into both copies of `figures/pd-palette.tex`; four pairs whose ceiling sits below the floor are pinned as irreducible with their arithmetic rather than waved through by a lowered threshold. The step is wired into `library-checks.yml` as required rather than advisory, because it passes on the tree as it stands.
