- **The whitepaper build's render-clock pinning is now guarded by a test.** `scripts/build-whitepapers.sh` exports `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` and `FORCE_SOURCE_DATE=1` around every engine invocation, which is what makes a rebuild of unchanged source byte-identical and therefore what makes the SHA-256 manifest in `website-v2/public/whitepaper/publication-digests.json` satisfiable at all. Every neighbouring lever already had a guard — the stable `BUILD_DIR` that fixes the PDF trailer `/ID`, the digest-pinned `texlive/texlive` renderer, `paper_epoch`'s author-time derivation — but the clock pinning itself had none, so deleting it would have quietly turned `tests/purser/whitepaper-hashes.test.js` into a test that no commit could satisfy. Measured on TeX Live 2023: two `xelatex` runs of one source 1.2 s apart differ without these variables and are byte-identical with them, and two full builds of the Book through this script produced identical bytes (9,739,287 B, SHA-256 `91d50dc8…`).
