- Every figure in the Book now draws in one typographic voice, and in the document's own typeface. The shared figure style file states a single label size for every named text role — panel title, row label, axis numeral, direct label, note, identifier, verdict, kind tag, badge, legend entry, node text — and separates those roles by weight, slope, family and ink instead of by size. Half the corpus had been overriding the shared styles locally, which is why one figure could print its dark band in the document serif while every other label in it was set in sans, and why four figures asked for a sans face that clashed with the paragraph beside them. Thirty-eight figure fragments were converted; text that rendered below the seven-point legibility floor is gone from every figure reviewed, and the Book edition's data marks tell three shapes apart again instead of three colours of one square.
