# The Fourteen Principles (evidence base for the house style)

Load when justifying a style decision, teaching the style, or proposing template changes. Each principle: statement, source, one-line justification.

1. **Concrete before abstract; examples before definitions.** Sanderson/3Blue1Brown (SoME principles, 3blue1brown.com/about): "Concrete before abstract... typically backwards to how we think about things once we understand them." Novices generalize from populated examples, not the reverse.
2. **Say one thing, to someone.** Halmos, "How to Write Mathematics," L'Enseignement Mathématique 1970: have one main point; address one real reader. One governing idea is what survives.
3. **Rigor is a stage, not the start.** Tao, "There's more to mathematics than rigour and proofs" (blog): pre-rigorous → rigorous → post-rigorous. Meet readers pre-rigorous; carry them up.
4. **Analogies map relations, not attributes.** Gentner, "Structure-Mapping," Cognitive Science 7(2), 1983: relations between objects map; systematicity governs which. A structural analogy lets the reader derive new facts; a surface one misleads.
5. **Worked examples beat unguided problem-solving for newcomers.** Sweller & Cooper, Cognition & Instruction 2(1), 1985: ~half the time, ~1/5 the errors on transfer. Worked steps build the schema without search-thrashing.
6. **Fade guidance as expertise grows.** Kalyuga et al. 2003 (expertise-reversal); Renkl & Atkinson 2003 (fading). Novice scaffolding is expert load — hence Rail A and the Move-5 fade.
7. **Fight the curse of knowledge deliberately.** Camerer/Loewenstein/Weber, JPE 97(5), 1989; Pinker, The Sense of Style, 2014 ("the single best explanation... of why good people write bad prose"). You cannot feel your own jargon; engineer around it (misread-to-preempt, novice done-test).
8. **Make the medium do work.** Bret Victor, "Explorable Explanations" and "Ladder of Abstraction," 2011; Distill.pub (2017–2021). Diagrams externalize state readers would otherwise juggle — hence Rail B.
9. **Notation is designed, not defaulted.** Halmos 1970 ("resist symbols"); Knuth/Larrabee/Roberts, Mathematical Writing, 1989 (words between formulas; never open a sentence with a symbol). Bad notation is a per-line tax.
10. **Write for the perplexed.** Strogatz, "Writing about Math for the Perplexed and the Traumatized," Notices of the AMS 61(3), 2014: illuminate (the Aha), connect, treat the reader as a nonmathematical friend.
11. **One idea per visual; incremental reveal; one running example.** Practice of colah.github.io, Alammar's Illustrated Transformer (2018), Lil'Log. Keeps simultaneous novelty under working-memory limits.
12. **Spiral, rewrite, wastebasket.** Halmos's spiral plan; Tao's "use the wastebasket." The first pass is discovery, not the artifact.
13. **Cap the cost or the standard dies.** Distill's own guidance (100+ hours/article) and its 2021 hiatus editorial (volunteer burnout, structural friction). Hence: reuse one analogy repertoire and one figure grammar; ≤2h per figure.
14. **Honesty is a move.** Halmos, "Honesty is the best policy": the boundary of a claim is part of the claim. Hence Move 7 with its own figure.

Folklore check: the "Feynman technique" as a packaged four-step method is later popularization (Scott Young / Cal Newport lineage); the "explain it simply" tagline is apocryphally Einstein's. Use the peer-reviewed versions (5, 6) instead of the folklore when citing.

## Why v1 became v2 (critique record)
The original seven moves were ~80% aligned with the evidence. Three gaps closed:
- **No figure discipline** → Rail B (relation-map + regime diagram), the biggest lever per principles 8 and 11.
- **No notation rule** → point-of-use binding at the Move 3→4 seam (principle 9).
- **One path for all readers** → Rail A express lane + self-contained box (principle 6), plus Move-5 fading.
Two additions folded into existing moves rather than new ones: the misread-to-preempt (end of Move 3, from principles 7 and 14) and the numeric-claim provenance policy (from the external audit finding that readers cannot distinguish verified theorems from internal simulation counts).
Revisit triggers recorded at adoption: drop Rail A's prominence if readers ignore the express lane; simplify the figure grammar if production exceeds ~2h/figure; fold the misread line into Move 7 if it is empty for most results.
