# Chen versions, stratification, and deferred decisions

Chen’s DEXA 2007 paper, *Decomposing DAGs into Disjoint Chains*, is a distinct work from the ICEIS 2009 *Directed Acyclic Graphs and Disjoint Chains*. The 2009 primary PDF discusses a 2008 Chen-and-Chen algorithm that may fail to return a minimum set, then presents its own stratification, chain generation, virtual-node resolution, and combined alternating-graph procedure. Do not collapse these identities into “Chen stratification.”

A level partition can support a heuristic or the named source procedure, but adjacent-level matching alone is not a general proof of a global minimum reachability-chain partition. Treat application-level decision tokens, futures, or placeholders as constructed designs: preserve their input, unresolved state, resolution authority, and failure disposition.

Sources: [Chen 2007 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-74469-6_25), bibliographic/abstract access; [Chen 2009 PDF](https://www.scitepress.org/PublishedPapers/2009/18583/18583.pdf), searchable primary body, accessed 2026-09-24.
