# Causal evaluation under selection

Before comparing interventions, freeze eligibility and outcome definitions. Randomize within meaningful blocks; retain assignment records even when the option was not selected. Analyze intention-to-treat first, then uptake as a secondary mechanism. Use length-matched and resource-only controls when an intervention bundles prose, scripts, and context.

For adaptive attacks, a detector log cannot be the sole oracle. Use a separately durable hidden-truth ledger, hold out attack families, and report false acceptance conditional on a bad artifact, false alarm, and delay.
