# Review Questions

Use this when performing a skeptical product review.

## First-Run Questions

- What does the user see before they sign in?
- What value can they experience before connecting paid providers?
- What happens if they close the tab or terminal during setup?
- What is the empty state, and does it teach the next action?

## Account And Ownership

- How do users create an account?
- How do they sign in, recover access, delete data, and invite teammates?
- Who owns generated artifacts, transcripts, credentials, and billing?
- What happens when a team member leaves?

## Provider And Model Access

- Does the product assume Claude Max, OpenAI Pro, a local model, or MCP setup?
- Is there bring-your-own-key, routed-provider, demo, mock, or local fallback?
- How are missing credentials explained without sending the user to terminal instructions?

## Trust And Support

- What permissions does the product request, and why?
- What can the agent do without approval?
- Where are receipts, transcripts, diffs, costs, and rollback controls?
- How does a user report a failed run with enough context for support?

## Business Reality

- How is pricing or cost exposure explained?
- What is the support path?
- What telemetry is collected, and how does the user know?
- What is the launch incident plan?
