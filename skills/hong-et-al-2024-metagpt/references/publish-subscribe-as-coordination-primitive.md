# Shared pool, subscriptions, and prerequisites

The paper describes a shared message pool with role subscriptions and dependencies (arXiv:2308.00352v7, §3.2). Treat it as a logical coordination model, not a durable broker, typed protocol, replay service, ordering guarantee, or measured scaling result.

**Producer-to-consumer procedure.** A PM produces and publishes a PRD. The Architect is the named subscriber: it retrieves the PRD, checks its own prerequisite, consumes the context, performs design work, and publishes a design. The Project Manager consumes that design and publishes a task assigned to an Engineer. The Engineer must check for both its assigned task **and** the required design; a QA report, an unrelated message, or only one prerequisite does not wake that consumer. After implementation, QA becomes the named consumer and publishes the observed fixture result for the Engineer to consume on repair.

Test unmet prerequisite, unrelated message, missing recipient, and consumer identity separately. In v0.8.1, `Environment.publish_message` routes recipient addresses and an unmatched recipient logs while publication returns; caller-visible acceptance must be added locally if required.
