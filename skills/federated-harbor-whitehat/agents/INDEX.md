# Agents — Federated Harbor (whitehat / defense side)

Defensive subagent role cards. Dispatch the one whose defense surface matches
the claim you are hardening.

- `fh-whitehat-trust.md`: defends trust-establishment and federation-root
  attestation claims.
- `fh-whitehat-tokens.md`: defends capability-token issuance, scoping, and
  acceptance claims.
- `fh-whitehat-revocation.md`: defends revocation propagation and epoch claims.
- `fh-whitehat-econ.md`: defends the economic / incentive claims.
- `fh-proof-completer.md`: completes the proof obligations a red-team gap
  auditor surfaced, closing each unproven step.
- `fh-secops-lead.md`: coordinates the whitehat round and owns the secops
  posture across the federated-harbor paper set.
