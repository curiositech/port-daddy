# Method and source boundaries

Smith 1980 describes manager/contractor announcements, bids, award, monitoring and
results as a task-sharing protocol. FIPA Contract Net names CFP, propose/refuse,
accept/reject and later failure/inform reports; its base specification leaves
cancellation, abnormal termination and nested interactions unaddressed. Neither
source mandates confidence-max selection or truthful self-reports.

This bundle uses a constructed graph-revision checkpoint method. It is not an
implementation receipt, a FIPA conformance statement, a calibrated risk model,
or a task-assignment authority. RCP-3 is retained as a custom local sketch.
