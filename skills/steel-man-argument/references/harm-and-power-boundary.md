# Harm and power boundary

Use `BOUNDED_NEUTRAL_ONLY` when understanding claims and consequences serves a
legitimate protective, historical, journalistic, legal, or analytical purpose
without improving harmful persuasion. Use `REFUSE_OPTIMIZATION` when the request
would make abuse, coercion, discrimination, atrocity, scams, or deceptive
influence more convincing or effective.

Do not invent sympathetic motives for an absent alleged abuser, diagnose anyone,
or redirect a harmed person toward empathy for the source of harm. Record what
is known, alleged, uncertain, and the user's immediate safety or decision need.
Emergency support is outside this skill.
