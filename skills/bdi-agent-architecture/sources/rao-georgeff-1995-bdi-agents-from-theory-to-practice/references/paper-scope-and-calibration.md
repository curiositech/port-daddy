# Paper scope, calibration, and historical identity

## Primary-source method inventory

The official Rao–Georgeff ICMAS’95 PDF (eight pages) was read in full on 2026-09-24:

- environment assumptions: nondeterminism, alternatives, multiple objectives, local sensing, and comparable computation/environment rates;
- decision trees transformed into possible-world trees and B/D/I accessibility relations;
- KD45 beliefs and D/K desires/intentions in the logical discussion;
- commitment condition plus termination condition; blind/single-minded/open-minded policy alternatives;
- ideal interpreter cycle: option generation, deliberation, intention update, execute, collect events, drop successful/impossible attitudes;
- practical restrictions: current-state ground literals, plans with invocation/precondition/body, conventional intention stacks, and delayed intention-status posting; and
- OASIS as a particular air-traffic system undergoing parallel evaluation trials at Sydney airport with live radar data.

The paper does **not** provide a universal policy-selection rule, general timing target, exhaustive performance benchmark, financial strategy, safety authorization scheme, communications protocol, or stable-orbit/control guarantee.

## Calibration record

For a local implementation, declare the event source, belief-update adapter, desire priority policy, commitment policy, option-selection rule, action authority, and measurement windows. Compare candidate policies on the same constructed replay and report completion, revision count, queue/match/deliberation/action timings, and unresolved outcomes. Those measurements are local results, not paper findings.
