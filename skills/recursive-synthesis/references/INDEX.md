# Recursive synthesis references

This skill is a first-party workflow for multi-perspective document drafting and review. Its phases and templates are configurable local process guidance. It is not defined by the similarly named terminal-task paper.

| Reference | Use when | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| [process-design.md](process-design.md) | Choosing, adapting, or simplifying the local document workflow | Design rationale, review practices, failure signals, and evidence limits; not empirical proof that a phase or technique always works |
| [phase-templates.md](phase-templates.md) | Reusing prompts, claim ledgers, and phase handoffs | Long-form template library; counts, roles, model labels, dates, verdicts, and outputs are editable examples, not requirements |
| [source-algorithm-and-verification.md](source-algorithm-and-verification.md) | Comparing with Li et al.'s terminal-task data-generation method | Paper identity, pinned version, algorithm summary, author-reported results, and transfer limits; not the definition of this document-synthesis skill |

## Related paper

Li et al., “Recursive Synthesis for Long-Horizon Terminal Tasks,” arXiv:2608.05466 v1 (2026-08-05), [pinned full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.05466v1). The reported method grows terminal-task examples from verified seeds through solution extension, instruction/verifier realignment, and fresh sandbox validation. That task-data method is related by name only; it is not the source of the document workflow above. See the dedicated method reference for the reviewed sections and limitations.
