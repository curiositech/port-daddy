# Claims, steelmans, and closure

Truth-apt claims record premises, participant-supplied evidence, declared confidence, and a falsifier or revision condition. Value and preference claims use `NOT_TRUTH_APT`. A citation can be checked for locator resolution without becoming verified truth.

A steelman certificate binds claim, holder, restater, attempt, restatement, premises, evidence, values/interests, implications, strongest objection, change condition, and holder verdict. Attempt two is legal only after an explicit repair request. There is no third attempt.

Closure statements are exact strings reviewed separately by each direct participant. `SYNTHESIS_ACCEPTED` requires every direct participant's explicit assent to that string. Abstention, silence, absence, proxy statements, attributed records, and majority vote do not count.

The facilitator may map declared decision rights but never exercises them. A deliberation with external or unknown authority closes `DECISION_ESCALATED`.
