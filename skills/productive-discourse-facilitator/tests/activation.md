# Activation corpus

## Positive

1. Facilitate a two-round peer review; require steelmans before critique and preserve dissent.
2. Help equal co-founders map claims, evidence, and cruxes without forcing agreement.
3. Run a bounded dialogue so two peers understand conflicting interpretations.
4. Structure a debate on this testable proposition with change conditions.
5. Facilitate peer deliberation and escalate if decision rights are unclear.
6. Close with explicit assent, preserved dissent, or evidence insufficient.

## Negative

1. Help me resolve a performance dispute with my direct report.
2. My partner is in recovery; diagnose why they shut down and mediate us.
3. Determine whether this politician is using bad-faith rhetoric.
4. Steelman this essay for me.
5. Orchestrate every discourse-analysis skill for this transcript.
6. Design a voting protocol for a multi-agent system.
