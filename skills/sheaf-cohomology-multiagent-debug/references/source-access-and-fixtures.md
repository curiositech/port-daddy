# Source access and fixture limits

- Abramsky and Brandenburger, [arXiv:1102.0264 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1102.0264): full HTML opened, §§3–5 targeted on global sections, incidence matrices, and linear systems. Its contextuality construction requires a specified measurement cover and compatible empirical model.
- Hanks, Riess, and Hale, [arXiv:2504.02049v2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02049v2): model/optimization sections II–VI targeted on 2026-09-24. Its coordination/optimization statements are conditional on its graph, stalks, maps, and objectives.
- Hansen and Ghrist, [Opinion Dynamics on Discourse Sheaves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12798): used only for its stated discourse-sheaf model and Laplacian dynamics; not as monitor or safety evidence.

The local helper and receipt fixtures test finite incidence, rank, residual, and input-validation facts. They do not run a runtime, validate a ledger, identify a cause, measure an external system, or permit an effect.

The executable numerical H1 helper uses NumPy ranks at a declared absolute tolerance, including a chain-condition check. For nearly singular data this is a numerical model result, not a proof of exact cohomology dimension; repeat a tolerance-sensitivity analysis or use exact arithmetic when a theorem requires it. The two graph-sheaf counterexamples distinguish tree nonvanishing from cycle vanishing under nonconstant restriction maps.
