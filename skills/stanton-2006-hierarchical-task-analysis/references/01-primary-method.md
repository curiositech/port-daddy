## Source-supported method units

- §§1–2: HTA’s historical control-theory framing of human behavior; Miller’s (1960) test–operate–test–exit unit; nested feedback hierarchy; and goal-directed performance.
- §2: goal in real terms; subgoals that contribute to it; inclusion separated from the plan.
- §3/Figure 5: purpose/boundary; evidence gathering; overall goal/subgoals/plan; adequacy review; further-redescription decision; terminal notation; revision.
- §3/Tables 8–9: fixed, contingent, parallel, free-order, cyclic, and selection plan relationships.
- §3: P×C is a rough aid; quantification difficulty and analysis purpose limit its use.
- Figure 6 (p.65) and Figure 7 (p.66) Plan 0: wait 1, then 2, then 3; hazard → 4 then 5 → exit; otherwise exit. Figure 7 Plan 2 permits 2.1 at any time if appropriate, separately from the ordered 2.2 then 2.3 chain. Table 10 (p.67) is separately abridged: wait 1 then 2; hazard → 3 then 4 → exit; otherwise exit. It is not harmonized with the figures.
- Table 5: SGT terms and process-control development scope.
- §§4–5/Table 15: HTA can seed later analysis; SHERPA has its own task classes, error analysis, ordinal ratings, recovery, and remedy work.

The paper does not supply a fixed stopping depth, a universal 80/20 rule, a calibrated error-variance equation, an incident operating procedure, or proof of an automation decision.
