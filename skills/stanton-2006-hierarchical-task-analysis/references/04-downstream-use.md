# Downstream handoff contract

A downstream analyst receives the declared purpose/boundary; goal and plan table; evidence provenance; terminal reasons; unresolved disagreements; and revision version. The specialist then chooses and validates its own method.

| Candidate follow-on | HTA provides | HTA does not provide |
|---|---|---|
| SHERPA | bottom-level steps and their plan context | error modes, credible-error judgement, ratings, or remedies |
| Interface/training work | goals, plan conditions, evidence questions | interface requirements or a training design |
| Function allocation | candidate subgoals and context | human/machine assignment or an authority decision |
| Workload/team analysis | scope and relevant subgoals | duration, workload, coordination, or performance measures |

Do not mark any downstream outcome as complete merely because a hierarchy has been dispatched, listed, or terminal-marked.
