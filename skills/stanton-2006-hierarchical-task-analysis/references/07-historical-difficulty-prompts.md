# Historical difficulty prompts and a separate three-column form

As discussed by Stanton (2006) §2, the earlier Annett et al. HTA tabular approach includes two useful difficulty prompts:

| Prompt | Ask |
|---|---|
| **I/F** (input/feedback) | Is the necessary information perceptible, and can feedback reveal success or failure? |
| **A** (action) | Is the required action practicable and distinguishable? |

These two prompts are not a three-class error method and are not SHERPA’s task classification. They can surface a question for redesign or further analysis; they do not establish an error rate, remedy, or certified design.

Stanton also reports a distinct Patrick (1986) **three-column training form** (p.60). Its existence means a blanket statement that historical HTA has no three-column formats would be false. The correction is narrower: do not label I/F and A as three error classes or conflate either historical form with SHERPA.

**Source.** Annett et al. (1971) and Patrick (1986), as reported/discussed by Stanton (2006) §2. Their primary bodies were not separately accessed for this repair.
