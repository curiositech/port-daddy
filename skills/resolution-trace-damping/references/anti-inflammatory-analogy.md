# Biological analogy: metaphor only

“Anti-inflammatory” and “computational autoimmune” are metaphors for a hypothesized feedback pattern: repeated work increases attraction to an already visited location, while a completion signal attenuates that attraction. They are not claims of biological equivalence, medical mechanism transfer, measured prevalence, or proven mitigation.

Do not transfer immune-system concentrations, cell ratios, suppression factors, or clinical thresholds into `d`, resolution intensity, or timeout settings. No source in this bundle validates such a mapping. Use the task-set evaluation in `reopen-and-decay-policy.md` to measure duplicate revisits and missed reopened work.
