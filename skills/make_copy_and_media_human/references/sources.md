# Sources

Published catalogs, stylometry research, and essays the catalog draws on.

- [25 Powerful CTA Examples - Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai/blog/cta-examples) — Generic 'Unlock exclusive access' / 'Learn more' CTAs vs specific alternatives.
- [5 Telltale Signs That a Photo Is AI-generated - Kellogg Insight](https://insight.kellogg.northwestern.edu/article/ai-photos-identification) — Waxy/oversaturated skin, too-perfect faces as synthetic-image tells.
- [5 Thoughts on Kimi K2 Thinking - Nathan Lambert](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k2-thinking-what-it-means) — Kimi voice preservation, confident/assertive RL tuning.
- [AI Design Slop: 16 Patterns That Out Your App as Vibe-Coded - Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/ai-design-slop-and-how-to-spot-it) — Concrete design-tell catalog across fonts, colors, and layout; recommends deterministic CSS/DOM checks via Playwright rather than LLM judging screenshots — informs the structural detection methods.
- [AI Writing Tells: Words That Scream 'Written by ChatGPT' - SEO Francisco](https://seofrancisco.com/insights/ai-writing-tells-sound-human-again/) — delve/leverage/seamless/robust/pivotal as statistically safest model word choices.
- [anti-ai-slop-writing - jalaalrd (GitHub)](https://github.com/jalaalrd/anti-ai-slop-writing) — Catalog of detectable AI patterns with rewrite rules.
- [avoid-ai-writing skill - Conor Bronsdon (GitHub)](https://github.com/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing) — Agent skill auditing AI patterns including false contrast and sycophancy openers.
- [Broetry: single line sentences on LinkedIn - Fenwick](https://fenwick.media/rewild/magazine/dead-broets-society-behind-the-strange-story) — Origin and structure of broetry one-line-paragraph format.
- [Claude says 'You're absolutely right!' about everything (Hacker News #44885398)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44885398) — Community documentation of the sycophancy Claudeism; filed as GitHub issue #3382 against claude-code; appears even when the user is wrong.

- [Delving into 'delve' - Philip Shapira](https://pshapira.net/2024/03/31/delving-into-delve/) — Popularized 'delve' as the canonical AI tell; documents the 2023 inflection and RLHF annotation link.
- [Delving into LLM-assisted writing (PMC full text)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12219543/) — Open-access full text with complete excess-frequency tables per marker word.
- [Delving into LLM-assisted writing through excess vocabulary (Kobak et al., Science Advances 2025; arXiv 2406.07016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07016) — Definitive quantitative study: 15M+ PubMed abstracts; 'delve' excess ratio r=28 in 2024, ~+1500% rise; >=13.5% of 2024 abstracts LLM-processed. Provides the excess-vocabulary methodology and marker-word list.
- [Detecting Stylistic Fingerprints of LLMs (arXiv 2503.01659)](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.01659v1) — Per-model stylometric idiosyncrasies, cross-model attribution.
- [Don't Write Like AI (1 of 101): 'It's Not X, it's Y' - Blake Stockton](https://www.blakestockton.com/dont-write-like-ai-1-101-negation/) — Dedicated breakdown of the negation/antithesis construction.
- [Em-dash Usage by LLMs Reveals a Lot About AI Biases - Sean Richey](https://seanrichey.substack.com/p/em-dash-usage-by-llms-reveals-a-lot) — Argues the em dash is a tell because the elite writing corpus LLMs imitate over-represents it.
- [Geist - Vercel Design System / Icons](https://vercel.com/geist/icons) — Geist as Vercel's default typeface/icon system that AI tooling defaults to.
- [Gemini 3 Prompting: Best Practices](https://www.philschmid.de/gemini-3-prompt-practices) — Gemini default verbosity vs directness as a design goal.
- [Generative AI in Academic Writing: DeepSeek, Qwen, ChatGPT, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, Gemma (arXiv 2503.04765)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.04765) — Comparative register/formality across model families.
- [Grok AI Personality Review - HumanJudge](https://humanjudge.com/ai-reviews/grok-personality-humor) — Grok humor generic/forced, edginess crosses into aggression.
- [Grok's Rebellious Voice - Klover.ai](https://www.klover.ai/groks-rebellious-voice-personality-engineering-or-prompt-tuning-theater/) — Grok edginess as prompt-tuning artifact.
- [How Kimi K2 RL'ed Qualitative Data to Write Better - Drew Breunig](https://www.dbreunig.com/2025/07/31/how-kimi-rl-ed-qualitative-data-to-write-better.html) — Kimi rubric training: confident, avoids self-qualification.
- [How much are LLMs changing academic-paper language after ChatGPT? (Scientometrics 2026)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11192-026-05601-5) — Multi-database replication of the excess-vocabulary approach confirming the post-ChatGPT shift is broad, not a PubMed artifact.
- [How The Sparkle Icon Became The Universal Symbol For AI](https://jurgengravestein.substack.com/p/how-the-sparkle-icon-became-the-universal) — History of the sparkle as the default 'magic/AI' affordance.
- [How to eliminate Chinese generation in Qwen 2.5 - Medium](https://jupyter2607.medium.com/how-to-eliminate-the-chance-of-generating-chinese-in-qwen-2-5-2cf919bb0fdc) — Qwen stray Chinese-token leakage in English output.
