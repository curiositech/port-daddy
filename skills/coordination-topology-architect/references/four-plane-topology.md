# Four planes for coordination design

Record planning dependencies, communication routes, authority decisions, and
the actual execution substrate separately. For each edge, state whether it is
advisory, enforced, or merely observed. A chosen topology is a hypothesis about
task structure until it improves a matched single-agent baseline under the same
quality and spend budget.

Kim et al. evaluate many agent-system configurations and report that performance
depends materially on task and architecture; their results do not license a
universal scaling threshold: [Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems,
v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08296v3).
