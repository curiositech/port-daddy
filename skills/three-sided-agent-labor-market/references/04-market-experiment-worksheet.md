# Market experiments and reputation evidence

Use this worksheet to turn a market claim into a falsifiable question. It is a constructed research design; no listed local experiment has run.

## What the inspected studies actually add

In [AI-Work v2, §§3.1–3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.04988v2), exposing previous winning bids increased underbidding and reduced training relative to sealed bidding. Performance-linked pay increased training and market utility relative to flat fees. Higher concurrent job capacity concentrated work; more task types reduced concentration in the studied setup. These are simulation findings with particular scoring, learning, matching and reputation rules. They do not establish that real worker self-improvement necessarily destroys surplus, or prescribe a cap on reputation returns.

[Resnick et al.](https://rzeckhauser.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum4441/files/rzeckhauser/files/reputation_ebay.pdf) varied seller identity while selling matched postcard lots. That design is more informative about reputation's price effect than simply comparing unrelated sellers. Its reported premium must not be used as an agent-asset valuation multiplier.

[Tadelis's review](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/stadelis/Annual_Review_Tadelis.pdf) distinguishes hidden information from hidden actions and discusses feedback bias. Record transactions without feedback as missing observations, not automatic successes. A positive-rating fraction and a success rate over all attempts have different denominators.

## Constructed intervention matrix

| Question | Intervention | Hold fixed and record | Outcomes and failure interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does revealing bids change investment? | Previous winning prices visible versus concealed | task arrivals, worker population, initial state, budget, observation permissions | bids, completed quality, training cost/time, entry/exit; cheaper bids alone do not prove better welfare |
| Does quality-linked pay improve delivered value? | Flat fee versus a specified quality rule | total funding, evaluator revision, task mix, dispute/appeal process | quality, net reward, evaluator gaming, unpaid disputes; distinguish evaluator score from actual quality |
| Does capacity amplify incumbency? | Concurrent job limit varied | arrival stream, matching rule, skill/reputation initialization | concentration, queue delay, unfilled jobs, newcomer outcomes; high share may also reflect higher useful capacity |
| Does a reputation record convey transferable information? | History disclosure varied within an authorised evaluation | identity binding, task class, fresh independent outcome test | calibration, price/selection response, false-positive acceptance; no cross-domain transfer presumed |

Choose the independent unit before sampling. Repeated rounds within one simulated market are dependent; report separate seeded markets rather than treating every bid as an independent replicate. For real experiments, participation and disclosure require an approved protocol. Freeze one primary outcome and analysis before looking at results; retain all failed and interrupted runs. Report distributions, uncertainty and sensitivity to alternate matching/evaluator rules, not only the best seed.

## Three accounting questions the market graph cannot answer

1. **Who funds the hold?** For the 11.5-credit example, identify separate funders for bounty, bond, lease and license. Do not debit a worker for a requester's obligation just because both appear in one total.
2. **What does the claim cover?** For a delivery receipt, name subject/revision, acceptance criteria, evaluator and decision authority. A receipt can establish an observation without authorising payment.
3. **When is reversal effective?** A superseding decision before release changes the eligible release. After release, recovery needs a separately authorised transfer or recorded loss; history cannot mint recovered funds.

## Record the conclusion

Write: population/task domain; intervention and comparator; source or run identifiers; measured outcomes with denominators; unresolved confounders; accounting failures; and the narrow conclusion justified. A proposed design becomes empirical evidence only after those runs and checks exist. No source here certifies a three-sided market, truthful bidding, or incentive compatibility of the local settlement proposal.
