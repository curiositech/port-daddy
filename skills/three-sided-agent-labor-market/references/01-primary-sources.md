## Root primary-source additions

- Rochet–Tirole, *Two-Sided Markets: A Progress Report*: [author working paper dated November 29, 2005](https://idei.fr/sites/default/files/medias/doc/wp/2005/2sided_markets.pdf), §§2–3.1 read; published in RAND 37(3), 645–667 (2006). The fixed-total-price counterfactual belongs to its specified interaction model, not an automatic count of local firms or roles.
- Resnick, Zeckhauser, Swanson & Lockwood (2006), [author-hosted full paper](https://rzeckhauser.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum4441/files/rzeckhauser/files/reputation_ebay.pdf): abstract and experimental-design discussion accessed. The established versus new seller comparison used matched postcard lots and estimated an 8.1% willingness-to-pay difference. It establishes neither an agent-market premium nor a third side.
- Tadelis (2016), [author manuscript](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/stadelis/Annual_Review_Tadelis.pdf): introduction, evidence discussion and feedback-bias sections accessed. Reputation evidence needs context: hidden quality and hidden actions differ, and missing feedback or selection can distort apparent quality. This is a literature synthesis, not a new experiment in agent markets.
- Chiu et al. v2: root independently read §§3.1–3.3 and the institutional-scope discussion. The added worksheet below separates these reported simulation interventions from proposed local evaluations.

These additions were inspected on 2026-09-24. No economic or runtime experiment was executed by this skill-authoring campaign.
