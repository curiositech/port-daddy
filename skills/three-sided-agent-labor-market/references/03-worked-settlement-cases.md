# Constructed settlement cases

All numbers below are first-party examples. They check arithmetic and state distinctions only.

## Success

Held: bounty 8 + bond 2 + lease 1 + license 0.5 = 11.5. Fee: 3% × 8 = 0.24. Proposed transfers: operator 7.76, bond return 2, lease 1, license 0.5, fee 0.24. Total: 11.5.

**Positive check:** 7.76 + 2 + 1 + 0.5 + 0.24 = 11.5.

## Partial completion

A delivered artifact satisfies only the first of two acceptance criteria. The record must say which criterion passed, who assessed it, and what partial-release rule has authority. There is no source-backed rule that awards 4 credits merely because “half” the criteria passed.

**Negative check:** rejecting an unexplained 4-credit payout preserves an unresolved hold rather than inventing proportionality.

## Failure, dispute, and reversal

A worker starts work and an evaluator later reports success against the wrong revision. This is not “failure before work”; effects may exist. Move to dispute with 11.5 held, preserve evidence hashes and decision D17, and let an authorised process issue D18 if it reverses D17.

**Negative check:** releasing 11.5 under D17 and then releasing again under D18 violates one-release accounting. A reversal must supersede the prior decision and show an idempotent release record.

## Collusion allegation

R alleges that O and W coordinated a false result. Record allegation, claimed evidence, conflict rules, independent reviewer, and appeal. No automatic slash follows from the allegation alone. The remaining loss bound is unknown until authority, evidence quality, and insolvency assumptions are specified.
