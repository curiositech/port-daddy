# First-party proposal boundary

The original bundle’s float-plan escrow, operator/asset/licensor roles, hosted-trust framing, continuity/reputation chain, metering, clawback, and reputation actions are retained as **first-party design proposals**. This authoring pass did not inspect a current contract, escrow state machine, or deployed ledger. Therefore none of these items is evidence of shipped behavior, available authority, enforced conservation, or measured market impact.

Before adopting a proposal, read the applicable first-party contract/state machine and record:

1. plan version, signer, funding source, and amendment authority;
2. accepted state transitions, including partial, failure, dispute, appeal, reversal, and unknown-effect;
3. evidence producer, reviewer/arbiter authority, and appeal procedure;
4. release idempotency and retained-hold behavior;
5. identity/reputation write scope and correction path; and
6. tests or readbacks that support a claim of enforcement.

“Hosted trust” may be a product positioning proposal. It is not an economics conclusion that payment rails are interchangeable or that a ledger/relay/reputation service is defensible.
