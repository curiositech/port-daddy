# Sources and revision boundary

[AWS versions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/latest/dg/concepts-state-machine-version.html) documents immutable product snapshots; [Temporal versioning](https://github.com/temporalio/documentation/blob/main/docs/develop/typescript/workflows/versioning.mdx) documents replay-compatible histories. CAS admission, unknown-effect reconciliation, and typed invalidation are local proposals.
