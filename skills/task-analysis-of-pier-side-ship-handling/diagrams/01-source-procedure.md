# Source procedure and bounded review

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[Scope the two simulator evolutions] --> B[Documents plus initial SME refine provisional GOMS]
  B --> C[Give scenario to each participant before interview]
  C --> D[Uninterrupted account using scenario aid]
  D --> E[Probe phases and perceptual cues]
  E --> F[Format account as GOMS and map cue inventory]
  F --> G[Later appointment: compare account and initial model]
  G --> H{Discrepancy requires model change?}
  H -->|yes| I[Revise model and cue links; retain reason]
  H -->|no| J[Retain model with documented limits]
  I --> K[Bounded simulator-analysis handoff]
  J --> K
```

Source abstraction of Grassi IV.A–C, printed pp. 43–50, and Chapters V–VI. IV.C.3 calls its process three phases but enumerates four study steps. Generic CDM in II.F.1 has a separate three-pass account. The diagram preserves the later comparison appointment and does not imply that a complete cue inventory predated the interviews. It is analysis, not authorization for an operational effect.
