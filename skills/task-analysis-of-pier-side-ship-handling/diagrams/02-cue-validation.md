# Cue-to-goal validation card

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[CCI cue] --> B[Link cue to GOMS goal or selection point]
  B --> C[Record observable form and what the cue supports]
  C --> D{Representation and source link adequate?}
  D -- no --> E[Mark missing or ambiguous; collect review evidence]
  E --> A
  D -- yes --> F[Specify simulator display, audio, or interaction]
  F --> G[Keep context and scenario limitation]
  G --> H[Do not infer reliability, independence, or outcome proof]
```

For example, Grassi's engine-order execution cue rows in Chapter V.C, Table 6 (printed p. 106) concern checking execution. They do not prove later vessel response or provide a quantified sensor-fusion rule. Appendix A is “Standard Commands,” not the CCI appendix.
