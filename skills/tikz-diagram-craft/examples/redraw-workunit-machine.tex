\caption{The work-unit machine: six phases down the spine from proposed to a
settlement record, contested leaving the path at witnessed, and the four
append-only write targets on the right. Blue is the kernel's own state, teal
the records it may append to, gold the settlement, red the contested
off-ramp. Five transitions are guarded; the legend gives each guard's
predicate, the crime a checker constructs once that guard is removed, and the
length of the shortest violating trace. With every guard on, all 536 reachable
states of the two-principal instance satisfy the six invariants [internal,
\texttt{c0\_workunit.py}].}
\label{fig:swk-workunit-machine}
