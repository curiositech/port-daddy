FULL BLEED: the etching fills the image edge to edge to edge to edge. No plate mark, no border
rule, no surrounding sheet, no blank margin on any side.

Reference image 1 is the STYLE TEMPLATE for craft only: match its seventeenth-century copper-plate
etching technique, its single dark brown-black ink on aged laid paper, its dense burin
cross-hatching and fine stipple, its baroque frontispiece character.

COMPOSITION, after the frontispiece of Hobbes's Leviathan, in four registers stacked down the page:

TOP REGISTER: a wide ornamental banner spanning the full width, its interior a completely blank,
smooth, empty panel. Nothing is written in it.

UPPER REGISTER, the largest: a colossal allegorical figure rising from the sea, filling the centre,
its torso and arms built entirely from hundreds of tiny identical clerks seated at desks over
ledgers, in strict receding rows. Its head is a calm machine-like helm of concentric rings. In its
right hand it holds a surveyor's staff upright; in its left, an unrolled scroll whose surface is
blank. Behind it a lighthouse on a rock and a working harbour of stone quays. Flanking the figure,
one tall stone pilaster at the far left and one at the far right, each carrying a narrow vertical
blank panel down its shaft -- smooth and empty, no writing.

LOWER REGISTER: two symmetrical columns of small framed vignettes, five stacked down the left edge
and five down the right edge, each in its own thin ruled compartment, each a separate little
engraved still life in the same ink: a single open ledger with one pen laid across it; a lighthouse
seen close; an anchor with a coil of chain; a heavy strongroom door with one slot; a pair of
balance scales; a mariner's compass rose; a bonded chest with a broken seal; a fleet of small
boats seen from above; a surveyor's chain and rod; a bridge of one span between two shores.

BASE REGISTER: a low plinth running the full width, its face a blank empty panel.

Absolutely no lettering, numerals, words, letterforms, inscriptions, signatures or symbols that
resemble writing anywhere in the image. Every banner, panel, scroll, pilaster and plinth face is
left smooth and completely empty; the lettering is typeset afterwards.
