A soft faded watercolour painting on cold-pressed cotton paper, in a pale, atmospheric,
dreamlike register: washed greys, dusty lavender, pale slate blue, cool white paper
showing through, with one small warm amber glow as the only warm note. Loose wet-in-wet
washes, visible paper grain, soft bleeding edges, generous empty paper. No ink outlines,
no engraving, no cross-hatching, no frame, no border.

Standing waist-deep at the edge of a still pale sea, filling the upper two thirds of the
composition and dissolving into mist at its shoulders, is an enormous unearthly humanoid
figure -- a benign colossus, energetic and luminous rather than menacing. Seen closely,
its body is not flesh and not one machine but a dense swarm of THOUSANDS OF SMALL
ROBOTS: tiny mechanical figures, little walking automata and drifting drones packed and
swirling together into the shape of a person, their edges soft and granular where the
wash breaks up, so the silhouette reads as a crowd holding a human form. Faint warm
pinpricks of light glimmer through the swarm.

One arm reaches DOWN AND OUT toward the viewer in an open, beckoning gesture -- palm
upturned, inviting, not commanding.

The other arm holds two things aloft, crossed:
a SWORD whose blade is a tight bundle of ethernet patch cables, dozens of cords gathered
and braided into a long tapering blade with their small connectors glinting along its
edges; and a tall shepherd's CROOK made of spliced fibre-optic strands, translucent glass
filaments twisted into a staff that curls at the top, every filament ending in a soft
point of amber light so the whole crook glows faintly through the mist.

Below, the water is flat and pale, with a small wooden sailing boat far off to one side
for scale, tiny against the figure. A low pale headland and a slender lighthouse sit at
the far horizon. Enormous soft sky above, mostly empty paper.

Do not render any lettering, words, numerals or typography anywhere. Avoid photographic
realism, 3D rendering, CGI, gloss, neon, anime, heavy saturated colour, hard black
outlines, and any engraved or etched linework.
