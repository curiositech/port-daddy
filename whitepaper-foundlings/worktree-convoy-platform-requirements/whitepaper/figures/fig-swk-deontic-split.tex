\caption{Three normative kinds require three different mechanisms. A prohibition
is either regimented before effect or detected after commit and compensated. An
obligation is a durable but violable interval whose deadline is daemon-derived and
whose successful closure requires typed evidence. Permission is a recorded
normative status distinct from raw ability, so an actor can remain technically able
while forbidden. The traces cannot be collapsed into one generic policy box
without erasing the distinctions the kernel must preserve.}
