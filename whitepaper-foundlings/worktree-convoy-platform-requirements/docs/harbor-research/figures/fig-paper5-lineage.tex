% Worked lineage DAG for Theorem 1 (paper5 review C1). Two columns, the same
% fork-plus-re-derivation shape under the two semantics, with every node's
% LIVE spendable balance printed on it and a running Phi under each column.
%
% Every number below is the output of the Ledger class in
% skills/harbor-results/scripts/a6_no_mint.py, replayed op by op --
% derive(parents,weights,gamma) gives the child gamma*w*spend[p] and, unless
% MUT['copy_full'], debits the source w*spend[p], so repeated derivations
% compound on WHAT REMAINS (the script's own comment).
%
% LEFT, transfer semantics, gamma=0.9, three splits at w=1/3 then one
% re-derivation at w=1:
%   witness e            spend(e)=1.000                       Phi=1.000
%   derive a1 (w=1/3)    a1=0.9*(1/3)*1.000    =0.300  e->0.667  Phi=0.967
%   derive a2 (w=1/3)    a2=0.9*(1/3)*0.667    =0.200  e->0.444  Phi=0.944
%   derive a3 (w=1/3)    a3=0.9*(1/3)*0.444    =0.133  e->0.296  Phi=0.930
%   derive a4 from a1 (w=1)  a4=0.9*1*0.300    =0.270  a1->0.000 Phi=0.900
%   Phi <= W = 1.000 at every step, and nonincreasing under derivation.
%
% RIGHT, copy-full mutant (children inherit undebited), gamma=0.9, w=1 --
% these are the script's two exhibits verbatim:
%   2-op shortest crime:  1 + 0.9 + 0.9 = 2.800 > W = 1.000
%   8-way copy-fork:      1 + 8*0.9     = 8.200 = 8.2x W   (the 8.2x figure
%   quoted in the abstract, the Theorem 1 box, the relation-map and Sec. 7,
%   and until now never drawn).

\caption{The same lineage DAG under both semantics, with each node's live spendable balance on it and the running total $\Phi$
beneath. Left, transfer semantics: a three-way fork at $w=\tfrac13$ and one re-derivation cycle $a_1\to a_4$ at $w=1$, all at
$\gamma=0.9$. Each derivation debits its source, so $\Phi$ falls monotonically from $1.000$ to $0.900$ and never reaches the
witnessed total $W=1.000$ --- the supermartingale of Theorem 1, readable by adding up one column. The re-derivation edge is the
theorem's least intuitive clause and the figure settles it: $a_4$ is paid out of $a_1$'s remaining $0.300$, which is why a cycle
mints nothing. Right, the copy-full mutant on the same shape: nothing is debited, so two forks already put $\Phi=2.800$ behind one
witnessed unit and eight put $8.200$ --- the $8.2\times$ quorum multiplier, drawn. All balances replayed op by op from the ledger
in \texttt{a6\_no\_mint.py} [internal, \texttt{a6\_no\_mint.py}, seed 20260816].}
