% infeasible whenever G_max > L -- neither is drawn here, and the caption
% below says so rather than asserting unqualified uniqueness.

\caption{Regime diagram for Theorem 4, clause (ii) --- the \emph{uncapped} corner. Honest burden of a deterrence-tight schedule
holding its gap mass at a single period $t$, with $g_t\ge0$ and no per-period ceiling (log scale). Every step later multiplies the
honest tax by $\delta_h/\delta_f=1.58$ while buying zero extra deterrence, so among these single-spike schedules the cliff at $t=0$
is the unique minimum. Under the per-period cap $g_t\le L$ of clause (iii) the spike at $t=0$ is infeasible once $G_{\max}>L$ and
the unique optimum widens into a bang-bang fill of the first $k$ periods --- a case this plot does not draw, and which
\texttt{b6\_probation.py} does not yet sweep, since the script carries no cap parameter [verified for the uncapped case;
