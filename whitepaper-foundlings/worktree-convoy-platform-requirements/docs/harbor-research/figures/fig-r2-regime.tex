\caption{Regime diagram for the split penalty. Panel (a): the bits a ``two summaries $=$ two $\times$ one summary'' budget is short, at $N{=}60,m{=}8$ --- $0.80$ bits at the reference $k{=}2$, $5.72$ bits by $k{=}4$. Panel (b): the same penalty as a ratio over double. The finding is not a band: $\mathrm{Floor}(2k)/(2\,\mathrm{Floor}(k))$ exceeds $1$ at every regime and grows without bound in $k$ --- $1.067$ at the reference regime, $1.224$ at $k{=}4$ ($m{=}8$), $1.392$ by $k{=}10$ ($m{=}20$) [verified]. Budgeting ``two summaries'' by doubling one summary's budget under-provisions, and under-provisions worse the more each reader must catch.}
