\caption{\textbf{Static escrow and competitive underwriting occupy different
points on a reserve-adequacy frontier.} Static escrow immobilizes the full
per-transaction bond. Adequately reserved underwriting can reduce capital tied to
one transaction by pooling the tail, but the insurer must still carry that tail in
its reserve or reinsurance. The amber expected-loss-only point is cheaper because
it violates the required coverage. Coordinates are schematic; the structural
comparison is the claim.}
